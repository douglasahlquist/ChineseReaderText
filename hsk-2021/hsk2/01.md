## 语法：怎么样 | Yǔ fǎ : zěn me yàng | Grammar: 怎么样: how is …? how about …?

> After a noun, **怎么样** asks what something is like: 这个菜怎么样？ “How's this dish?” After a suggestion it means “how about it?”: 我们一起去，怎么样？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天 天气 怎么样？ | Jīn tiān tiān qì zěn me yàng ? | What's the weather like today? |
| 我们 一起 去， 怎么样？ | Wǒ men yì qǐ qù , zěn me yàng ? | How about we go together? |

## 语法：挺 | Yǔ fǎ : tǐng | Grammar: 挺……的: quite, pretty

> **挺** + adjective, usually with 的 at the end, is a friendly spoken “quite” or “pretty”: 挺好吃的 “pretty tasty”, 挺冷的 “quite cold”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 菜 挺 好吃 的。 | Zhè gè cài tǐng hǎo chī de . | This dish is pretty tasty. |

## 语法：好久 | Yǔ fǎ : hǎo jiǔ | Grammar: 好久: for a long time

> **好久** is “a long time”. 好久不见！ is “long time no see!”. To say something hasn't happened for a long time: 好久没有 + verb + 了.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 好久 不 见！ | Hǎo jiǔ bú jiàn ! | Long time no see! |
| 好久 没有 下雨 了。 | Hǎo jiǔ méi yǒu xià yǔ le . | It hasn't rained for a long time. |

## 语法：一路平安 | Yǔ fǎ : yí lù píng ān | Grammar: Good wishes

> Chinese has set phrases for good wishes: **一路平安** and **一路顺风** for a journey, **晚安** at bedtime, **生日快乐** and **新年快乐**, **加油** to encourage someone, and **干杯** for a toast.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一路平安！ | Yí lù píng ān ! | Have a safe trip! |
| 加油！ | Jiā yóu ! | You can do it! |
