## 语法：咱们 | Yǔ fǎ : zán men | Grammar: 咱们 and 我们

> **咱们** “we” always includes the listener: 咱们走吧 “let's go (you and me)”. **我们** may or may not include them. 咱 is a northern short form.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 咱们 走 吧！ | Zán men zǒu ba ! | Let's go! |

## 语法：自己 | Yǔ fǎ : zì jǐ | Grammar: 自己: oneself

> **自己** follows a person to mean “myself, yourself”: 我自己做 “I'll do it myself”. It also stands alone as an object: 照顾好自己.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 自己 做。 | Wǒ zì jǐ zuò . | I'll do it myself. |

## 语法：其中 | Yǔ fǎ : qí zhōng | Grammar: 其中: among them

> **其中** “among them” follows a group to pick out part of it: 有二十个人，其中五个是留学生.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 其中 一 个 是 我 的 朋友。 | Qí zhōng yí gè shì wǒ de péng you . | One of them is my friend. |

## 语法：有人 | Yǔ fǎ : yǒu rén | Grammar: 有人: someone

> **有人** “there is someone” works as “someone”: 有人找你. 这儿有人吗？ asks if a seat is taken.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 有人 找 你。 | Yǒu rén zhǎo nǐ . | Someone's looking for you. |
