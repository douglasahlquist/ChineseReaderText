## 语法：越来越 | Yǔ fǎ : yuè lái yuè | Grammar: 越来越 + adjective

> **越来越** “more and more”: 天越来越冷了. With two verbs, **越 A 越 B** is “the more A, the more B”: 越快越好.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 的 汉语 越来越 好 了！ | Nǐ de Hàn yǔ yuè lái yuè hǎo le ! | Your Chinese is getting better and better! |
| 越 快 越 好。 | Yuè kuài yuè hǎo . | The faster the better. |

## 语法：不如 | Yǔ fǎ : bù rú | Grammar: A 不如 B: A isn't as good as B

> **A 不如 B** says A falls short of B: 我的汉语不如你. **更** “even more” goes before the adjective: 这个更好.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 的 汉语 不如 你。 | Wǒ de Hàn yǔ bù rú nǐ . | My Chinese isn't as good as yours. |

## 语法：有点儿 | Yǔ fǎ : yǒu diǎn r | Grammar: 有点儿 + adjective: a bit (too)

> **有点儿** / **有一点儿** before an adjective usually signals something unwelcome: 有点儿累, 有点儿贵. Compare 一点儿 after: 便宜一点儿 “a bit cheaper”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 有点儿 累。 | Wǒ yǒu diǎn r lèi . | I'm a bit tired. |

## 语法：多么 | Yǔ fǎ : duō me | Grammar: 多么 …！: how …!

> **多么** + adjective makes an exclamation: 多么漂亮！ “How beautiful!”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 大自然 多么 漂亮！ | Dà zì rán duō me piào liang ! | How beautiful nature is! |
