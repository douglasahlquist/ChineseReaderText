## 语法：多久 | Yǔ fǎ : duō jiǔ | Grammar: 多久: how long

> **多久** asks for a length of time: 要多久？ “how long will it take?”, 你学了多久汉语？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 要 多久？ | Yào duō jiǔ ? | How long will it take? |

## 语法：有空儿 | Yǔ fǎ : yǒu kòng r | Grammar: 有空儿: be free

> **有空儿** “have free time”: 你有空儿吗？ 有空儿来我家玩儿 “come over when you're free”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 有空儿 吗？ | Nǐ yǒu kòng r ma ? | Are you free? |

## 语法：等到 | Yǔ fǎ : děng dào | Grammar: 等到 …: by the time …

> **等到** + time or event is “wait until, by the time”: 等到春天，我们去旅行.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 等到 明天 吧。 | Děng dào míng tiān ba . | Wait till tomorrow. |

## 语法：快点儿 | Yǔ fǎ : kuài diǎn r | Grammar: 快点儿 and 慢点儿

> **快点儿** “a bit faster” hurries someone up; 慢点儿 tells them to slow down.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 快点儿 走！ | Kuài diǎn r zǒu ! | Walk faster! |
