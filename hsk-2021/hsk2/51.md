## 语法：最近 | Yǔ fǎ : zuì jìn | Grammar: 最近: lately

> **最近** is “recently, lately”: 最近怎么样？ “How have you been?” Before a noun it is “nearest”: 最近的地铁站.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 最近 怎么样？ | Zuì jìn zěn me yàng ? | How have you been? |

## 语法：啊 | Yǔ fǎ : a | Grammar: 啊: a friendly particle

> **啊** at the end of a sentence softens or brightens it: 好啊！ “Sure!”, 是啊 “yes indeed”, 谁啊？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 好 啊！ | Hǎo a ! | Sure! |

## 语法：不要 | Yǔ fǎ : bú yào | Grammar: 不要, 不行, 不太

> **不要** + verb is “don't”: 不要哭. **不行** is “no good, won't do”. **不太** softens: 不太好 “not very good”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 不要 哭。 | Bú yào kū . | Don't cry. |
| 这样 不行。 | Zhè yàng bù xíng . | This won't do. |

## 语法：省 | Yǔ fǎ : shěng | Grammar: 省: province; save

> **省** is a province: 你是哪个省的？ As a verb it is “save”: 省钱, 省时间.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 坐 地铁 省 时间。 | Zuò dì tiě shěng shí jiān . | Taking the subway saves time. |
