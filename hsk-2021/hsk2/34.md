## 语法：量词 | Yǔ fǎ : liàng cí | Grammar: Measure words

> Chinese counts most nouns through a measure word that suits their shape or kind: **条** long things (fish, roads, rivers), **件** clothes and matters, **辆** vehicles, **座** mountains and buildings, **位** people (politely), **瓶** bottles, **篇** articles, **道** questions and dishes, **句** sentences.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一 条 鱼。 | Yì tiáo yú . | A fish. |
| 一 辆 车。 | Yí liàng chē . | A car. |
| 一 道 题。 | Yí dào tí . | A question. |

## 语法：遍 | Yǔ fǎ : biàn | Grammar: 遍: times through

> **遍** counts doing something all the way through: 再说一遍 “say it again”, 看了三遍 “watched it three times”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 再 说 一 遍。 | Zài shuō yí biàn . | Say it once more. |

## 语法：位 | Yǔ fǎ : wèi | Grammar: 位: polite measure for people

> **位** is the polite measure word for people: 这位是王老师. Restaurants ask 几位？ “How many?”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 几 位？ | Jǐ wèi ? | How many in your party? |

## 语法：段 | Yǔ fǎ : duàn | Grammar: 段 and 节

> **段** is a stretch of time, road or text: 一段时间. **节** is a class period: 一节课.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一 段 时间。 | Yí duàn shí jiān . | A period of time. |
| 一 节 课。 | Yì jié kè . | A class. |
