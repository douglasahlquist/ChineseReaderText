## 语法：字词 | Yǔ fǎ : zì cí | Grammar: 字, 词, 词语 and 句子

> **字** is one character. **词** is a word, which may be one character or several (学习 is one 词 of two 字). **词语** means words and phrases in general, and **句子** is a sentence.

> A **字典** lists characters; a **词典** lists words.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 词 有 两 个 字。 | Zhè gè cí yǒu liǎng gè zì . | This word has two characters. |

## 语法：课堂上 | Yǔ fǎ : kè táng shàng | Grammar: 课堂上, 黑板上: in class, on the board

> As in HSK 1, 上 after a noun can mean “on” or “in”: **黑板上** “on the board”, **课堂上** “in class”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 课堂 上 别 说话。 | Kè táng shàng bié shuō huà . | Don't talk in class. |
| 老师 在 黑板 上 写 字。 | Lǎo shī zài hēi bǎn shàng xiě zì . | The teacher is writing on the board. |

## 语法：复习 | Yǔ fǎ : fù xí | Grammar: 练习 and 复习

> **练习** is to practice, or an exercise: 做练习 “do an exercise”. **复习** is to review what you've already learned.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
