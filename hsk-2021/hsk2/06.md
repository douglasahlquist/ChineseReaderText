## 语法：周 | Yǔ fǎ : zhōu | Grammar: 周 and 星期; 上周 and 下周

> **周** means “week”, like 星期, and is a little more formal: 一周, 这周. **周末** is the weekend. **上周** and **下周** are last week and next week, like 上星期 and 下星期.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 下周 见！ | Xià zhōu jiàn ! | See you next week! |
| 周末 你 做 什么？ | Zhōu mò nǐ zuò shén me ? | What do you do at the weekend? |

## 语法：以前 | Yǔ fǎ : yǐ qián | Grammar: 以前 and 以后

> On their own, **以前** is “before, in the past” and **以后** is “after, in the future”. After an event or a time they mean “before …” and “after …”: 吃饭以前 “before eating”, 下课以后 “after class”, 三天以后 “in three days”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 吃饭 以前 要 洗 手。 | Chī fàn yǐ qián yào xǐ shǒu . | Wash your hands before eating. |
| 下课 以后 我 去 图书馆。 | Xià kè yǐ hòu wǒ qù tú shū guǎn . | After class I go to the library. |

## 语法：后来 | Yǔ fǎ : hòu lái | Grammar: 后来 or 以后?

> **后来** “afterwards” is only for the past, when telling what happened next: 后来他去了北京. **以后** can also look to the future: 以后我想去中国.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 后来 他 去 了 北京。 | Hòu lái tā qù le Běi jīng . | Later he went to Beijing. |
| 以后 我 想 去 中国。 | Yǐ hòu wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōng guó . | In the future I want to go to China. |

## 语法：那时 | Yǔ fǎ : nà shí | Grammar: Then and now: 当时, 那时(候), 那会儿, 这时(候)

> **当时**, **那时**, **那时候** and the spoken **那会儿** all mean “at that time” in the past. **这时** and **这时候** mean “at this moment” or, in a story, “just then”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 当时 我 不 知道。 | Dāng shí wǒ bù zhī dào . | I didn't know at the time. |
| 这时 他 来 了。 | Zhè shí tā lái le . | Just then he arrived. |
