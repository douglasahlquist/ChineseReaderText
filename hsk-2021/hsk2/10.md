## 语法：中餐 | Yǔ fǎ : zhōng cān | Grammar: 中餐, 西餐 and 快餐

> 餐 means “meal”: **中餐** is Chinese food, **西餐** Western food, and **快餐** “fast meal” is fast food. Compare 早餐, 午餐 and 晚餐 from lesson 4.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 中餐 还是 西餐？ | Zhōng cān hái shi xī cān ? | Chinese food or Western? |

## 语法：吃饱 | Yǔ fǎ : chī bǎo | Grammar: Verb + result: 吃饱

> **饱** after 吃 says what eating achieved: **吃饱了** “I've eaten my fill”. The negative uses 没: 我还没吃饱 “I'm still hungry”. Compare 听懂 and 看懂 in lesson 3.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 吃 饱 了 吗？ | Nǐ chī bǎo le ma ? | Have you had enough to eat? |
| 我 还 没 吃 饱。 | Wǒ hái méi chī bǎo . | I'm still hungry. |

## 语法：碗 | Yǔ fǎ : wǎn | Grammar: 碗 as a measure word

> **碗** “bowl” counts what is in a bowl, like 杯 for cups: 一碗米饭 “a bowl of rice”, 两碗面条儿. 再来一碗！ is “another bowl, please!”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一 碗 米饭。 | Yì wǎn mǐ fàn . | A bowl of rice. |
| 再 来 一 碗！ | Zài lái yì wǎn ! | Another bowl, please! |

## 语法：饭馆 | Yǔ fǎ : fàn guǎn | Grammar: 饭馆 and 饭店

> **饭馆** is an everyday restaurant. **饭店** (HSK 1) is also a restaurant, but can mean a big hotel.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 去 饭馆 吃饭 吧。 | Wǒ men qù fàn guǎn chī fàn ba . | Let's eat at a restaurant. |
