## 语法：春夏秋冬 | Yǔ fǎ : chūn xià qiū dōng | Grammar: The four seasons

> **春天, 夏天, 秋天, 冬天** are spring, summer, autumn and winter, often said together as 春夏秋冬. Like other time words they go before the verb: 夏天我们放假.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 春天 来 了！ | Chūn tiān lái le ! | Spring is here! |
| 夏天 我们 放假。 | Xià tiān wǒ men fàng jià . | We have holidays in summer. |

## 语法：天气 | Yǔ fǎ : tiān qì | Grammar: Weather words: 晴, 阴, 多云, 下雪

> **晴** is clear and sunny, **阴** overcast, **多云** cloudy. Add 了 for a change: 天晴了 “it's cleared up”, 天阴了 “it's clouded over”. **下雪** is to snow, like 下雨.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 天 晴 了。 | Tiān qíng le . | The sky has cleared. |
| 下雪 了！ | Xià xuě le ! | It's snowing! |

## 语法：凉 | Yǔ fǎ : liáng | Grammar: 凉 and 凉快

> **凉** is “cool, cold”, of things and food as well as weather: 茶凉了 “the tea has gone cold”. **凉快** is always pleasant: “nicely cool” weather or places.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 茶 凉 了。 | Chá liáng le . | The tea has gone cold. |
| 今天 很 凉快。 | Jīn tiān hěn liáng kuai . | It's nice and cool today. |

## 语法：多了 | Yǔ fǎ : duō le | Grammar: Adjective + 多了: much more

> After an adjective, **多了** means “much more so” than before: 凉快多了 “much cooler”, 好多了 “much better”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 下 了 雨， 凉快 多 了。 | Xià le yǔ , liáng kuai duō le . | After the rain it's much cooler. |
