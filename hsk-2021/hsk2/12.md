## 语法：红色 | Yǔ fǎ : hóng sè | Grammar: Colors: 红 and 红色

> A single color character works like an adjective: 天很蓝 “the sky is blue”, 脸红了 “blushed”. With **色** it is the name of the color: 我喜欢红色. Before a noun, use the 色 form with 的: 红色的衣服, or the single character directly: 红衣服.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 喜欢 红色。 | Wǒ xǐ huan hóng sè . | I like red. |
| 红色 的 车 是 谁 的？ | Hóng sè de chē shì shéi de ? | Whose is the red car? |

## 语法：的 | Yǔ fǎ : de | Grammar: Color + 的: “the red one”

> A color + **的** can stand for the thing: 我要白的 “I'll have the white one”, 他的车是黑色的 “his car is black”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 有 黑色 的 吗？ | Nǐ yǒu hēi sè de ma ? | Do you have a black one? |

## 语法：了 | Yǔ fǎ : le | Grammar: Changing color with 了

> **了** after a color says it has changed: 天黑了 “it's got dark”, 树绿了 “the trees have turned green”, 他的脸红了 “he blushed”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 天 黑 了。 | Tiān hēi le . | It's got dark. |
| 春天 树 绿 了。 | Chūn tiān shù lǜ le . | In spring the trees turn green. |

## 语法：漂亮 | Yǔ fǎ : piào liang | Grammar: 漂亮, 好看, 可爱 and 难看

> **漂亮** “beautiful, pretty” and **好看** “good-looking” are close; **可爱** is “cute, lovable”; **难看** “ugly” is the opposite of 好看.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 真 漂亮！ | Nǐ zhēn piào liang ! | You look beautiful! |
| 这 个 孩子 真 可爱！ | Zhè gè hái zi zhēn kě ài ! | What a cute child! |
