## 语法：层 | Yǔ fǎ : céng | Grammar: 层: floors

> Floors are counted with **层**: 我家在三层 “I live on the third floor”, 你住在几层？ In China the ground floor is 一层.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 住 在 几 层？ | Nǐ zhù zài jǐ céng ? | Which floor do you live on? |

## 语法：院 | Yǔ fǎ : yuàn | Grammar: 院: courtyards and institutions

> **院** is a walled courtyard (**院子**), and also an institution: 医院 “hospital”, 学院 “college”. Its head is the **院长**.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 孩子们 在 院子 里 玩儿。 | Hái zi men zài yuàn zi lǐ wán r . | The children are playing in the yard. |

## 语法：长 | Yǔ fǎ : cháng | Grammar: 长 zhǎng: the head of something

> Read **zhǎng**, 长 means “head, chief”: **市长** “mayor”, **院长** “dean”, 校长 “principal”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他 是 市长。 | Tā shì shì zhǎng . | He's the mayor. |

## 语法：园 | Yǔ fǎ : yuán | Grammar: 园, 馆 and 场: kinds of places

> **园** is a garden or park: 公园, 花园, 动物园. **馆** is a hall: 体育馆, 饭馆. **场** is an open ground: 体育场, 广场, 停车场.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 去 动物园 吧！ | Wǒ men qù dòng wù yuán ba ! | Let's go to the zoo! |
