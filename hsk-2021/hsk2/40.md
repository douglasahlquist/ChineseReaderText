## 语法：大声 | Yǔ fǎ : dà shēng | Grammar: 大声 and 小声

> **大声** “big voice” and **小声** “small voice” go before a verb: 大声读 “read aloud”, 小声说话 “talk quietly”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 请 大声 说。 | Qǐng dà shēng shuō . | Please speak up. |

## 语法：故意 | Yǔ fǎ : gù yì | Grammar: 不是故意的: didn't mean to

> **故意** is “on purpose”. 我不是故意的 is the standard apology for an accident.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 对不起， 我 不 是 故意 的。 | Duì bu qǐ , wǒ bú shì gù yì de . | Sorry, I didn't do it on purpose. |

## 语法：随便 | Yǔ fǎ : suí biàn | Grammar: 随便: as you like

> **随便** “follow convenience” means “whatever, as you like”: 随便坐 “sit anywhere”. As an answer it can sound off-hand.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 随便 坐。 | Suí biàn zuò . | Sit anywhere. |

## 语法：称 | Yǔ fǎ : chēng | Grammar: 称 + person + name

> **称** is to call someone by a title: 我们称他王老师. It also means “weigh”: 称一下儿.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 称 他 王 老师。 | Wǒ men chēng tā Wáng lǎo shī . | We call him Teacher Wang. |
