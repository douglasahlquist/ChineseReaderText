## 语法：够 | Yǔ fǎ : gòu | Grammar: 够 and 不够

> **够** is “enough”: 钱够吗？ 够了！ “That's enough!” The question form is 够不够？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 钱 够 吗？ | Qián gòu ma ? | Is there enough money? |
| 时间 不够 了。 | Shí jiān bú gòu le . | We're running out of time. |

## 语法：多长 | Yǔ fǎ : duō cháng | Grammar: 多 + adjective: how …?

> **多** before an adjective asks “how …?”: 多长？ “how long?”, 多高？ “how tall?”, 多远？ “how far?”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 条 路 有 多 长？ | Zhè tiáo lù yǒu duō cháng ? | How long is this road? |

## 语法：角 | Yǔ fǎ : jiǎo | Grammar: Money: 块, 角, 分

> Chinese money is counted in **块** (yuan), **角** (a tenth of a yuan) and 分. In speech 角 is often 毛: 一块五角 or 一块五 “1.50”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一 块 五 角。 | Yí kuài wǔ jiǎo . | One yuan fifty. |

## 语法：满 | Yǔ fǎ : mǎn | Grammar: Verb + 满: filled up

> **满** after a verb means “to capacity”: 住满了 “fully booked”, 坐满了 “all seats taken”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 酒店 住 满 了。 | Jiǔ diàn zhù mǎn le . | The hotel is fully booked. |
