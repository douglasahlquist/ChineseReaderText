## 语法：海 | Yǔ fǎ : hǎi | Grammar: 海, 河 and 湖

> **海** is the sea (**大海** “the open sea”), **河** a river and **湖** a lake. Things in them are 海里, 河里, 湖里.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 海 里 有 很 多 鱼。 | Hǎi lǐ yǒu hěn duō yú . | There are lots of fish in the sea. |

## 语法：开灯 | Yǔ fǎ : kāi dēng | Grammar: 开 and 关: on and off

> **开** turns things on or opens them; **关** turns them off or shuts them: 开灯 / 关灯, 开门 / 关门, 开机 / 关机.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 开 灯。 | Kāi dēng . | Turn on the light. |
| 关 灯。 | Guān dēng . | Turn off the light. |

## 语法：亮 | Yǔ fǎ : liàng | Grammar: 天亮了 and 天黑了

> **亮** “bright” and **黑** “dark” with 了 describe the day changing: 天亮了 “it's getting light”, 天黑了 “it's got dark”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 天 亮 了。 | Tiān liàng le . | It's getting light. |
| 天 还 没 亮。 | Tiān hái méi liàng . | It's not light yet. |

## 语法：边 | Yǔ fǎ : biān | Grammar: Place + 边: by the …

> **边** after a place means “by, alongside”: 海边 “seaside”, 河边 “riverside”, 湖边 “lakeside”, 路边 “roadside”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 家 在 河 边。 | Wǒ jiā zài hé biān . | My home is by the river. |
