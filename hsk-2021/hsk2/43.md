## 语法：习惯 | Yǔ fǎ : xí guàn | Grammar: 习惯: be used to

> **习惯** + verb or noun is “be used to”: 我习惯早起, 你习惯了吗？ It is also a noun: 好习惯.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 习惯 了 吗？ | Nǐ xí guàn le ma ? | Have you got used to it? |

## 语法：好像 | Yǔ fǎ : hǎo xiàng | Grammar: 好像: seem

> **好像** before a verb or adjective is “seem, look like”: 他好像病了, 好像要下雨了.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 好像 要 下雨 了。 | Hǎo xiàng yào xià yǔ le . | It looks like rain. |

## 语法：过去 | Yǔ fǎ : guò qù | Grammar: 过去 and 过来

> **过去** is “go over (away from me)” and also “the past”; **过来** is “come over (toward me)”: 你过来！

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 过来！ | Nǐ guò lái ! | Come here! |
| 过去 的 事。 | Guò qù de shì . | Things in the past. |

## 语法：常 | Yǔ fǎ : cháng | Grammar: 常见 and 常用

> **常** “often” + verb makes “common”: **常见** “commonly seen”, **常用** “commonly used”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 字 很 常用。 | Zhè gè zì hěn cháng yòng . | This character is used a lot. |
