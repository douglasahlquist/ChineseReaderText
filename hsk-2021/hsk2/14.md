## 语法：坐骑 | Yǔ fǎ : zuò qí | Grammar: 坐, 骑 and 开: getting around

> You **坐** (sit in) a subway, bus or taxi: 坐地铁, 坐公交车, 坐出租车. You **骑** (ride astride) a bicycle: 骑自行车, or simply 骑车. You **开** (drive) a car: 开车.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 坐 地铁 去 上班。 | Wǒ zuò dì tiě qù shàng bān . | I take the subway to work. |
| 我 骑车 去 学校。 | Wǒ qí chē qù xué xiào . | I ride my bike to school. |

## 语法：公交车 | Yǔ fǎ : gōng jiāo chē | Grammar: 公交车 and 公共汽车

> Both mean “bus”. **公交车** (public-transport vehicle) is what most people say today; **公共汽车** is the older, fuller word.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 公共汽车 和 公交车 一样。 | Gōng gòng qì chē hé gōng jiāo chē yí yàng . | 公共汽车 and 公交车 mean the same. |

## 语法：出租 | Yǔ fǎ : chū zū | Grammar: 出租 and 出租车

> **出租** is to rent out: 房子出租 “house for rent”. **出租车** “rental car” is a taxi.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 房子 出租。 | Zhè gè fáng zi chū zū . | This house is for rent. |
| 我们 坐 出租车 吧。 | Wǒ men zuò chū zū chē ba . | Let's take a taxi. |

## 语法：停车 | Yǔ fǎ : tíng chē | Grammar: 停车 and 停车场

> **停车** is to park (literally “stop the car”), and **停车场** “car-stopping ground” is a parking lot.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这儿 能 停车 吗？ | Zhè r néng tíng chē ma ? | Can I park here? |
