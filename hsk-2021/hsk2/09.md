## 语法：感觉 | Yǔ fǎ : gǎn jué | Grammar: 感觉 and 感到: feel

> **感觉** and **感到** both mean “to feel”: 我感觉很好, 我感到很高兴. 感觉 is also a noun, “a feeling”, and can mean “have the feeling that”: 我感觉他不高兴.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 感觉 怎么样？ | Nǐ gǎn jué zěn me yàng ? | How do you feel? |
| 我 感到 很 高兴。 | Wǒ gǎn dào hěn gāo xìng . | I feel very happy. |

## 语法：小心 | Yǔ fǎ : xiǎo xīn | Grammar: 小心: careful!

> **小心** + a thing is “watch out for”: 小心车！ Add 别 for what to avoid: 小心，别生病. On its own, 小心！ is “careful!”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 小心 车！ | Xiǎo xīn chē ! | Watch out for cars! |

## 语法：怕 | Yǔ fǎ : pà | Grammar: 怕 and 可怕

> **怕** is to be afraid of, or to dread: 我怕冷, 小明怕医生. **可怕** describes what is frightening: 那个电影很可怕.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 别 怕！ | Bié pà ! | Don't be afraid! |
| 那 个 电影 很 可怕。 | Nà gè diàn yǐng hěn kě pà . | That movie is scary. |

## 语法：住院 | Yǔ fǎ : zhù yuàn | Grammar: 住院 and 出院

> **住院** “live in the hospital” is to be admitted; **出院** “leave the hospital” is to be discharged. Like 上班 and 下班, they need no other object.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 爷爷 住院 了。 | Yé ye zhù yuàn le . | Grandpa's been admitted to hospital. |
| 爷爷 出院 了！ | Yé ye chū yuàn le ! | Grandpa's out of hospital! |
