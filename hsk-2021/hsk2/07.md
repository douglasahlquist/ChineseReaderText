## 语法：姓 | Yǔ fǎ : xìng | Grammar: 姓 and 名: names

> The surname comes first: 王大明 is surname 王, given name 大明. Say your surname with **姓** and your whole name with 叫: 我姓王，叫王大明. **您贵姓？** is the polite way to ask someone's surname.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 您 贵 姓？ | Nín guì xìng ? | May I ask your surname? |
| 我 姓 王， 名 大明。 | Wǒ xìng Wáng , míng Dà míng . | My surname is Wang and my given name is Daming. |

## 语法：是的 | Yǔ fǎ : shì de | Grammar: 是……的: when, where, how

> To ask or tell when, where or how something in the past happened, put **是** before that detail and **的** at the end: 我是在北京长大的 “it was in Beijing that I grew up”, 我是二零零九年出生的.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 是 在 哪儿 长大 的？ | Nǐ shì zài nǎ r zhǎng dà de ? | Where did you grow up? |
| 我 是 二 零 零 九 年 出生 的。 | Wǒ shì èr líng líng jiǔ nián chū shēng de . | I was born in 2009. |

## 语法：小时候 | Yǔ fǎ : xiǎo shí hou | Grammar: 小时候 and 从小

> **小时候** is “in childhood, when I was little”. **从小** is “since childhood”, for something that started then and still goes on: 我从小喜欢唱歌.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 小时候 住 在 北京。 | Wǒ xiǎo shí hou zhù zài Běi jīng . | I lived in Beijing as a child. |
| 我 从小 喜欢 唱歌。 | Wǒ cóng xiǎo xǐ huan chàng gē . | I've loved singing since I was little. |

## 语法：年轻 | Yǔ fǎ : nián qīng | Grammar: 年轻, 少年, 青年 and 大人

> **年轻** is the adjective “young”. **少年** is a teenager, **青年** a young adult, and **大人** a grown-up (as opposed to a child).

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 教会 有 很 多 年轻 人。 | Jiào huì yǒu hěn duō nián qīng rén . | There are lots of young people at church. |
