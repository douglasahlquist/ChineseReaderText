## 语法：心 | Yǔ fǎ : xīn | Grammar: 心: the heart

> **心** “heart” builds feeling words: **心情** “mood”, **心里** and **心中** “in one's heart”, **放心** “put one's heart down — don't worry”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天 我 心情 很 好。 | Jīn tiān wǒ xīn qíng hěn hǎo . | I'm in a good mood today. |

## 语法：对满意 | Yǔ fǎ : duì mǎn yì | Grammar: 对 … 满意 / 不满

> Being pleased or unhappy with something uses **对**: 老师对我的成绩很满意, 他对服务不满.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 老师 对 我 的 成绩 很 满意。 | Lǎo shī duì wǒ de chéng jì hěn mǎn yì . | The teacher is pleased with my grades. |

## 语法：难 | Yǔ fǎ : nán | Grammar: 难 + verb: unpleasant to …

> **难** before a sense verb means “unpleasant”: 难看 “ugly”, 难听 “awful to hear”, 难过 “sad”, 难受 “feel bad”. Its opposite is 好: 好看, 好听.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 歌 很 难听。 | Zhè gè gē hěn nán tīng . | This song is awful. |

## 语法：别 | Yǔ fǎ : bié | Grammar: 别 + verb + 了: stop doing

> **别 … 了** tells someone to stop: 别哭了 “stop crying”, 别气了 “don't be angry anymore”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 别 气 了。 | Bié qì le . | Don't be angry. |
