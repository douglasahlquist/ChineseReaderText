## 语法：意思 | Yǔ fǎ : yì si | Grammar: 什么意思？ and 有意思

> **……是什么意思？** asks what something means. **有意思** “having meaning” means “interesting”: 这本书很有意思.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 词 是 什么 意思？ | Zhè gè cí shì shén me yì si ? | What does this word mean? |
| 学 汉语 非常 有意思。 | Xué Hàn yǔ fēi cháng yǒu yì si . | Learning Chinese is really interesting. |

## 语法：懂 | Yǔ fǎ : dǒng | Grammar: 懂, 听懂 and 看懂

> **懂** is to understand a language or a meaning. After 听 or 看 it says you understood what you heard or read: **听懂了**, **看懂了**. The negative is 没听懂. **懂得** is a deeper “know, understand”: 懂得感谢别人 “know to thank others”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 听 懂 了。 | Wǒ tīng dǒng le . | I understood what I heard. |
| 你 看 懂 了 吗？ | Nǐ kàn dǒng le ma ? | Did you understand what you read? |

## 语法：讲 | Yǔ fǎ : jiǎng | Grammar: 讲 and 说

> **讲** overlaps with 说, but leans toward explaining, telling and speaking to a group: 讲课文 “explain the text”, 讲话 “give a talk”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 老师 在 讲 课文。 | Lǎo shī zài jiǎng kè wén . | The teacher is explaining the text. |

## 语法：说英语 | Yǔ fǎ : shuō yīng yǔ | Grammar: Talking about how someone speaks

> Say who, then the language, then how: 安娜说普通话很流利 “Anna speaks Mandarin fluently”, or more simply 她的汉语很流利.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 安娜 说 普通话 很 流利。 | Ān nà shuō pǔ tōng huà hěn liú lì . | Anna speaks Mandarin fluently. |
