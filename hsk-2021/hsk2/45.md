## 语法：问题 | Yǔ fǎ : wèn tí | Grammar: 问题: question or problem

> **问题** is both a question to ask and a problem to solve. 没问题！ is “no problem!”. A **难题** is a hard one.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 没 问题！ | Méi wèn tí ! | No problem! |

## 语法：原来 | Yǔ fǎ : yuán lái | Grammar: 原来: so it turns out

> **原来** shows sudden understanding: 原来是你！ “Oh, it's you!” It also means “originally”: 我原来住在南方.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 原来 是 你！ | Yuán lái shì nǐ ! | Oh, it's you! |

## 语法：影响 | Yǔ fǎ : yǐng xiǎng | Grammar: 对 … 有影响

> **影响** is “to affect” and “influence”: 下雨影响了交通, 他对我影响很大.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 下雨 影响 了 交通。 | Xià yǔ yǐng xiǎng le jiāo tōng . | The rain affected traffic. |

## 语法：当 | Yǔ fǎ : dāng | Grammar: 当 dāng: work as; when

> **当** + a role is “serve as, become”: 我想当老师. **当 … 的时候** is “when …”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 想 当 老师。 | Wǒ xiǎng dāng lǎo shī . | I want to be a teacher. |
