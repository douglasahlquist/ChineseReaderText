## 语法：开始 | Yǔ fǎ : kāi shǐ | Grammar: 开始 and 结束: beginning and end

> **开始** can stand alone (电影开始了) or take a verb after it: 开始下雨, 开始学习. **结束** is its opposite: 课结束了. Both often go with 以前, 以后 and 的时候.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 电影 开始 以前， 我们 吃 了 饭。 | Diàn yǐng kāi shǐ yǐ qián , wǒ men chī le fàn . | Before the movie started we ate. |
| 课 结束 以后， 我们 继续 说话。 | Kè jié shù yǐ hòu , wǒ men jì xù shuō huà . | After class ended we kept talking. |

## 语法：其实 | Yǔ fǎ : qí shí | Grammar: 其实: actually

> **其实** tells the real situation, usually after something that seems different: 看起来很难，其实不难. It goes at the start of a sentence or before the verb.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 大家 都 说 很 远， 其实 很 近。 | Dà jiā dōu shuō hěn yuǎn , qí shí hěn jìn . | Everyone says it's far, but actually it's close. |

## 语法：只是 | Yǔ fǎ : zhǐ shì | Grammar: 就是 and 只是

> **就是** points to exactly this one: 就是他 “it's him”. After a good point, **就是** or **只是** add a small “but”: 很好，就是太贵 / 只是有点儿贵. **只是** also means “merely”: 只是问问.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 房间 很 好， 就是 小 了 一点儿。 | Zhè gè fáng jiān hěn hǎo , jiù shì xiǎo le yì diǎn r . | This room is nice — just a little small. |
| 我 不 是 不 想 去， 只是 没 时间。 | Wǒ bú shì bù xiǎng qù , zhǐ shì méi shí jiān . | It's not that I don't want to go; I just don't have time. |
