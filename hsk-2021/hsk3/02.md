# 第二课：把和被 | Dì èr kè : bǎ hé bèi | HSK 3 · Lesson 2: Ba and bei

> **Draft** — not yet reviewed by a native speaker.

> **Goal:** Use 把 to say what you do to something, and 被 to say what happened to it; move, hang, push, press and tidy things.

## 语法：把 | Yǔ fǎ : bǎ | Grammar: 把: doing something to the object

> **把** brings the object before the verb when you do something to it with a result: 把门开开, 把书放到桌子上, 把茶喝完. The verb never stands alone after 把: it has a result (完, 好, 错), a place (在, 到) or 一 下.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 把 门 开开。 | Bǎ mén kāi kai . | Open the door. |
| 我 把 书 放到 书架 上 了。 | Wǒ bǎ shū fàng dào shū jià shàng le . | I put the book on the shelf. |

## 语法：被 | Yǔ fǎ : bèi | Grammar: 被: what happened to something

> **被** turns it round: the thing comes first, then 被 and who did it: 手机被他拿走了. The doer can be left out: 那本书被借走了. Like 把, the verb usually has a result.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 的 面包 被 吃 了！ | Wǒ de miàn bāo bèi chī le ! | My bread has been eaten! |
| 门 被 风 推开 了。 | Mén bèi fēng tuī kāi le . | The door was pushed open by the wind. |

## 语法：放到 | Yǔ fǎ : fàng dào | Grammar: 放到, 搬到: putting and moving to a place

> **到** after a verb tells where something ends up: 放到桌子上, 搬到北京. With 把 it says where you put it: 把书放到书架上.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 把 椅子 搬 到 外边 去。 | Bǎ yǐ zi bān dào wài bian qù . | Move the chair outside. |
