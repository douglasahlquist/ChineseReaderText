# HSK 3 介绍 | HSK sān jiè shào | About HSK 3

> **Draft** — the lessons are not yet reviewed by a native speaker.

## 目标 | Mù biāo | The goal

> HSK 3 is the level 3 of the HSK 3.0 standard (2021). It adds about 970 words to the 1,250 of HSK 1 and 2. This course teaches the **952 HSK 3 words** not already met in the earlier lessons, in **56 lessons** of about 17 new words each. Every sentence uses only words from the earlier levels and the HSK 3 words taught before it.

> By the end you can talk about work, school, news, sport, travel, health and feelings; link ideas with 因此, 由于, 不仅 and 除了; use 把 and 被; give opinions and reasons; and follow longer conversations at home, at church and in town.

## 每课 | Měi kè | How each lesson works

> Each lesson works like HSK 1 and 2: the goal and an always-visible word list first, then about eight sentences for each new word, grammar notes, and dialogs that use everything learned so far. Tap a word for its pinyin and sound; double-tap a line for its English.

> One HSK 3 word was already taught in HSK 1 and isn't repeated: 钟 (HSK 1, lesson 52).
