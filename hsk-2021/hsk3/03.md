## 语法：以来 | Yǔ fǎ : yǐ lái | Grammar: 以来, 自从, 至今: since and until now

> **以来** follows the starting point: 来中国以来 “since coming to China”. **自从** goes in front of it: 自从来了中国. They often come together: 自从…以来. **至今** means “up to now”: 至今还记得.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 自从 来 了 中国 以来， 我 一直 很 忙。 | Zì cóng lái le Zhōng guó yǐ lái , wǒ yì zhí hěn máng . | Ever since I came to China I've been busy. |

## 语法：直到 | Yǔ fǎ : zhí dào | Grammar: 直到…才: not until

> **直到** marks the end point: 等到十二点 / 直到十二点. With **才** it means “not until”: 直到今天我才知道 “I didn't know until today”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 直到 晚上 他 才 回来。 | Zhí dào wǎn shang tā cái huí lai . | He didn't come back until evening. |

## 语法：时 | Yǔ fǎ : shí | Grammar: 时 and 的时候

> **时** is a shorter, more written way to say **的时候**: 上课时 = 上课的时候. Both follow what is happening.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 吃饭 的 时候 = 吃饭 时 | Chī fàn de shí hou = chī fàn shí | At mealtime. |
