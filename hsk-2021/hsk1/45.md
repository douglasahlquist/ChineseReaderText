## 语法：一块儿 | Yǔ fǎ : yí kuài r | Grammar: 一块儿 and 一起: together

> **一块儿** means the same as 一起, “together”, and is more casual and northern: 我们一块儿去吧.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 一块儿 去 吧。 | Wǒ men yí kuài r qù ba . | Let's go together. |

## 语法：有的 | Yǔ fǎ : yǒu de | Grammar: 有的……有的……: some … others …

> **有的** “some” goes before a noun, often in pairs to contrast groups: 有的人喜欢打球，有的人喜欢唱歌. **有一些** “there are some” states that some exist.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 有的 学生 来 了， 有的 没有 来。 | Yǒu de xué sheng lái le , yǒu de méi yǒu lái . | Some students came, and some didn't. |

## 语法：干什么 | Yǔ fǎ : gàn shén me | Grammar: 干 and 干什么

> **干** is a casual word for “do”, like 做. **干什么？** “What are you doing?” or “What for?” is very common in speech.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 在 干什么？ | Nǐ zài gàn shén me ? | What are you doing? |

## 语法：那些 | Yǔ fǎ : nà xiē | Grammar: 那些: those

> **那些** is the plural of 那: 那些人 “those people”. No measure word is needed after it.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 那些 人 是 谁？ | Nà xiē rén shì shéi ? | Who are those people? |

## 语法：动 | Yǔ fǎ : dòng | Grammar: Verb + 不动: can't any more

> **动** is “move”. After a verb, **不动** means you no longer have the strength: 我跑不动了 “I can't run any more”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 跑 不 动 了。 | Wǒ pǎo bú dòng le . | I can't run any more. |
