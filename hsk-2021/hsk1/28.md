## 语法：东南西北 | Yǔ fǎ : dōng nán xī běi | Grammar: 东, 南, 西, 北

> The four directions are said in the order **东南西北** “east, south, west, north”. In combinations east or west comes first: 东南 “southeast”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 东 南 西 北。 | Dōng nán xī běi . | East, south, west, north. |

## 语法：边 | Yǔ fǎ : biān | Grammar: 边: side

> **边** “side” turns a direction into a place, just like 前边 and 左边: 东边, 西边, 上边, 下边. As before, the landmark comes first: 学校东边, 桌子上边.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 学校 东边 有 一 个 商场。 | Xué xiào dōng bian yǒu yí gè shāng chǎng . | There's a mall to the east of the school. |
| 你 的 书包 在 桌子 下边。 | Nǐ de shū bāo zài zhuō zi xià bian . | Your schoolbag is under the desk. |

## 语法：东西 | Yǔ fǎ : dōng xi | Grammar: 东西 is not 东 + 西

> Don't mix up **东西** (dōng xi) “thing” with 东 and 西 “east and west”. The second syllable of 东西 “thing” is toneless.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 买 了 一些 东西。 | Wǒ mǎi le yì xiē dōng xi . | I bought a few things. |

## 对话一 | Duì huà yī | Dialog 1 · Directions

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| Anna：请问， 电影院 在 哪儿？ | Qǐng wèn , diàn yǐng yuàn zài nǎ r ? | Excuse me, where's the cinema? |
| Man：在 东边， 商场 旁边。 | Zài dōng bian , shāng chǎng páng biān . | On the east side, next to the mall. |
