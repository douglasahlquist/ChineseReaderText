## 语法：打电话 | Yǔ fǎ : dǎ diàn huà | Grammar: 打电话 and 喂

> **打电话** is to make a phone call; to call someone is 跟 (or 和) someone 打电话. Answer the phone with **喂** (wéi), “hello?”, and ask for someone with 我找……

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 喂， 我 找 大明。 | Wéi , wǒ zhǎo Dà míng . | Hello, I'd like to speak to Daming. |

## 语法：一下儿 | Yǔ fǎ : yí xià r | Grammar: Verb + 一下儿: just for a moment

> **一下儿** after a verb means “a moment, briefly” and makes a request softer: 等一下儿 “wait a moment”, 看一下儿 “have a look”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 请 等 一下儿。 | Qǐng děng yí xià r . | Please wait a moment. |

## 语法：别 | Yǔ fǎ : bié | Grammar: 别: don't

> **别** + verb means “don't”, like 不要: 别说话. **别……了** means “stop doing”: 别看电视了 “stop watching TV”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 别 说话！ | Bié shuō huà ! | Don't talk! |
| 别 看 电视 了！ | Bié kàn diàn shì le ! | Stop watching TV! |

## 语法：就 | Yǔ fǎ : jiù | Grammar: 就: just, right, as early as

> **就** adds emphasis before a verb: 就是他 “that's him!”, 就在那儿 “right there”, 我就来 / 马上就来 “I'm coming right now”. With a time it means “as early as”: 他七点就来了.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 电影院 就 在 那儿。 | Diàn yǐng yuàn jiù zài nà r . | The cinema's right there. |
