## 语法：网上 | Yǔ fǎ : wǎng shàng | Grammar: 网上 and 上网

> **网上** “on the net” is a place: 在网上买东西 “shop online”. **上网** is a verb, “go online”: 别上网了！

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 在 网上 买 东西。 | Wǒ zài wǎng shàng mǎi dōng xi . | I shop online. |
| 我 晚上 上网。 | Wǒ wǎn shang shàng wǎng . | I go online in the evenings. |

## 语法：读书 | Yǔ fǎ : dú shū | Grammar: 读书: read, and study at a school

> **读书** is reading books, and also means studying at a school: 哥哥在大学读书 “my brother studies at university”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 哥哥 在 大学 读书。 | Gē ge zài dà xué dú shū . | My older brother studies at university. |

## 语法：饭店 | Yǔ fǎ : fàn diàn | Grammar: 饭店, 书店 and 图书馆

> 店 is a shop: **饭店** “restaurant” (big ones are also hotels), **书店** “bookshop”. A library is **图书馆**, a place to read, not to buy.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 去 饭店 吃饭。 | Wǒ men qù fàn diàn chī fàn . | Let's eat at a restaurant. |
