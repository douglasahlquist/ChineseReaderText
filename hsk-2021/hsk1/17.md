## 语法：老师 | Yǔ fǎ : lǎo shī | Grammar: Family name + 老师

> Students call a teacher by family name + **老师**: 李老师 “Ms. / Mr. Li”. A class greets the teacher with 老师好！ and the teacher answers 同学们好！

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 李 老师 好！ | Lǐ lǎo shī hǎo ! | Hello, Ms. Li! |
| 同学们 好！ | Tóng xué men hǎo ! | Hello, everyone! |

## 语法：大学 | Yǔ fǎ : dà xué | Grammar: Schools: 小学, 中学, 大学

> **小学** is primary school, **中学** middle and high school, **大学** university. Add 生 for the students: 小学生, 中学生, 大学生.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 小明 是 小学生。 | Xiǎo míng shì xiǎo xué shēng . | Xiaoming is a primary school pupil. |
| 安娜 是 大学生。 | Ān nà shì dà xué shēng . | Anna is a university student. |

## 语法：在 | Yǔ fǎ : zài | Grammar: 在 + school: be a student at

> 在 + a school says where someone studies: 小月在中学 “Xiaoyue is in middle school”, 我哥哥在大学 “my brother is at university”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 小月 在 中学。 | Xiǎo yuè zài zhōng xué . | Xiaoyue is in middle school. |
