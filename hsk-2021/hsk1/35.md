## 语法：给 | Yǔ fǎ : gěi | Grammar: 给: give, and to / for

> **给** + person + thing is “give someone something”: 妈妈给我十块钱. **给** + person before a verb means “to” or “for” them: 给奶奶打电话 “call Grandma”, 给小明买了一本书 “bought Xiaoming a book”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 是 给 你 的。 | Zhè shì gěi nǐ de . | This is for you. |
| 我 给 奶奶 打电话 了。 | Wǒ gěi nǎi nai dǎ diàn huà le . | I called Grandma. |

## 语法：送 | Yǔ fǎ : sòng | Grammar: 送: give a present, see off

> **送** is to give something as a present: 我想送奶奶一本书. It also means to see someone off or take them somewhere: 我送你去机场.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 送 你 去 机场。 | Wǒ sòng nǐ qù jī chǎng . | I'll take you to the airport. |

## 语法：年 | Yǔ fǎ : nián | Grammar: Years: 年, 今年, 明年, 去年

> Years are read digit by digit: 二零二六年 “2026”. 年 needs no measure word: 一年, 两年. This year, next year and last year are **今年**, **明年** and **去年**.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今年 是 二 零 二 六 年。 | Jīn nián shì èr líng èr liù nián . | This year is 2026. |

## 语法：生日 | Yǔ fǎ : shēng rì | Grammar: Birthdays and greetings

> Ask 你的生日是几月几号？ Greet with **生日快乐！** and **新年快乐！** (快乐 “happy” is an extra word here).

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 生日 快乐！ | Shēng rì kuài lè ! | Happy birthday! |
