## 语法：帮 | Yǔ fǎ : bāng | Grammar: 帮 and 帮忙

> **帮** takes the person you help, and often what you help them do: 我帮妈妈做饭. **帮忙** “help out” takes no object: 我来帮忙！

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 帮 妈妈 做 饭。 | Wǒ bāng mā ma zuò fàn . | I help Mom cook. |
| 我 来 帮忙！ | Wǒ lái bāng máng ! | I'll help! |

## 语法：一边 | Yǔ fǎ : yì biān | Grammar: 一边……一边……: doing two things at once

> **一边 A 一边 B** means doing A and B at the same time: 奶奶一边做饭一边唱歌 “Grandma sings while she cooks”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 一边 喝 茶 一边 说话。 | Wǒ men yì biān hē chá yì biān shuō huà . | We chat over tea. |

## 语法：记得 | Yǔ fǎ : jì de | Grammar: 记, 记住, 记得, 忘 and 忘记

> **记** is to note down or learn, **记住** to fix in memory, **记得** to remember (still have in memory). **忘** and **忘记** are “forget”, usually with 了: 我忘了.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 不 记得 了。 | Wǒ bú jì de le . | I don't remember. |
| 别 忘 了！ | Bié wàng le ! | Don't forget! |

## 语法：生气 | Yǔ fǎ : shēng qì | Grammar: 生气: angry

> **生气** is “to get angry”: 妈妈生气了. Angry with someone is 跟 someone 生气. 别生气！ is “don't be angry!”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 别 生气！ | Bié shēng qì ! | Don't be angry! |
