## 语法：来去 | Yǔ fǎ : lái qù | Grammar: Verb + 来 or 去: which way

> **来** after a verb means toward the speaker, **去** away from the speaker: 进来 “come in” (said from inside), 进去 “go in” (said from outside). The same goes for 出来 / 出去 and 回来 / 回去.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 快 进来！ | Kuài jìn lai ! | Come in, quick! |
| 你 进去 看 一下儿。 | Nǐ jìn qu kàn yí xià r . | Go in and have a look. |

## 语法：回到 | Yǔ fǎ : huí dào | Grammar: 回到 and 来到: arriving

> **回到** + place is “get back to”: 我回到家了. **来到** + place is “arrive at, come to”: 欢迎来到我们的教会！

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 回到 家 了。 | Wǒ huí dào jiā le . | I'm home. |
| 欢迎 来到 我们 的 教会！ | Huān yíng lái dào wǒ men de jiào huì ! | Welcome to our church! |

## 语法：吧 | Yǔ fǎ : ba | Grammar: 吧: let's, go ahead, I suppose

> **吧** at the end of a sentence softens it. It makes a suggestion (我们走吧 “let's go”), a gentle request (进来吧 “come on in”), or a guess you want confirmed (你是安娜吧？ “you must be Anna?”).

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 走 吧。 | Wǒ men zǒu ba . | Let's go. |
| 你 累 了 吧？ | Nǐ lèi le ba ? | You must be tired. |
