## 语法：吗 | Yǔ fǎ : ma | Grammar: Yes/no questions with 吗

> Put **吗** (ma) at the end of a statement to turn it into a yes/no question. The word order doesn't change: 你是大明。→ 你是大明吗？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 是 安娜 吗？ | Nǐ shì Ān nà ma ? | Are you Anna? |
| 你 好 吗？ | Nǐ hǎo ma ? | How are you? |

## 语法：很 | Yǔ fǎ : hěn | Grammar: Adjectives: 很好, not 是好

> Words like 好 work like verbs, so they don't take 是: say **我很好**, not 我是好. **很** (hěn) means “very”, but in 我很好 it is mostly there to make the sentence sound complete.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 很 好。 | Wǒ hěn hǎo . | I'm (very) well. |
| 你 的 名字 很 好。 | Nǐ de míng zi hěn hǎo . | Your name is very nice. |

## 语法：不 | Yǔ fǎ : bù | Grammar: Saying “not” with 不

> **不** (bù) goes right before the verb or adjective: 不是, 不叫, 不好.

> Before a 4th-tone syllable 不 is said *bú*: 不是 bú shì, 不叫 bú jiào. The pinyin in these lessons shows the change.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 不 是 大明。 | Wǒ bú shì Dà míng . | I'm not Daming. |
| 不 好。 | Bù hǎo . | Not good. |

## 语法：呢 | Yǔ fǎ : ne | Grammar: “And you?” with 呢

> **呢** (ne) after a word asks the same question back: 我很好，你呢？ “I'm well — and you?”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 叫 大明， 你 呢？ | Wǒ jiào Dà míng , nǐ ne ? | I'm Daming, and you? |
