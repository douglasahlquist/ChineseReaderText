## 语法：谁 | Yǔ fǎ : shéi | Grammar: Who? 谁 goes where the answer goes

> **谁** (shéi) means “who”. Like 什么, it takes the place of the answer: 他是谁？ → 他是大明。 谁的 means “whose”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 那 是 谁？ | Nà shì shéi ? | Who is that? |
| 这 是 谁 的？ | Zhè shì shéi de ? | Whose is this? |

## 语法：们 | Yǔ fǎ : men | Grammar: Plurals with 们

> **们** (men) makes pronouns plural: 我们 we, 你们 you all, 他们 / 她们 they. It is also added to words for people when talking to a group: 朋友们 “friends”. Most nouns don't change for plural at all.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你们 好！ | Nǐ men hǎo ! | Hello, everyone! |
| 朋友们， 再见！ | Péng you men , zài jiàn ! | Goodbye, friends! |

## 语法：都 | Yǔ fǎ : dōu | Grammar: 都: “all”, before the verb

> **都** (dōu) means “all / both” and always comes before the verb or adjective, never before the noun: 我们都很好 “we are all well”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他们 都 是 朋友。 | Tā men dōu shì péng you . | They're all friends. |
