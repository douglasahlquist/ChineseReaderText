## 语法：学 | Yǔ fǎ : xué | Grammar: 学 and 学习

> Both mean “learn, study”. **学** is the everyday word and takes what you learn: 学汉语. **学习** is a little more formal and can stand on its own: 我在学习 “I'm studying”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 学 汉语。 | Wǒ xué Hàn yǔ . | I'm learning Chinese. |
| 我 在 学习。 | Wǒ zài xué xí . | I'm studying. |

## 语法：在 | Yǔ fǎ : zài | Grammar: 在 + verb: in the middle of doing

> 在 before a verb says something is going on right now: 小月在学课文 “Xiaoyue is learning the text”. (Before a place, 在 means “at”.)

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 大明 在 学习。 | Dà míng zài xué xí . | Daming is studying. |

## 语法：汉语 | Yǔ fǎ : hàn yǔ | Grammar: 汉语, 中文, 汉字 and 字

> **汉语** is the Chinese language, especially as the subject you study. **中文** “Chinese” is the everyday word, used a lot for written Chinese. **汉字** are Chinese characters, and **字** is any single character.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 的 中文 很 好！ | Nǐ de Zhōng wén hěn hǎo ! | Your Chinese is very good! |
| 我 认识 一百 个 汉字。 | Wǒ rèn shi yì bǎi gè Hàn zì . | I know a hundred characters. |
