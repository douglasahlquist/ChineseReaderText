## 语法：你好 | Yǔ fǎ : nǐ hǎo | Grammar: Greeting with 你好

> **你好** (nǐ hǎo) is the everyday “hello”: literally “you good”. Answer with 你好 too. 你好吗？ (lesson 2) asks how someone is.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 好！ | Nǐ hǎo ! | Hello! |
| 大明， 你 好！ | Dà míng , nǐ hǎo ! | Hello, Daming! |

## 语法：是 | Yǔ fǎ : shì | Grammar: A 是 B: “A is B”

> **是** (shì) links two nouns or names: 我是大明 “I am Daming”. It doesn't change for I/you/he, and there is no “a/the”.

> 是 on its own also answers “yes, that's right”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 是 安娜。 | Wǒ shì Ān nà . | I am Anna. |
| 你 是 大明？ —— 是。 | Nǐ shì Dà míng ? Shì . | You're Daming? — Yes. |

## 语法：叫什么名字 | Yǔ fǎ : jiào shén me míng zi | Grammar: Names: 叫 and 你叫什么名字？

> **叫** (jiào) means “to be called”. Ask a name with 你叫什么名字？ — the question word 什么 sits exactly where the answer goes: 我叫大明.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 叫 什么 名字？ | Nǐ jiào shén me míng zi ? | What's your name? |
| 我 叫 大明。 | Wǒ jiào Dà míng . | My name is Daming. |

## 语法：的 | Yǔ fǎ : de | Grammar: 的 for “my”, “your”, “Daming's”

> **的** (de) after a person makes it possessive: 我的 “my / mine”, 你的 “your / yours”, 大明的 “Daming's”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 的 名字 | Wǒ de míng zi | my name |
| 是 安娜 的。 | Shì Ān nà de . | It's Anna's. |
