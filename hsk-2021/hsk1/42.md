## 语法：找到 | Yǔ fǎ : zhǎo dào | Grammar: Verb + result: 找到, 打开, 关上, 记住

> A second word after a verb can say what the action achieved: 找 “look for” → **找到** “find”, 记 → **记住** “memorize”. 打开 “open”, 关上 “close”. The negative uses 没有: 我没有找到.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 找到 我 的 钱包 了。 | Wǒ zhǎo dào wǒ de qián bāo le . | I found my wallet. |
| 我 没有 找到。 | Wǒ méi yǒu zhǎo dào . | I didn't find it. |

## 语法：放在 | Yǔ fǎ : fàng zài | Grammar: 放在 + place: put somewhere

> **放在** + place says where something is put: 放在桌子上 “put it on the table”, 放在书包里 “put it in the schoolbag”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 杯子 放 在 桌子 上。 | Bēi zi fàng zài zhuō zi shàng . | The cups go on the table. |

## 语法：开关 | Yǔ fǎ : kāi guān | Grammar: 开 and 关

> **开** is open or turn on, **关** is close or turn off: 开门, 关电视. 商店几点开门？ asks when a shop opens.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 商店 几 点 开 门？ | Shāng diàn jǐ diǎn kāi mén ? | What time does the shop open? |
