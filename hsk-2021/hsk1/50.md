## 语法：爸妈 | Yǔ fǎ : bà mā | Grammar: Short forms: 爸, 妈, 哥, 姐 …

> In everyday speech family members are often called by one syllable: **爸**, **妈**, **哥**, **姐**, and less often 弟 and 妹. **爸妈** is “Mom and Dad”. In front of a name, 哥 or 姐 is a friendly “big brother / big sister”: 小月姐.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 妈， 我 饿 了！ | Mā , wǒ è le ! | Mom, I'm hungry! |
| 安娜 姐 很 好。 | Ān nà jiě hěn hǎo . | Anna is really nice. |

## 语法：小孩儿 | Yǔ fǎ : xiǎo hái r | Grammar: 小孩儿, 孩子 and 小朋友

> **小孩儿** “kid” is casual and northern; 孩子 is the general word, also used for someone's own children; 小朋友 is how you speak to a child.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 小孩儿 都 爱 玩儿。 | Xiǎo hái r dōu ài wán r . | All kids love to play. |

## 语法：小姐 | Yǔ fǎ : xiǎo jie | Grammar: 小姐: Miss

> Family name + **小姐** is “Miss …”: 李小姐. Calling a stranger 小姐 is common in some places and avoided in others; 你好 alone is always safe.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 李 小姐。 | Lǐ xiǎo jie . | Miss Li. |
