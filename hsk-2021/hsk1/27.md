# 第二十七课：在哪儿 | Dì èr shí qī kè : zài nǎ r | HSK 1 · Lesson 27: Where is it?

> **Draft** — not yet reviewed by a native speaker.

> **Goal:** Say where things are — in front, behind, left, right, beside, between — and use 前 and 后 for before, after and ago.

## 语法：前边 | Yǔ fǎ : qián bian | Grammar: Place + 前边, 后边, 旁边 …

> The landmark comes first and the direction word after it: 学校前边 “in front of the school”, 我旁边 “next to me”, 爸爸和妈妈中间 “between Dad and Mom”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 车站 就 在 学校 旁边。 | Chē zhàn jiù zài xué xiào páng biān . | The bus stop is right next to the school. |

## 语法：在有是 | Yǔ fǎ : zài yǒu shì | Grammar: 在, 有 and 是 for where things are

> **X 在 Y旁边** says where X is: 车站在学校旁边. **Y旁边有 X** says what's there: 学校旁边有车站 “there's a bus stop next to the school”. **Y旁边是 X** names what's there: 我家旁边是教会.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 学校 前边 有 一 个 车站。 | Xué xiào qián bian yǒu yí gè chē zhàn . | There's a bus stop in front of the school. |
| 我 家 旁边 是 教会。 | Wǒ jiā páng biān shì jiào huì . | Next to my home is the church. |

## 语法：前后 | Yǔ fǎ : qián hòu | Grammar: 前 and 后 for time

> After an event, **前** means “before” and **后** “after”: 吃饭前, 下课后. After a length of time, 前 means “ago” and 后 “in … time”: 三天前 “three days ago”, 三天后 “in three days”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 下课 后 我 去 商店。 | Xià kè hòu wǒ qù shāng diàn . | After class I go to the shop. |
| 三 天 前。 | Sān tiān qián . | Three days ago. |
