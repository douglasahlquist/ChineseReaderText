## 语法：对错 | Yǔ fǎ : duì cuò | Grammar: 对, 错 and 不对

> **对** is “right”, **错** and **不对** are “wrong”. A verb + 错 says something was done wrong: 写错了 “wrote it wrong”, 坐错车了 “took the wrong bus”. 不错 means “not bad, pretty good”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 字 写 错 了。 | Zhè gè zì xiě cuò le . | This character is written wrong. |
| 不 错！ | Bú cuò ! | Not bad! |

## 语法：是不是 | Yǔ fǎ : shì bu shì | Grammar: 是不是: checking what you think

> **是不是** before a verb, or at the end, checks something you think is true: 你是不是病了？ “Are you sick?”, 你累了，是不是？ “You're tired, aren't you?”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 累 了， 是不是？ | Nǐ lèi le , shì bu shì ? | You're tired, aren't you? |

## 语法：行 | Yǔ fǎ : xíng | Grammar: 行 and 不行

> **行** means “OK, that works”; **不行** is “no, that won't do”. Ask permission with …，行吗？ or 行不行？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 明天 去， 行 吗？ | Wǒ míng tiān qù , xíng ma ? | I'll go tomorrow, is that OK? |
| 不 行。 | Bù xíng . | No way. |

## 语法：没什么 | Yǔ fǎ : méi shén me | Grammar: 没什么 and 没事儿

> **没什么** “it's nothing” answers thanks or worry, like 没事儿. Before a noun it means “not much”: 没什么钱 “not much money”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 谢谢！ —— 没什么。 | Xiè xie ! Méi shén me . | Thanks! — Don't mention it. |
