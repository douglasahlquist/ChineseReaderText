> **Goal:** Tell the time every way: hours and minutes, half and quarter hours, the minutes before the hour with 差, parts of the day, and how long with 分钟 and 小时.

## 语法：点分 | Yǔ fǎ : diǎn fēn | Grammar: Hours and minutes: 点 and 分

> Say the hour with **点** and the minutes with **分**: 三点十分 “3:10”, 八点二十五分 “8:25”. For one to nine minutes add 零: 八点零五分 “8:05”. Two o'clock is 两点, never 二点.

> On the hour you can add **钟**: 三点钟 “three o'clock”. Ask 现在几点？ or, for the exact time, 现在几点几分？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 现在 几 点 几 分？ | Xiàn zài jǐ diǎn jǐ fēn ? | What's the exact time? |
| 八 点 零 五 分。 | Bā diǎn líng wǔ fēn . | 8:05. |
| 两 点 钟。 | Liǎng diǎn zhōng . | Two o'clock. |

## 语法：半刻 | Yǔ fǎ : bàn kè | Grammar: Half and quarter hours: 半 and 刻

> **半** is half past: 三点半 “3:30”. **刻** is a quarter of an hour: 一刻 is 15 minutes and 三刻 is 45, so 三点一刻 is 3:15 and 八点三刻 is 8:45. (There's no 两刻; say 半.)

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 三 点 半。 | Sān diǎn bàn . | Half past three. |
| 三 点 一 刻。 | Sān diǎn yí kè . | A quarter past three. |
| 八 点 三 刻。 | Bā diǎn sān kè . | A quarter to nine. |

## 语法：差 | Yǔ fǎ : chà | Grammar: Before the hour: 差

> **差** “short of” counts the minutes left before the hour, and comes first: 差五分十点 “five to ten”, 差一刻八点 “a quarter to eight”. You can also simply say 九点五十五分.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 差 五 分 十 点。 | Chà wǔ fēn shí diǎn . | Five to ten. |
| 差 一 刻 八 点。 | Chà yí kè bā diǎn . | A quarter to eight. |

## 语法：上午下午 | Yǔ fǎ : shàng wǔ xià wǔ | Grammar: The part of the day comes first

> Chinese uses the part of the day instead of a.m. and p.m., and puts it before the time: 早上七点, 上午十点, 中午十二点, 下午三点半, 晚上八点一刻. The whole time goes before the verb: 我晚上八点吃晚饭.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 下午 三 点 半。 | Xià wǔ sān diǎn bàn . | 3:30 p.m. |
| 我 晚上 八 点 吃 晚饭。 | Wǒ wǎn shang bā diǎn chī wǎn fàn . | I have dinner at 8 p.m. |

## 语法：分钟 | Yǔ fǎ : fēn zhōng | Grammar: A point in time, or how long

> **分** and **点** name a point on the clock and go before the verb: 我八点上课. **分钟** and **小时** say how long something lasts, and go after the verb: 我等了二十分钟, 我学习了两个小时.

> **从……到……** gives a span: 礼拜从十点到十一点半.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 八 点 上课。 | Wǒ bā diǎn shàng kè . | I have class at eight. |
| 我 等 了 二十 分钟。 | Wǒ děng le èr shí fēn zhōng . | I waited twenty minutes. |
| 礼拜 从 十 点 到 十一 点 半。 | Lǐ bài cóng shí diǎn dào shí yī diǎn bàn . | The service is from 10:00 to 11:30. |

## 对话一 | Duì huà yī | Dialog 1 · What time is it?

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| Xiao：妈妈， 现在 几 点？ | Mā ma , xiàn zài jǐ diǎn ? | Mom, what time is it? |
| Mama：七 点 一 刻。 | Qī diǎn yí kè . | A quarter past seven. |
| Xiao：我 几 点 上学？ | Wǒ jǐ diǎn shàng xué ? | What time do I leave for school? |
| Mama：七 点 半。 你 还有 十五 分钟！ | Qī diǎn bàn . Nǐ hái yǒu shí wǔ fēn zhōng ! | Half past seven. You have fifteen minutes! |
| Xiao：我 马上 好！ | Wǒ mǎ shàng hǎo ! | I'll be ready in a moment! |
