## 语法：在 | Yǔ fǎ : zài | Grammar: 在 + place: “be at / in”

> **在** (zài) + a place says where someone or something is: 我在家 “I'm at home”. Its negative is 不在: 爷爷不在家.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他 在 教会。 | Tā zài jiào huì . | He's at church. |

## 语法：去来 | Yǔ fǎ : qù lái | Grammar: 去 and 来 + place (no “to”)

> **去** (qù) “go” and **来** (lái) “come” take the place directly, with no word for “to”: 我去教会 “I go to church”, 你来我家吗？ “Are you coming to my home?”

> 想 + verb means “want to”: 我想去 “I want to go”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 去 教会。 | Wǒ qù jiào huì . | I'm going to church. |
| 你 想 去 吗？ | Nǐ xiǎng qù ma ? | Do you want to go? |

## 语法：哪儿 | Yǔ fǎ : nǎ r | Grammar: Where? 哪儿 and 哪里

> **哪儿** (nǎr) and **哪里** (nǎ li) both mean “where”, and like other question words they go where the answer goes: 你去哪儿？ → 我去教会。 哪儿, 这儿, 那儿 are more northern; 哪里, 这里, 那里 are used everywhere.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 教会 在 哪儿？ | Jiào huì zài nǎ r ? | Where is the church? |
| 教会 在 这里。 | Jiào huì zài zhè li . | The church is here. |

## 语法：和 | Yǔ fǎ : hé | Grammar: 和 joins nouns

> **和** (hé) means “and”, but only between nouns or people: 爸爸和妈妈. It doesn't join two sentences.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 安娜 和 小月 是 朋友。 | Ān nà hé Xiǎo yuè shì péng you . | Anna and Xiaoyue are friends. |
