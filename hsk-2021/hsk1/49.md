## 语法：到 | Yǔ fǎ : dào | Grammar: 到: arrive, and up to

> **到** is “arrive”: 我到了 “I'm here”. After a verb it means “up to”: 学到第十课 “studied up to lesson ten”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 到 了！ | Wǒ dào le ! | I'm here! |
| 我们 学 到 第 十 课 了。 | Wǒ men xué dào dì shí kè le . | We've studied up to lesson ten. |

## 语法：没 | Yǔ fǎ : méi | Grammar: 没, short for 没有

> **没** is the everyday short form of 没有: 我没去 “I didn't go”, 我没钱 “I don't have any money”, 还没到 “not there yet”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 没 去。 | Wǒ méi qù . | I didn't go. |
| 我 还 没 到。 | Wǒ hái méi dào . | I'm not there yet. |

## 语法：一半 | Yǔ fǎ : yí bàn | Grammar: 一半, 半年 and 半

> **一半** is “half (of something)”: 我吃了一半. With time, 半 goes straight before the unit: **半年** “half a year”, 半个小时 “half an hour”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 吃 了 一半。 | Wǒ chī le yí bàn . | I ate half. |
| 我 学 了 半年 汉语。 | Wǒ xué le bàn nián Hàn yǔ . | I've studied Chinese for half a year. |

## 语法：路上 | Yǔ fǎ : lù shang | Grammar: 路上: on the way

> **路上** is “on the way”: 我在路上 “I'm on my way”. 回家的路上 is “on the way home”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 回家 的 路上 我 买 了 水果。 | Huí jiā de lù shang wǒ mǎi le shuǐ guǒ . | I bought fruit on the way home. |
