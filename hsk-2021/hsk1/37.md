## 语法：早晚 | Yǔ fǎ : zǎo wǎn | Grammar: 早 and 晚

> **早** is “early” and **晚** is “late”: 我起床很早. 来晚了 is “arrived late”. On its own, **早！** is a quick “good morning!”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 对不起， 我 来 晚 了。 | Duì bu qǐ , wǒ lái wǎn le . | Sorry I'm late. |
| 我 起床 很 早。 | Wǒ qǐ chuáng hěn zǎo . | I get up very early. |

## 语法：快 | Yǔ fǎ : kuài | Grammar: 快 and 快……了

> **快** is “fast” or “hurry”: 快来！ **快……了** means “almost” or “about to”: 快十点了 “it's almost ten”, 快下雨了 “it's about to rain”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 快 十 点 了。 | Kuài shí diǎn le . | It's almost ten. |

## 语法：回来 | Yǔ fǎ : huí lai | Grammar: 回, 回家 and 回来

> **回** + place is “go back to”: 回北京, 回学校. **回家** is “go home”. **回来** is “come back” (toward where the speaker is).

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 回家 了。 | Wǒ huí jiā le . | I'm going home. |
| 爸爸 回来 了。 | Bà ba huí lai le . | Dad's back. |

## 语法：一些 | Yǔ fǎ : yì xiē | Grammar: Adjective + 一些: a bit more

> **一些** after an adjective means “a bit more …”: 慢一些 “a bit slower”, 早一些 “a bit earlier”. 请慢一些说 “please speak more slowly”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 请 慢 一些 说。 | Qǐng màn yì xiē shuō . | Please speak more slowly. |
