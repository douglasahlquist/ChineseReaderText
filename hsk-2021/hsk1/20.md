# 第二十课：说中文 | Dì èr shí kè : shuō zhōng wén | HSK 1 · Lesson 20: Speaking Chinese

> **Draft** — not yet reviewed by a native speaker.

## 语法：会能 | Yǔ fǎ : huì néng | Grammar: 会 and 能: can

> **会** is “can” for something learned: 我会说汉语 “I can speak Chinese”. It also means “will (probably)”: 他会来 “he'll come”.

> **能** is “can” in the sense of able to, or allowed to: 你能来吗？ “Can you come?”, 上课的时候不能说话 “you can't talk in class”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 会 说 汉语 吗？ | Nǐ huì shuō Hàn yǔ ma ? | Can you speak Chinese? |
| 你 能 来 吗？ | Nǐ néng lái ma ? | Can you come? |

## 语法：知道 | Yǔ fǎ : zhī dào | Grammar: 知道 and 认识

> **知道** is to know a fact: 我知道安娜在哪儿. **认识** is to know a person, or to recognize a character: 我认识她, 我认识这个字.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 知道 她 是 谁。 | Wǒ zhī dào tā shì shéi . | I know who she is. |
| 我 认识 她。 | Wǒ rèn shi tā . | I know her. |

## 语法：听到 | Yǔ fǎ : tīng dào | Grammar: 听, 听到 and 听见

> **听** is to listen. **听到** and **听见** are to hear, that is, the sound reaches you. Say what you didn't hear with 没有: 我没有听见.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 在 听。 | Wǒ zài tīng . | I'm listening. |
| 我 没有 听见。 | Wǒ méi yǒu tīng jiàn . | I didn't hear it. |

## 语法：跟 | Yǔ fǎ : gēn | Grammar: 跟: with, from, after

> **跟** + person means “with”: 我跟安娜去. 跟我读 is “read after me”, 跟李老师学 “learn from Ms. Li”, and 跟他说 “tell him”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 跟 安娜 去 商场。 | Wǒ gēn Ān nà qù shāng chǎng . | I'm going to the mall with Anna. |
| 跟 我 读！ | Gēn wǒ dú ! | Read after me! |
