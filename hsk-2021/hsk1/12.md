## 语法：要 | Yǔ fǎ : yào | Grammar: 要 and 想: want

> **要** + a thing means “want (it)”, and is how you order or accept: 我要一杯水 “I'll have a glass of water”. A one-word 要 means “yes, please”.

> **想** + verb is the softer “would like to”: 我想喝水. 要 + verb is firmer: “want to, going to”.

> **要……了** means “about to”: 我们要去教会了 “we're about to go to church”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 要 茶 吗？ | Nǐ yào chá ma ? | Would you like tea? |
| 我们 要 去 教会 了。 | Wǒ men yào qù jiào huì le . | We're about to go to church. |

## 语法：杯 | Yǔ fǎ : bēi | Grammar: 杯 as a measure word

> **杯** (bēi) “cup, glass” works like 个 between a number and a drink: 一杯水, 两杯茶. 杯子 is the cup itself: 这个杯子.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一 杯 茶 | Yì bēi chá | a cup of tea |
| 这 个 杯子 | Zhè gè bēi zi | this cup |

## 语法：饿了 | Yǔ fǎ : è le | Grammar: 饿了, 渴了: a new state

> 了 after a word like 饿 or 渴 says the state is new: **我饿了** “I'm hungry (now)”. Without 了, 我很饿 simply describes: “I'm very hungry”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 饿 了。 | Wǒ è le . | I'm hungry. |
| 我 很 渴。 | Wǒ hěn kě . | I'm very thirsty. |
