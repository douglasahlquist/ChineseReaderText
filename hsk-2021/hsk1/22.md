## 语法：看 | Yǔ fǎ : kàn | Grammar: 看: look, watch and read

> **看** covers looking, watching (TV, a movie) and reading to yourself: 看书 “read a book”. 读 is reading aloud, or studying a text.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 在 看 书。 | Wǒ zài kàn shū . | I'm reading. |
| 我 在 看 电视。 | Wǒ zài kàn diàn shì . | I'm watching TV. |

## 语法：看见 | Yǔ fǎ : kàn jiàn | Grammar: 看 and 看到 / 看见

> 看 is to look; **看到** and **看见** are to see, that is, to catch sight of something. A sentence like 我看见他了 says it happened; the negative drops 了: 我没有看见他.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 看见 他 了。 | Wǒ kàn jiàn tā le . | I saw him. |
| 我 没有 看见 你。 | Wǒ méi yǒu kàn jiàn nǐ . | I didn't see you. |

## 语法：好看 | Yǔ fǎ : hǎo kàn | Grammar: 好 + verb: good to …

> **好** before a verb makes “good to …”: 好看 “good-looking, good to watch”, 好吃 “tasty”, 好玩儿 “fun”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 电影 很 好看。 | Zhè gè diàn yǐng hěn hǎo kàn . | This movie is very good. |

## 对话一 | Duì huà yī | Dialog 1 · Movie night

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| Ming：安娜， 星期六 你 想 看 电影 吗？ | Ān nà , xīng qī liù nǐ xiǎng kàn diàn yǐng ma ? | Anna, do you want to see a movie on Saturday? |
| Anna：想！ 什么 电影？ | Xiǎng ! Shén me diàn yǐng ? | Yes! What movie? |
| Ming：一 个 很 好玩儿 的 电影。 | Yí gè hěn hǎo wán r de diàn yǐng . | A really fun one. |
| Anna：在 哪儿 看？ | Zài nǎ r kàn ? | Where? |

| Anna：好！ 几 点？ | Hǎo ! Jǐ diǎn ? | Great! What time? |
| Ming：晚上 七 点。 | Wǎn shang qī diǎn . | Seven in the evening. |
