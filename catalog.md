# Catalog

Sections that can be added from the ＋ button in the app. Each `##` heading is one section:
`id · title`. The id is what the app remembers once the section is added, so keep it the same
when the title changes. Under the heading: the section's index file, then one line describing it.

- index: [dialogs/README.md](dialogs/README.md)
- Conversations for everyday life, from beginner to advanced.

## hsk-2021 · HSK-2021
- index: [hsk-2021/README.md](hsk-2021/README.md)
- Courses and word lists for the 2021 HSK standard (GF 0025-2021): HSK 1 (506 words) and HSK 2 (750 words) courses, word lists to HSK 7–9.
