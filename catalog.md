# Catalog

Sections that can be added from the ＋ button in the app. Each `##` heading is one section:
`id · title`. The id is what the app remembers once the section is added, so keep it the same
when the title changes. Under the heading: the section's index file, then one line describing it.

## dialogs · 对话 Dialogs
- index: [dialogs/README.md](dialogs/README.md)
- Conversations for everyday life, from beginner to advanced.

## hsk-2021 · HSK-2021
- index: [hsk-2021/README.md](hsk-2021/README.md)
- Courses and word lists for the 2021 HSK standard (GF 0025-2021): HSK 1 (506 words) and HSK 2 (750 words) courses, word lists to HSK 7–9.

## hsk-standard · HSK 标准教程 Standard Course
- index: [hsk-standard/README.md](hsk-standard/README.md)
- Lessons on the chapters of HSK Standard Course (HSK 2.0 textbooks): each chapter's words and grammar with new sentences and dialogs.

## hsk-2025 · HSK-2025
- index: [hsk-2025/README.md](hsk-2025/README.md)
- Word lists of the HSK 3.0 exam syllabus (November 2025; exams from 13 December 2026): HSK 1 (300 words) to HSK 7–9 (11,000 in all), with pinyin, traditional forms and English.

## bible · 圣经 The Bible
- index: [bible/README.md](bible/README.md)
- The books of the Old and New Testaments, in Chinese with English.

## idioms · 成语 Idioms
- index: [idioms/README.md](idioms/README.md)
- About 6,300 common Chinese idioms with pinyin, Chinese meaning and English.

## egyptian-glyphs · 𓂀 Egyptian Glyphs
- index: [egyptian/README.md](egyptian/README.md)
- Gardiner's list of hieroglyphic signs: 769 signs in 26 groups, each with its code, what it shows and how it's used.

## music · 诗歌 Music
- index: [music/README.md](music/README.md)
- Hymns and songs.

## other-topics · 其他 Other Topics
- index: [other_topics/README.md](other_topics/README.md)
