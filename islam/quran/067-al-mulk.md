# 67. Al-Mulk

> Arabic (Uthmani script) · Romanization (generated, approximate) · Pickthall, The Meaning of the Glorious Koran (1930)

## Verses 1–10

<!-- reader: table=verses -->
| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 1 تَبَـٰرَكَ ٱلَّذِى بِيَدِهِ ٱلْمُلْكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ قَدِيرٌ | Tabāraka ladhi biyadihi l-mulku wahuwa ʿalā kulli shāʾin qadīr | Blessed is He in Whose hand is the Sovereignty, and, He is Able to do all things. |
| 2 ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلْمَوْتَ وَٱلْحَيَوٰةَ لِيَبْلُوَكُمْ أَيُّكُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًا ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْغَفُورُ | Alladhi khalaqa l-mawta walḥayawāta liyabluwakum ʾayyukum ʾaḥsanu ʿamalan wahuwa l-ʿazīzu l-ghafūr | Who hath created life and death that He may try you which of you is best in conduct; and He is the Mighty, the Forgiving, |
| 3 ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمَـٰوَٰتٍ طِبَاقًا ۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِى خَلْقِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ مِن تَفَـٰوُتٍ ۖ فَٱرْجِعِ ٱلْبَصَرَ هَلْ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورٍ | Alladhi khalaqa sabʿa samāwātin ṭibāqan mā tarā fi khalqi r-raḥmāni min tafāwutin farjiʿi l-baṣara hal tarā min fuṭūr | Who hath created seven heavens in harmony. Thou (Muhammad) canst see no fault in the Beneficent One's creation; then look again: Canst thou see any rifts? |
| 4 ثُمَّ ٱرْجِعِ ٱلْبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيْنِ يَنقَلِبْ إِلَيْكَ ٱلْبَصَرُ خَاسِئًا وَهُوَ حَسِيرٌ | Thumma rjiʿi l-baṣara karratayni yanqalib ʾilayka l-baṣaru khāsiʾan wahuwa ḥasīr | Then look again and yet again, thy sight will return unto thee weakened and made dim. |
| 5 وَلَقَدْ زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنْيَا بِمَصَـٰبِيحَ وَجَعَلْنَـٰهَا رُجُومًا لِّلشَّيَـٰطِينِ ۖ وَأَعْتَدْنَا لَهُمْ عَذَابَ ٱلسَّعِيرِ | Walaqad zayyannā s-samāʾa d-dunyā bimaṣābīḥa wajaʿalnāhā rujūman lilshshayāṭīni waʾaʿtadnā lahum ʿadhāba s-saʿīr | And verily We have beautified the world's heaven with lamps, and We have made them missiles for the devils, and for them We have prepared the doom of flame. |
| 6 وَلِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ بِرَبِّهِمْ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ ۖ وَبِئْسَ ٱلْمَصِيرُ | Walilladhīna kafarū birabbihim ʿadhābu jahannama wabiʾsa l-maṣīr | And for those who disbelieve in their Lord there is the doom of hell, a hapless journey's end! |
| 7 إِذَآ أُلْقُوا۟ فِيهَا سَمِعُوا۟ لَهَا شَهِيقًا وَهِىَ تَفُورُ | ʾidhā ʾulqū fīhā samiʿū lahā shahīqan wahiya tafūr | When they are flung therein they hear its roaring as it boileth up, |
| 8 تَكَادُ تَمَيَّزُ مِنَ ٱلْغَيْظِ ۖ كُلَّمَآ أُلْقِىَ فِيهَا فَوْجٌ سَأَلَهُمْ خَزَنَتُهَآ أَلَمْ يَأْتِكُمْ نَذِيرٌ | Takādu tamayyazu mina l-ghayẓi kullamā ʾulqiya fīhā fawjun saʾalahum khazanatuhā ʾalam yaʾtikum nadhīr | As it would burst with rage. Whenever a (fresh) host is flung therein the wardens thereof ask them: Came there unto you no warner? |
| 9 قَالُوا۟ بَلَىٰ قَدْ جَآءَنَا نَذِيرٌ فَكَذَّبْنَا وَقُلْنَا مَا نَزَّلَ ٱللَّهُ مِن شَىْءٍ إِنْ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا فِى ضَلَـٰلٍ كَبِيرٍ | Qālū balā qad jāʾanā nadhīrun fakadhdhabnā waqulnā mā nazzala llāhu min shāʾin ʾin ʾantum ʾillā fi ḍalālin kabīr | They say: Yea, verily, a warner came unto us; but we denied and said: Allah hath naught revealed; ye are in naught but a great error. |
| 10 وَقَالُوا۟ لَوْ كُنَّا نَسْمَعُ أَوْ نَعْقِلُ مَا كُنَّا فِىٓ أَصْحَـٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ | Waqālū law kunnā nasmaʿu ʾaw naʿqilu mā kunnā fi ʾaṣḥābi s-saʿīr | And they say: Had we been wont to listen or have sense, we had not been among the dwellers in the flames. |

<!-- reader: table=verses -->
| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |

| 11 فَٱعْتَرَفُوا۟ بِذَنۢبِهِمْ فَسُحْقًا لِّأَصْحَـٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ | Faʿtarafū bidhanbihim fasuḥqan liʾaṣḥābi s-saʿīr | So they acknowledge their sins; but far removed (from mercy) are the dwellers in the flames. |
| 12 إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخْشَوْنَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلْغَيْبِ لَهُم مَّغْفِرَةٌ وَأَجْرٌ كَبِيرٌ | ʾinna ladhīna yakhshawna rabbahum bilghaybi lahum maghfiratun waʾajrun kabīr | Lo! those who fear their Lord in secret, theirs will be forgiveness and a great reward. |
| 13 وَأَسِرُّوا۟ قَوْلَكُمْ أَوِ ٱجْهَرُوا۟ بِهِۦٓ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمٌۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ | Waʾasirrū qawlakum ʾawi jharū bihi ʾinnahu ʿalīmun bidhāti ṣ-ṣudūr | And keep your opinion secret or proclaim it, lo! He is Knower of all that is in the breasts (of men). |
| 14 أَلَا يَعْلَمُ مَنْ خَلَقَ وَهُوَ ٱللَّطِيفُ ٱلْخَبِيرُ | ʾalā yaʿlamu man khalaqa wahuwa l-laṭīfu l-khabīr | Should He not know what He created? And He is the Subtile, the Aware. |
| 15 هُوَ ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلْأَرْضَ ذَلُولًا فَٱمْشُوا۟ فِى مَنَاكِبِهَا وَكُلُوا۟ مِن رِّزْقِهِۦ ۖ وَإِلَيْهِ ٱلنُّشُورُ | Huwa ladhi jaʿala lakumu l-ʾarḍa dhalūlan famshū fi manākibihā wakulū min rizqihi waʾilayhi n-nushūr | He it is Who hath made the earth subservient unto you, so Walk in the paths thereof and eat of His providence. And unto Him will be the resurrection (of the dead). |
| 16 ءَأَمِنتُم مَّن فِى ٱلسَّمَآءِ أَن يَخْسِفَ بِكُمُ ٱلْأَرْضَ فَإِذَا هِىَ تَمُورُ | ʾaʾamintum man fi s-samāʾi ʾan yakhsifa bikumu l-ʾarḍa faʾidhā hiya tamūr | Have ye taken security from Him Who is in the heaven that He will not cause the earth to swallow you when lo! it is convulsed? |
| 17 أَمْ أَمِنتُم مَّن فِى ٱلسَّمَآءِ أَن يُرْسِلَ عَلَيْكُمْ حَاصِبًا ۖ فَسَتَعْلَمُونَ كَيْفَ نَذِيرِ | ʾam ʾamintum man fi s-samāʾi ʾan yursila ʿalaykum ḥāṣiban fasataʿlamūna kayfa nadhīr | Or have ye taken security from Him Who is in the heaven that He will not let loose on you a hurricane? But ye shall know the manner of My warning. |
| 18 وَلَقَدْ كَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ فَكَيْفَ كَانَ نَكِيرِ | Walaqad kadhdhaba ladhīna min qablihim fakayfa kāna nakīr | And verily those before them denied, then (see) the manner of My wrath (with them)! |
| 19 أَوَلَمْ يَرَوْا۟ إِلَى ٱلطَّيْرِ فَوْقَهُمْ صَـٰٓفَّـٰتٍ وَيَقْبِضْنَ ۚ مَا يُمْسِكُهُنَّ إِلَّا ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنُ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِكُلِّ شَىْءٍۭ بَصِيرٌ | ʾawalam yaraw ʾilā ṭ-ṭayri fawqahum ṣāffātin wayaqbiḍna mā yumsikuhunna ʾillā r-raḥmānu ʾinnahu bikulli shāʾin baṣīr | Have they not seen the birds above them spreading out their wings and closing them? Naught upholdeth them save the Beneficent. Lo! He is Seer of all things. |
| 20 أَمَّنْ هَـٰذَا ٱلَّذِى هُوَ جُندٌ لَّكُمْ يَنصُرُكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ۚ إِنِ ٱلْكَـٰفِرُونَ إِلَّا فِى غُرُورٍ | ʾamman hādhā ladhi huwa jundun lakum yanṣurukum min dūni r-raḥmāni ʾini l-kāfirūna ʾillā fi ghurūr | Or who is he that will be an army unto you to help you instead of the Beneficent? The disbelievers are in naught but illusion. |

| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 21 أَمَّنْ هَـٰذَا ٱلَّذِى يَرْزُقُكُمْ إِنْ أَمْسَكَ رِزْقَهُۥ ۚ بَل لَّجُّوا۟ فِى عُتُوٍّ وَنُفُورٍ | ʾamman hādhā ladhi yarzuqukum ʾin ʾamsaka rizqahu bal lajjū fi ʿutuwwin wanufūr | Or who is he that will provide for you if He should withhold His providence? Nay, but they are set in pride and frowardness. |
| 22 أَفَمَن يَمْشِى مُكِبًّا عَلَىٰ وَجْهِهِۦٓ أَهْدَىٰٓ أَمَّن يَمْشِى سَوِيًّا عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٍ مُّسْتَقِيمٍ | ʾafaman yamshi mukibban ʿalā wajhihi ʾahdā ʾamman yamshi sawiyyan ʿalā ṣirāṭin mustaqīm | Is he who goeth groping on his face more rightly guided, or he who walketh upright on a straight road? |
| 23 قُلْ هُوَ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنشَأَكُمْ وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمْعَ وَٱلْأَبْصَـٰرَ وَٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةَ ۖ قَلِيلًا مَّا تَشْكُرُونَ | Qul huwa ladhi ʾanshaʾakum wajaʿala lakumu s-samʿa walʾabṣāra walʾafidata qalīlan mā tashkurūn | Say (unto them, O Muhammad): He it is who gave you being, and hath assigned unto you ears and eyes and hearts. Small thanks give ye! |
| 24 قُلْ هُوَ ٱلَّذِى ذَرَأَكُمْ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ وَإِلَيْهِ تُحْشَرُونَ | Qul huwa ladhi dharaʾakum fi l-ʾarḍi waʾilayhi tuḥsharūn | Say: He it is Who multiplieth you in the earth, and unto Whom ye will be gathered. |
| 25 وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَـٰذَا ٱلْوَعْدُ إِن كُنتُمْ صَـٰدِقِينَ | Wayaqūlūna matā hādhā l-waʿdu ʾin kuntum ṣādiqīn | And they say: When (will) this promise (be fulfilled), if ye are truthful? |
| 26 قُلْ إِنَّمَا ٱلْعِلْمُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَإِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ نَذِيرٌ مُّبِينٌ | Qul ʾinnamā l-ʿilmu ʿinda llāhi waʾinnamā ʾanā nadhīrun mubīn | Say: The knowledge is with Allah only, and I am but a plain warner; |
| 27 فَلَمَّا رَأَوْهُ زُلْفَةً سِيٓـَٔتْ وُجُوهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ وَقِيلَ هَـٰذَا ٱلَّذِى كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَدَّعُونَ | Falammā raʾawhu zulfatan siyat wujūhu ladhīna kafarū waqīla hādhā ladhi kuntum bihi taddaʿūn | But when they see it nigh, the faces of those who disbelieve will be awry, and it will be said (unto them): This is that for which ye used to call. |
| 28 قُلْ أَرَءَيْتُمْ إِنْ أَهْلَكَنِىَ ٱللَّهُ وَمَن مَّعِىَ أَوْ رَحِمَنَا فَمَن يُجِيرُ ٱلْكَـٰفِرِينَ مِنْ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ | Qul ʾaraʾaytum ʾin ʾahlakaniya llāhu waman maʿiya ʾaw raḥimanā faman yujīru l-kāfirīna min ʿadhābin ʾalīm | Say (O Muhammad): Have ye thought: Whether Allah causeth me (Muhammad) and those with me to perish or hath mercy on us, still, who will protect the disbelievers from a painful doom? |
| 29 قُلْ هُوَ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنُ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ وَعَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْنَا ۖ فَسَتَعْلَمُونَ مَنْ هُوَ فِى ضَلَـٰلٍ مُّبِينٍ | Qul huwa r-raḥmānu ʾāmannā bihi waʿalayhi tawakkalnā fasataʿlamūna man huwa fi ḍalālin mubīn | Say: He is the Beneficent. In Him we believe and in Him we put our trust. And ye will soon know who it is that is in error manifest. |
| 30 قُلْ أَرَءَيْتُمْ إِنْ أَصْبَحَ مَآؤُكُمْ غَوْرًا فَمَن يَأْتِيكُم بِمَآءٍ مَّعِينٍۭ | Qul ʾaraʾaytum ʾin ʾaṣbaḥa māʾukum ghawran faman yaʾtīkum bimāʾin maʿīn | Say: Have ye thought: If (all) your water were to disappear into the earth, who then could bring you gushing water? |
