> Arabic (Uthmani script) · Romanization (generated, approximate) · Pickthall, The Meaning of the Glorious Koran (1930)

## Verses 1–10

<!-- reader: table=verses -->
| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 1 ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ | Iqraʾ bismi rabbika ladhi khalaq | Read: In the name of thy Lord Who createth, |
| 2 خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ | Khalaqa l-ʾinsāna min ʿalaq | Createth man from a clot. |
| 3 ٱقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ | Iqraʾ warabbuka l-ʾakram | Read: And thy Lord is the Most Bounteous, |
| 4 ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ | Alladhi ʿallama bilqalam | Who teacheth by the pen, |
| 5 عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ | ʿallama l-ʾinsāna mā lam yaʿlam | Teacheth man that which he knew not. |
| 6 كَلَّآ إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ | Kallā ʾinna l-ʾinsāna layaṭghā | Nay, but verily man is rebellious |
| 7 أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ | ʾan raʾāhu staghnā | That he thinketh himself independent! |
| 8 إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ | ʾinna ʾilā rabbika r-rujʿā | Lo! unto thy Lord is the return. |
| 9 أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ | ʾaraʾayta ladhi yanhā | Hast thou seen him who dissuadeth |
| 10 عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ | ʿabdan ʾidhā ṣallā | A slave when he prayeth? |
