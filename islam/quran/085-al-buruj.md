# 85. Al-Buruj

> Arabic (Uthmani script) · Romanization (generated, approximate) · Pickthall, The Meaning of the Glorious Koran (1930)

## Verses 1–10

<!-- reader: table=verses -->
| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 1 وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلْبُرُوجِ | Walssamāʾi dhāti l-burūj | By the heaven, holding mansions of the stars, |
| 2 وَٱلْيَوْمِ ٱلْمَوْعُودِ | Walyawmi l-mawʿūd | And by the Promised Day. |
| 3 وَشَاهِدٍ وَمَشْهُودٍ | Washāhidin wamashhūd | And by the witness and that whereunto he beareth testimony, |
| 4 قُتِلَ أَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلْأُخْدُودِ | Qutila ʾaṣḥābu l-ʾukhdūd | (Self-)destroyed were the owners of the ditch |
| 5 ٱلنَّارِ ذَاتِ ٱلْوَقُودِ | An-nāri dhāti l-waqūd | Of the fuel-fed fire, |
| 6 إِذْ هُمْ عَلَيْهَا قُعُودٌ | ʾidh hum ʿalayhā quʿūd | When they sat by it, |
| 7 وَهُمْ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفْعَلُونَ بِٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ شُهُودٌ | Wahum ʿalā mā yafʿalūna bilmuʾminīna shuhūd | And were themselves the witnesses of what they did to the believers. |
| 8 وَمَا نَقَمُوا۟ مِنْهُمْ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤْمِنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلْحَمِيدِ | Wamā naqamū minhum ʾillā ʾan yuʾminū billlahi l-ʿazīzi l-ḥamīd | They had naught against them save that they believed in Allah, the Mighty, the Owner of Praise, |
| 9 ٱلَّذِى لَهُۥ مُلْكُ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ شَهِيدٌ | Alladhi lahu mulku s-samāwāti walʾarḍi walllahu ʿalā kulli shāʾin shahīd | Him unto Whom belongeth the Sovereignty of the heavens and the earth; and Allah is of all things the Witness. |
| 10 إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ فَتَنُوا۟ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَٱلْمُؤْمِنَـٰتِ ثُمَّ لَمْ يَتُوبُوا۟ فَلَهُمْ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ وَلَهُمْ عَذَابُ ٱلْحَرِيقِ | ʾinna ladhīna fatanū l-muʾminīna walmuʾmināti thumma lam yatūbū falahum ʿadhābu jahannama walahum ʿadhābu l-ḥarīq | Lo! they who persecute believing men and believing women and repent not, theirs verily will be the doom of hell, and theirs the doom of burning. |

## Verses 11–20

<!-- reader: table=verses -->
| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 11 إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتِ لَهُمْ جَنَّـٰتٌ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَـٰرُ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْفَوْزُ ٱلْكَبِيرُ | ʾinna ladhīna ʾāmanū waʿamilū ṣ-ṣāliḥāti lahum jannātun tajri min taḥtihā l-ʾanhāru dhālika l-fawzu l-kabīr | Lo! those who believe and do good works, theirs will be Gardens underneath which rivers flow. That is the Great Success. |
| 12 إِنَّ بَطْشَ رَبِّكَ لَشَدِيدٌ | ʾinna baṭsha rabbika lashadīd | Lo! the punishment of thy Lord is stern. |
| 13 إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ يُبْدِئُ وَيُعِيدُ | ʾinnahu huwa yubdiʾu wayuʿīd | Lo! He it is Who produceth, then reproduceth, |
| 14 وَهُوَ ٱلْغَفُورُ ٱلْوَدُودُ | Wahuwa l-ghafūru l-wadūd | And He is the Forgiving, the Loving, |
| 15 ذُو ٱلْعَرْشِ ٱلْمَجِيدُ | Dhū l-ʿarshi l-majīd | Lord of the Throne of Glory, |
| 16 فَعَّالٌ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ | Faʿʿālun limā yurīd | Doer of what He will. |
| 17 هَلْ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلْجُنُودِ | Hal ʾatāka ḥadīthu l-junūd | Hath there come unto thee the story of the hosts |
| 18 فِرْعَوْنَ وَثَمُودَ | Firʿawna wathamūd | Of Pharaoh and (the tribe of) Thamud? |
| 19 بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ فِى تَكْذِيبٍ | Bali ladhīna kafarū fi takdhīb | Nay, but those who disbelieve live in denial |
| 20 وَٱللَّهُ مِن وَرَآئِهِم مُّحِيطٌۢ | Walllahu min warāʾihim muḥīṭ | And Allah, all unseen, surroundeth them. |
