| 2 هُدًى وَبُشْرَىٰ لِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ | Hudan wabushrā lilmuʾminīn | A guidance and good tidings for believers |
| 3 ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤْتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُم بِٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ هُمْ يُوقِنُونَ | Alladhīna yuqīmūna ṣ-ṣalawāta wayuʾtūna z-zakawāta wahum bilākhirati hum yūqinūn | Who establish worship and pay the poor-due and are sure of the Hereafter. |
| 4 إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ بِٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ زَيَّنَّا لَهُمْ أَعْمَـٰلَهُمْ فَهُمْ يَعْمَهُونَ | ʾinna ladhīna lā yuʾminūna bilākhirati zayyannā lahum ʾaʿmālahum fahum yaʿmahūn | Lo! as for those who believe not in the Hereafter, We have made their works fairseeming unto them so that they are all astray. |
| 5 أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَهُمْ سُوٓءُ ٱلْعَذَابِ وَهُمْ فِى ٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ هُمُ ٱلْأَخْسَرُونَ | ʾūlāʾika ladhīna lahum sūʾu l-ʿadhābi wahum fi lākhirati humu l-ʾakhsarūn | Those are they for whom is the worst of punishment, and in the Hereafter they will be the greatest losers. |
| 6 وَإِنَّكَ لَتُلَقَّى ٱلْقُرْءَانَ مِن لَّدُنْ حَكِيمٍ عَلِيمٍ | Waʾinnaka latulaqqā l-qurʾāna min ladun ḥakīmin ʿalīm | Lo! as for thee (Muhammad), thou verily receivest the Qur'an from the presence of One Wise, Aware. |
| 7 إِذْ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِأَهْلِهِۦٓ إِنِّىٓ ءَانَسْتُ نَارًا سَـَٔاتِيكُم مِّنْهَا بِخَبَرٍ أَوْ ءَاتِيكُم بِشِهَابٍ قَبَسٍ لَّعَلَّكُمْ تَصْطَلُونَ | ʾidh qāla mūsā liʾahlihi ʾinni ʾānastu nāran sātīkum minhā bikhabarin ʾaw ʾātīkum bishihābin qabasin laʿallakum taṣṭalūn | (Remember) when Moses said unto his household: Lo! I spy afar off a fire; I will bring you tidings thence, or bring to you a borrowed flame that ye may warm yourselves. |
| 8 فَلَمَّا جَآءَهَا نُودِىَ أَنۢ بُورِكَ مَن فِى ٱلنَّارِ وَمَنْ حَوْلَهَا وَسُبْحَـٰنَ ٱللَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ | Falammā jāʾahā nūdiya ʾan būrika man fi n-nāri waman ḥawlahā wasubḥāna llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn | But when he reached it, he was called, saying: Blessed is Whosoever is in the fire and Whosoever is round about it! And Glorified be Allah, the Lord of the Worlds! |
| 9 يَـٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ | Yāmūsā ʾinnahu ʾanā llāhu l-ʿazīzu l-ḥakīm | O Moses! Lo! it is I, Allah, the Mighty, the Wise. |
| 10 وَأَلْقِ عَصَاكَ ۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهَا تَهْتَزُّ كَأَنَّهَا جَآنٌّ وَلَّىٰ مُدْبِرًا وَلَمْ يُعَقِّبْ ۚ يَـٰمُوسَىٰ لَا تَخَفْ إِنِّى لَا يَخَافُ لَدَىَّ ٱلْمُرْسَلُونَ | Waʾalqi ʿaṣāka falammā raʾāhā tahtazzu kaʾannahā jānnun wallā mudbiran walam yuʿaqqib yāmūsā lā takhaf ʾinni lā yakhāfu ladayya l-mursalūn | And throw down thy staff! But when he saw it writhing as it were a demon, he turned to flee headlong; (but it was said unto him): O Moses! Fear not! the emissaries fear not in My presence, |

| 11 إِلَّا مَن ظَلَمَ ثُمَّ بَدَّلَ حُسْنًۢا بَعْدَ سُوٓءٍ فَإِنِّى غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ | ʾillā man ẓalama thumma baddala ḥusnan baʿda sūʾin faʾinni ghafūrun raḥīm | Save him who hath done wrong and afterward hath changed evil for good. And lo! I am Forgiving, Merciful. |
| 12 وَأَدْخِلْ يَدَكَ فِى جَيْبِكَ تَخْرُجْ بَيْضَآءَ مِنْ غَيْرِ سُوٓءٍ ۖ فِى تِسْعِ ءَايَـٰتٍ إِلَىٰ فِرْعَوْنَ وَقَوْمِهِۦٓ ۚ إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا۟ قَوْمًا فَـٰسِقِينَ | Waʾadkhil yadaka fi jaybika takhruj bayḍāʾa min ghayri sūʾin fi tisʿi ʾāyātin ʾilā firʿawna waqawmihi ʾinnahum kānū qawman fāsiqīn | And put thy hand into the bosom of thy robe, it will come forth white but unhurt. (This will be one) among nine tokens unto Pharaoh and his people Lo! they were ever evil-living folk. |
| 13 فَلَمَّا جَآءَتْهُمْ ءَايَـٰتُنَا مُبْصِرَةً قَالُوا۟ هَـٰذَا سِحْرٌ مُّبِينٌ | Falammā jāʾathum ʾāyātunā mubṣiratan qālū hādhā siḥrun mubīn | But when Our tokens came unto them, plain to see, they said: This is mere magic, |
| 14 وَجَحَدُوا۟ بِهَا وَٱسْتَيْقَنَتْهَآ أَنفُسُهُمْ ظُلْمًا وَعُلُوًّا ۚ فَٱنظُرْ كَيْفَ كَانَ عَـٰقِبَةُ ٱلْمُفْسِدِينَ | Wajaḥadū bihā wastayqanathā ʾanfusuhum ẓulman waʿuluwwan fanẓur kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu l-mufsidīn | And they denied them, though their souls acknowledged them, for spite and arrogance. Then see the nature of the consequence for the wrong-doers! |
| 15 وَلَقَدْ ءَاتَيْنَا دَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيْمَـٰنَ عِلْمًا ۖ وَقَالَا ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِى فَضَّلَنَا عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٍ مِّنْ عِبَادِهِ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ | Walaqad ʾātaynā dāwuda wasulaymāna ʿilman waqālā l-ḥamdu lillāhi ladhi faḍḍalanā ʿalā kathīrin min ʿibādihi l-muʾminīn | And We verily gave knowledge unto David and Solomon, and they said: Praise be to Allah, Who hath preferred us above many of His believing slaves! |
| 16 وَوَرِثَ سُلَيْمَـٰنُ دَاوُۥدَ ۖ وَقَالَ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ عُلِّمْنَا مَنطِقَ ٱلطَّيْرِ وَأُوتِينَا مِن كُلِّ شَىْءٍ ۖ إِنَّ هَـٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلْفَضْلُ ٱلْمُبِينُ | Wawaritha sulaymānu dāwuda waqāla yāʾayyuhā n-nāsu ʿullimnā manṭiqa ṭ-ṭayri waʾūtīnā min kulli shāʾin ʾinna hādhā lahuwa l-faḍlu l-mubīn | And Solomon was David's heir. And he said: O mankind! Lo! we have been taught the language of birds, and have been given (abundance) of all things. This surely is evident favour. |
| 17 وَحُشِرَ لِسُلَيْمَـٰنَ جُنُودُهُۥ مِنَ ٱلْجِنِّ وَٱلْإِنسِ وَٱلطَّيْرِ فَهُمْ يُوزَعُونَ | Waḥushira lisulaymāna junūduhu mina l-jinni walʾinsi walṭṭayri fahum yūzaʿūn | And there were gathered together unto Solomon his armies of the jinn and humankind, and of the birds, and they were set in battle order; |
| 18 حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَتَوْا۟ عَلَىٰ وَادِ ٱلنَّمْلِ قَالَتْ نَمْلَةٌ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّمْلُ ٱدْخُلُوا۟ مَسَـٰكِنَكُمْ لَا يَحْطِمَنَّكُمْ سُلَيْمَـٰنُ وَجُنُودُهُۥ وَهُمْ لَا يَشْعُرُونَ | Ḥattā ʾidhā ʾataw ʿalā wādi n-namli qālat namlatun yāʾayyuhā n-namlu dkhulū masākinakum lā yaḥṭimannakum sulaymānu wajunūduhu wahum lā yashʿurūn | Till, when they reached the Valley of the Ants, an ant exclaimed: O ants! Enter your dwellings lest Solomon and his armies crush you, unperceiving. |
| 19 فَتَبَسَّمَ ضَاحِكًا مِّن قَوْلِهَا وَقَالَ رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِىٓ أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِىٓ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَىَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَىَّ وَأَنْ أَعْمَلَ صَـٰلِحًا تَرْضَىٰهُ وَأَدْخِلْنِى بِرَحْمَتِكَ فِى عِبَادِكَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ | Fatabassama ḍāḥikan min qawlihā waqāla rabbi ʾawziʿni ʾan ʾashkura niʿmataka lati ʾanʿamta ʿalayya waʿalā wālidayya waʾan ʾaʿmala ṣāliḥan tarḍāhu waʾadkhilni biraḥmatika fi ʿibādika ṣ-ṣāliḥīn | And (Solomon) smiled, laughing at her speech, and said: My Lord, arouse me to be thankful for Thy favour wherewith Thou hast favoured me and my parents, and to do good that shall be pleasing unto Thee, and include me in (the number of) Thy righteous slaves. |
| 20 وَتَفَقَّدَ ٱلطَّيْرَ فَقَالَ مَا لِىَ لَآ أَرَى ٱلْهُدْهُدَ أَمْ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلْغَآئِبِينَ | Watafaqqada ṭ-ṭayra faqāla mā liya lā ʾarā l-hudhuda ʾam kāna mina l-ghāʾibīn | And he sought among the birds and said: How is it that I see not the hoopoe, or is he among the absent? |

| 23 إِنِّى وَجَدتُّ ٱمْرَأَةً تَمْلِكُهُمْ وَأُوتِيَتْ مِن كُلِّ شَىْءٍ وَلَهَا عَرْشٌ عَظِيمٌ | ʾinni wajadttu mraʾatan tamlikuhum waʾūtiyat min kulli shāʾin walahā ʿarshun ʿaẓīm | Lo! | 24 وَجَدتُّهَا وَقَوْمَهَا يَسْجُدُونَ لِلشَّمْسِ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيْطَـٰنُ أَعْمَـٰلَهُمْ فَصَدَّهُمْ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ فَهُمْ لَا يَهْتَدُونَ | Wajadttuhā waqawmahā yasjudūna lilshshamsi min dūni llāhi wazayyana lahumu sh-shayṭānu ʾaʿmālahum faṣaddahum ʿani s-sabīli fahum lā yahtadūn | I found her and her people worshipping the sun instead of Allah; and Satan maketh their works fairseeming unto them, and debarreth them from the way (of Truth), so that they go not aright; |
| 25 أَلَّا يَسْجُدُوا۟ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِى يُخْرِجُ ٱلْخَبْءَ فِى ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَيَعْلَمُ مَا تُخْفُونَ وَمَا تُعْلِنُونَ | ʾallā yasjudū lillāhi ladhi yukhriju l-khabʾa fi s-samāwāti walʾarḍi wayaʿlamu mā tukhfūna wamā tuʿlinūn | So that they worship not Allah, Who bringeth forth the hidden in the heavens and the earth, and knoweth what ye hide and what ye proclaim, |
| 26 ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَـٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلْعَرْشِ ٱلْعَظِيمِ ۩ | Allāhu lā ʾilāha ʾillā huwa rabbu l-ʿarshi l-ʿaẓīm | Allah; there is no Allah save Him, the Lord of the Tremendous Throne. |
| 27 ۞ قَالَ سَنَنظُرُ أَصَدَقْتَ أَمْ كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلْكَـٰذِبِينَ | Qāla sananẓuru ʾaṣadaqta ʾam kunta mina l-kādhibīn | (Solomon) said: We shall see whether thou speakest truth or whether thou art of the liars. |
| 28 ٱذْهَب بِّكِتَـٰبِى هَـٰذَا فَأَلْقِهْ إِلَيْهِمْ ثُمَّ تَوَلَّ عَنْهُمْ فَٱنظُرْ مَاذَا يَرْجِعُونَ | Idhhab bikitābi hādhā faʾalqih ʾilayhim thumma tawalla ʿanhum fanẓur mādhā yarjiʿūn | Go with this my letter and throw it down unto them; then turn away and see what (answer) they return, |
| 29 قَالَتْ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْمَلَؤُا۟ إِنِّىٓ أُلْقِىَ إِلَىَّ كِتَـٰبٌ كَرِيمٌ | Qālat yāʾayyuhā l-malaʾu ʾinni ʾulqiya ʾilayya kitābun karīm | (The Queen of Sheba) said (when she received the letter): O chieftains! Lo! there hath been thrown unto me a noble letter. |
| 30 إِنَّهُۥ مِن سُلَيْمَـٰنَ وَإِنَّهُۥ بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ | ʾinnahu min sulaymāna waʾinnahu bismi llāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīm | Lo! it is from Solomon, and lo! it is: In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful; |
