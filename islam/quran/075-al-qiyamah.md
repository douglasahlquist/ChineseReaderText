| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |

| 1 لَآ أُقْسِمُ بِيَوْمِ ٱلْقِيَـٰمَةِ | Lā ʾuqsimu biyawmi l-qiyāmat | Nay, I swear by the Day of Resurrection; |
| 2 وَلَآ أُقْسِمُ بِٱلنَّفْسِ ٱللَّوَّامَةِ | Walā ʾuqsimu bilnnafsi l-lawwāmat | Nay, I swear by the accusing soul (that this Scripture is true). |
| 3 أَيَحْسَبُ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ أَلَّن نَّجْمَعَ عِظَامَهُۥ | ʾayaḥsabu l-ʾinsānu ʾallan najmaʿa ʿiẓāmah | Thinketh man that We shall not assemble his bones? |
| 4 بَلَىٰ قَـٰدِرِينَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّسَوِّىَ بَنَانَهُۥ | Balā qādirīna ʿalā ʾan nusawwiya banānah | Yea, verily. We are Able to restore his very fingers! |
| 5 بَلْ يُرِيدُ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ لِيَفْجُرَ أَمَامَهُۥ | Bal yurīdu l-ʾinsānu liyafjura ʾamāmah | But man would fain deny what is before him. |
| 6 يَسْـَٔلُ أَيَّانَ يَوْمُ ٱلْقِيَـٰمَةِ | Yasalu ʾayyāna yawmu l-qiyāmat | He asketh: When will be this Day of Resurrection? |
| 7 فَإِذَا بَرِقَ ٱلْبَصَرُ | Faʾidhā bariqa l-baṣar | But when sight is confounded |
| 8 وَخَسَفَ ٱلْقَمَرُ | Wakhasafa l-qamar | And the moon is eclipsed |
| 9 وَجُمِعَ ٱلشَّمْسُ وَٱلْقَمَرُ | Wajumiʿa sh-shamsu walqamar | And sun and moon are united, |
| 10 يَقُولُ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ يَوْمَئِذٍ أَيْنَ ٱلْمَفَرُّ | Yaqūlu l-ʾinsānu yawmaʾidhin ʾayna l-mafarr | On that day man will cry: Whither to flee! |

| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |

| 11 كَلَّا لَا وَزَرَ | Kallā lā wazar | Alas! No refuge! |
| 12 إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ يَوْمَئِذٍ ٱلْمُسْتَقَرُّ | ʾilā rabbika yawmaʾidhin l-mustaqarr | Unto thy Lord is the recourse that day. |
| 13 يُنَبَّؤُا۟ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ يَوْمَئِذٍۭ بِمَا قَدَّمَ وَأَخَّرَ | Yunabbaʾu l-ʾinsānu yawmaʾidhin bimā qaddama waʾakhkhar | On that day man is told the tale of that which he hath sent before and left behind. |
| 14 بَلِ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ عَلَىٰ نَفْسِهِۦ بَصِيرَةٌ | Bali l-ʾinsānu ʿalā nafsihi baṣīrat | Oh, but man is a telling witness against himself, |
| 15 وَلَوْ أَلْقَىٰ مَعَاذِيرَهُۥ | Walaw ʾalqā maʿādhīrah | Although he tender his excuses. |
| 16 لَا تُحَرِّكْ بِهِۦ لِسَانَكَ لِتَعْجَلَ بِهِۦٓ | Lā tuḥarrik bihi lisānaka litaʿjala bih | Stir not thy tongue herewith to hasten it. |
| 17 إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا جَمْعَهُۥ وَقُرْءَانَهُۥ | ʾinna ʿalaynā jamʿahu waqurʾānah | Lo! upon Us (resteth) the putting together thereof and the reading thereof. |
| 18 فَإِذَا قَرَأْنَـٰهُ فَٱتَّبِعْ قُرْءَانَهُۥ | Faʾidhā qaraʾnāhu fattabiʿ qurʾānah | And when We read it, follow thou the reading; |
| 19 ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا بَيَانَهُۥ | Thumma ʾinna ʿalaynā bayānah | Then lo! upon Us (resteth) the explanation thereof. |
| 20 كَلَّا بَلْ تُحِبُّونَ ٱلْعَاجِلَةَ | Kallā bal tuḥibbūna l-ʿājilat | Nay, but ye do love the fleeting Now |

| 22 وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ نَّاضِرَةٌ | Wujūhun yawmaʾidhin nāḍirat | That day will faces be resplendent, |
| 23 إِلَىٰ رَبِّهَا نَاظِرَةٌ | ʾilā rabbihā nāẓirat | Looking toward their Lord; |
| 24 وَوُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍۭ بَاسِرَةٌ | Wawujūhun yawmaʾidhin bāsirat | And that day will other faces be despondent, |
| 25 تَظُنُّ أَن يُفْعَلَ بِهَا فَاقِرَةٌ | Taẓunnu ʾan yufʿala bihā fāqirat | Thou wilt know that some great disaster is about to fall on them. |
| 26 كَلَّآ إِذَا بَلَغَتِ ٱلتَّرَاقِىَ | Kallā ʾidhā balaghati t-tarāqiy | Nay, but when the life cometh up to the throat |
| 27 وَقِيلَ مَنْ ۜ رَاقٍ | Waqīla man rāq | And men say: Where is the wizard (who can save him now)? |
| 28 وَظَنَّ أَنَّهُ ٱلْفِرَاقُ | Waẓanna ʾannahu l-firāq | And he knoweth that it is the parting; |
| 29 وَٱلْتَفَّتِ ٱلسَّاقُ بِٱلسَّاقِ | Waltaffati s-sāqu bilssāq | And agony is heaped on agony; |
| 30 إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ يَوْمَئِذٍ ٱلْمَسَاقُ | ʾilā rabbika yawmaʾidhin l-masāq | Unto thy Lord that day will be the driving. |
