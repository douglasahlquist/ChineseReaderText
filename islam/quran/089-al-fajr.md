## Verses 11–20

<!-- reader: table=verses -->
| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 11 ٱلَّذِينَ طَغَوْا۟ فِى ٱلْبِلَـٰدِ | Alladhīna ṭaghaw fi l-bilād | Who (all) were rebellious (to Allah) in these lands, |
| 12 فَأَكْثَرُوا۟ فِيهَا ٱلْفَسَادَ | Faʾaktharū fīhā l-fasād | And multiplied iniquity therein? |
| 13 فَصَبَّ عَلَيْهِمْ رَبُّكَ سَوْطَ عَذَابٍ | Faṣabba ʿalayhim rabbuka sawṭa ʿadhāb | Therefore thy Lord poured on them the disaster of His punishment. |
| 14 إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَبِٱلْمِرْصَادِ | ʾinna rabbaka labilmirṣād | Lo! thy Lord is ever watchful. |
| 15 فَأَمَّا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ إِذَا مَا ٱبْتَلَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَأَكْرَمَهُۥ وَنَعَّمَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّىٓ أَكْرَمَنِ | Faʾammā l-ʾinsānu ʾidhā mā btalāhu rabbuhu faʾakramahu wanaʿʿamahu fayaqūlu rabbi ʾakraman | As for man, whenever his Lord trieth him by honouring him, and is gracious unto him, he saith: My Lord honoureth me. |
| 16 وَأَمَّآ إِذَا مَا ٱبْتَلَىٰهُ فَقَدَرَ عَلَيْهِ رِزْقَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّىٓ أَهَـٰنَنِ | Waʾammā ʾidhā mā btalāhu faqadara ʿalayhi rizqahu fayaqūlu rabbi ʾahānan | But whenever He trieth him by straitening his means of life, he saith: My Lord despiseth me. |
| 17 كَلَّا ۖ بَل لَّا تُكْرِمُونَ ٱلْيَتِيمَ | Kallā bal lā tukrimūna l-yatīm | Nay, but ye (for your part) honour not the orphan |
| 18 وَلَا تَحَـٰٓضُّونَ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلْمِسْكِينِ | Walā taḥāḍḍūna ʿalā ṭaʿāmi l-miskīn | And urge not on the feeding of the poor. |
| 19 وَتَأْكُلُونَ ٱلتُّرَاثَ أَكْلًا لَّمًّا | Wataʾkulūna t-turātha ʾaklan lammā | And ye devour heritages with devouring greed. |
| 20 وَتُحِبُّونَ ٱلْمَالَ حُبًّا جَمًّا | Watuḥibbūna l-māla ḥubban jammā | And love wealth with abounding love. |

| 28 ٱرْجِعِىٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةً مَّرْضِيَّةً | Irjiʿi ʾilā rabbiki rāḍiyatan marḍiyyatā | Return unto thy Lord, content in His good pleasure! |
