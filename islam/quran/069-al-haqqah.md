# 69. Al-Haqqah

> Arabic (Uthmani script) · Romanization (generated, approximate) · Pickthall, The Meaning of the Glorious Koran (1930)

## Verses 1–10

| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 1 ٱلْحَآقَّةُ | Al-ḥāqqat | The Reality! |
| 2 مَا ٱلْحَآقَّةُ | Mā l-ḥāqqat | What is the Reality? |
| 3 وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْحَآقَّةُ | Wamā ʾadrāka mā l-ḥāqqat | Ah, what will convey unto thee what the reality is! |
| 4 كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ وَعَادٌۢ بِٱلْقَارِعَةِ | Kadhdhabat thamūdu waʿādun bilqāriʿat | (The tribes of) Thamud and A'ad disbelieved in the judgment to come. |
| 5 فَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَأُهْلِكُوا۟ بِٱلطَّاغِيَةِ | Faʾammā thamūdu faʾuhlikū bilṭṭāghiyat | As for Thamud, they were destroyed by the lightning. |
| 6 وَأَمَّا عَادٌ فَأُهْلِكُوا۟ بِرِيحٍ صَرْصَرٍ عَاتِيَةٍ | Waʾammā ʿādun faʾuhlikū birīḥin ṣarṣarin ʿātiyat | And as for A'ad, they were destroyed by a fierce roaring wind, |
| 7 سَخَّرَهَا عَلَيْهِمْ سَبْعَ لَيَالٍ وَثَمَـٰنِيَةَ أَيَّامٍ حُسُومًا فَتَرَى ٱلْقَوْمَ فِيهَا صَرْعَىٰ كَأَنَّهُمْ أَعْجَازُ نَخْلٍ خَاوِيَةٍ | Sakhkharahā ʿalayhim sabʿa layālin wathamāniyata ʾayyāmin ḥusūman fatarā l-qawma fīhā ṣarʿā kaʾannahum ʾaʿjāzu nakhlin khāwiyat | Which He imposed on them for seven long nights and eight long days so that thou mightest have seen men lying overthrown, as they were hollow trunks of palm-trees. |
| 8 فَهَلْ تَرَىٰ لَهُم مِّنۢ بَاقِيَةٍ | Fahal tarā lahum min bāqiyat | Canst thou (O Muhammad) see any remnant of them? |
| 9 وَجَآءَ فِرْعَوْنُ وَمَن قَبْلَهُۥ وَٱلْمُؤْتَفِكَـٰتُ بِٱلْخَاطِئَةِ | Wajāʾa firʿawnu waman qablahu walmuʾtafikātu bilkhāṭiʾat | And Pharaoh and those before him, and the communities that were destroyed, brought error, |
| 10 فَعَصَوْا۟ رَسُولَ رَبِّهِمْ فَأَخَذَهُمْ أَخْذَةً رَّابِيَةً | Faʿaṣaw rasūla rabbihim faʾakhadhahum ʾakhdhatan rābiyatā | And they disobeyed the messenger of their Lord, therefor did He grip them with a tightening grip. |

## Verses 11–20

| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 11 إِنَّا لَمَّا طَغَا ٱلْمَآءُ حَمَلْنَـٰكُمْ فِى ٱلْجَارِيَةِ | ʾinnā lammā ṭaghā l-māʾu ḥamalnākum fi l-jāriyat | Lo! when the waters rose, We carried you upon the ship |
| 12 لِنَجْعَلَهَا لَكُمْ تَذْكِرَةً وَتَعِيَهَآ أُذُنٌ وَٰعِيَةٌ | Linajʿalahā lakum tadhkiratan wataʿiyahā ʾudhunun wāʿiyat | That We might make it a memorial for you, and that remembering ears (that heard the story) might remember. |
| 13 فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِى ٱلصُّورِ نَفْخَةٌ وَٰحِدَةٌ | Faʾidhā nufikha fi ṣ-ṣūri nafkhatun wāḥidat | And when the trumpet shall sound one blast |
| 14 وَحُمِلَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ وَٱلْجِبَالُ فَدُكَّتَا دَكَّةً وَٰحِدَةً | Waḥumilati l-ʾarḍu waljibālu fadukkatā dakkatan wāḥidatā | And the earth with the mountains shall be lifted up and crushed with one crash, |
| 15 فَيَوْمَئِذٍ وَقَعَتِ ٱلْوَاقِعَةُ | Fayawmaʾidhin waqaʿati l-wāqiʿat | Then, on that day will the Event befall. |
| 16 وَٱنشَقَّتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَهِىَ يَوْمَئِذٍ وَاهِيَةٌ | Wanshaqqati s-samāʾu fahiya yawmaʾidhin wāhiyat | And the heaven will split asunder, for that day it will be frail. |
| 17 وَٱلْمَلَكُ عَلَىٰٓ أَرْجَآئِهَا ۚ وَيَحْمِلُ عَرْشَ رَبِّكَ فَوْقَهُمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ ثَمَـٰنِيَةٌ | Walmalaku ʿalā ʾarjāʾihā wayaḥmilu ʿarsha rabbika fawqahum yawmaʾidhin thamāniyat | And the angels will be on the sides thereof, and eight will uphold the Throne of thy Lord that day, above them. |
| 18 يَوْمَئِذٍ تُعْرَضُونَ لَا تَخْفَىٰ مِنكُمْ خَافِيَةٌ | Yawmaʾidhin tuʿraḍūna lā takhfā minkum khāfiyat | On that day ye will be exposed; not a secret of you will be hidden. |
| 19 فَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِىَ كِتَـٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ هَآؤُمُ ٱقْرَءُوا۟ كِتَـٰبِيَهْ | Faʾammā man ʾūtiya kitābahu biyamīnihi fayaqūlu hāʾumu qraʾū kitābiyah | Then, as for him who is given his record in his right hand, he will say: Take, read my book! |
| 20 إِنِّى ظَنَنتُ أَنِّى مُلَـٰقٍ حِسَابِيَهْ | ʾinni ẓanantu ʾanni mulāqin ḥisābiyah | Surely I knew that I should have to meet my reckoning. |

## Verses 41–50

<!-- reader: table=verses -->
| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 41 وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوْلِ شَاعِرٍ ۚ قَلِيلًا مَّا تُؤْمِنُونَ | Wamā huwa biqawli shāʿirin qalīlan mā tuʾminūn | It is not poet's speech - little is it that ye believe! |
| 42 وَلَا بِقَوْلِ كَاهِنٍ ۚ قَلِيلًا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ | Walā biqawli kāhinin qalīlan mā tadhakkarūn | Nor diviner's speech - little is it that ye remember! |
| 43 تَنزِيلٌ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ | Tanzīlun min rabbi l-ʿālamīn | It is a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds. |
| 44 وَلَوْ تَقَوَّلَ عَلَيْنَا بَعْضَ ٱلْأَقَاوِيلِ | Walaw taqawwala ʿalaynā baʿḍa l-ʾaqāwīl | And if he had invented false sayings concerning Us, |
| 45 لَأَخَذْنَا مِنْهُ بِٱلْيَمِينِ | Laʾakhadhnā minhu bilyamīn | We assuredly had taken him by the right hand |
| 46 ثُمَّ لَقَطَعْنَا مِنْهُ ٱلْوَتِينَ | Thumma laqaṭaʿnā minhu l-watīn | And then severed his life-artery, |
| 47 فَمَا مِنكُم مِّنْ أَحَدٍ عَنْهُ حَـٰجِزِينَ | Famā minkum min ʾaḥadin ʿanhu ḥājizīn | And not one of you could have held Us off from him. |
| 48 وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَذْكِرَةٌ لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ | Waʾinnahu latadhkiratun lilmuttaqīn | And lo! it is a warrant unto those who ward off (evil). |
| 49 وَإِنَّا لَنَعْلَمُ أَنَّ مِنكُم مُّكَذِّبِينَ | Waʾinnā lanaʿlamu ʾanna minkum mukadhdhibīn | And lo! We know that some among you will deny (it). |
| 50 وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَسْرَةٌ عَلَى ٱلْكَـٰفِرِينَ | Waʾinnahu laḥasratun ʿalā l-kāfirīn | And lo! it is indeed an anguish for the disbelievers. |
