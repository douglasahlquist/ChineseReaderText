| 2 قُمِ ٱلَّيْلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلًا | Qumi layla ʾillā qalīlā | Keep vigil the night long, save a little - |

| 4 أَوْ زِدْ عَلَيْهِ وَرَتِّلِ ٱلْقُرْءَانَ تَرْتِيلًا | ʾaw zid ʿalayhi warattili l-qurʾāna tartīlā | Or add (a little) thereto - and chant the Qur'an in measure, |
| 5 إِنَّا سَنُلْقِى عَلَيْكَ قَوْلًا ثَقِيلًا | ʾinnā sanulqi ʿalayka qawlan thaqīlā | For we shall charge thee with a word of weight. |
| 6 إِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ ٱلَّيْلِ هِىَ أَشَدُّ وَطْـًٔا وَأَقْوَمُ قِيلًا | ʾinna nāshiʾata layli hiya ʾashaddu waṭan waʾaqwamu qīlā | Lo! the vigil of the night is (a time) when impression is more keen and speech more certain. |
| 7 إِنَّ لَكَ فِى ٱلنَّهَارِ سَبْحًا طَوِيلًا | ʾinna laka fi n-nahāri sabḥan ṭawīlā | Lo! thou hast by day a chain of business. |
| 8 وَٱذْكُرِ ٱسْمَ رَبِّكَ وَتَبَتَّلْ إِلَيْهِ تَبْتِيلًا | Wadhkuri sma rabbika watabattal ʾilayhi tabtīlā | So remember the name of thy Lord and devote thyself with a complete devotion - |
| 9 رَّبُّ ٱلْمَشْرِقِ وَٱلْمَغْرِبِ لَآ إِلَـٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ فَٱتَّخِذْهُ وَكِيلًا | Rabbu l-mashriqi walmaghribi lā ʾilāha ʾillā huwa fattakhidhhu wakīlā | Lord of the East and the West; there is no Allah save Him; so choose thou Him alone for thy defender - |
| 10 وَٱصْبِرْ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَٱهْجُرْهُمْ هَجْرًا جَمِيلًا | Waṣbir ʿalā mā yaqūlūna wahjurhum hajran jamīlā | And bear with patience what they utter, and part from them with a fair leave-taking. |

| 14 يَوْمَ تَرْجُفُ ٱلْأَرْضُ وَٱلْجِبَالُ وَكَانَتِ ٱلْجِبَالُ كَثِيبًا مَّهِيلًا | Yawma tarjufu l-ʾarḍu waljibālu wakānati l-jibālu kathīban mahīlā | On the day when the earth and the hills rock, and the hills become a heap of running sand. |
| 15 إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَآ إِلَيْكُمْ رَسُولًا شَـٰهِدًا عَلَيْكُمْ كَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَآ إِلَىٰ فِرْعَوْنَ رَسُولًا | ʾinnā ʾarsalnā ʾilaykum rasūlan shāhidan ʿalaykum kamā ʾarsalnā ʾilā firʿawna rasūlā | Lo! | 16 فَعَصَىٰ فِرْعَوْنُ ٱلرَّسُولَ فَأَخَذْنَـٰهُ أَخْذًا وَبِيلًا | Faʿaṣā firʿawnu r-rasūla faʾakhadhnāhu ʾakhdhan wabīlā | But Pharaoh rebelled against the messenger, whereupon We seized him with no gentle grip. |
| 17 فَكَيْفَ تَتَّقُونَ إِن كَفَرْتُمْ يَوْمًا يَجْعَلُ ٱلْوِلْدَٰنَ شِيبًا | Fakayfa tattaqūna ʾin kafartum yawman yajʿalu l-wildāna shībā | Then how, if ye disbelieve, will ye protect yourselves upon the day which will turn children grey, |
| 18 ٱلسَّمَآءُ مُنفَطِرٌۢ بِهِۦ ۚ كَانَ وَعْدُهُۥ مَفْعُولًا | As-samāʾu munfaṭirun bihi kāna waʿduhu mafʿūlā | The very heaven being then rent asunder. His promise is to be fulfilled. |
| 19 إِنَّ هَـٰذِهِۦ تَذْكِرَةٌ ۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلًا | ʾinna hādhihi tadhkiratun faman shāʾa takhadha ʾilā rabbihi sabīlā | Lo! This is a Reminder. Let him who will, then, choose a way unto his Lord. |
| 20 ۞ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَعْلَمُ أَنَّكَ تَقُومُ أَدْنَىٰ مِن ثُلُثَىِ ٱلَّيْلِ وَنِصْفَهُۥ وَثُلُثَهُۥ وَطَآئِفَةٌ مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ مَعَكَ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ يُقَدِّرُ ٱلَّيْلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ ۚ عَلِمَ أَن لَّن تُحْصُوهُ فَتَابَ عَلَيْكُمْ ۖ فَٱقْرَءُوا۟ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ ٱلْقُرْءَانِ ۚ عَلِمَ أَن سَيَكُونُ مِنكُم مَّرْضَىٰ ۙ وَءَاخَرُونَ يَضْرِبُونَ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ يَبْتَغُونَ مِن فَضْلِ ٱللَّهِ ۙ وَءَاخَرُونَ يُقَـٰتِلُونَ فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ۖ فَٱقْرَءُوا۟ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنْهُ ۚ وَأَقِيمُوا۟ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُوا۟ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَقْرِضُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ قَرْضًا حَسَنًا ۚ وَمَا تُقَدِّمُوا۟ لِأَنفُسِكُم مِّنْ خَيْرٍ تَجِدُوهُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ هُوَ خَيْرًا وَأَعْظَمَ أَجْرًا ۚ وَٱسْتَغْفِرُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ ۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌۢ | ʾinna rabbaka yaʿlamu ʾannaka taqūmu ʾadnā min thuluthayi layli waniṣfahu wathuluthahu waṭāʾifatun mina ladhīna maʿaka walllahu yuqaddiru layla walnnahāra ʿalima ʾan lan tuḥṣūhu fatāba ʿalaykum faqraʾū mā tayassara mina l-qurʾāni ʿalima ʾan sayakūnu minkum marḍā waʾākharūna yaḍribūna fi l-ʾarḍi yabtaghūna min faḍli llāhi waʾākharūna yuqātilūna fi sabīli llāhi faqraʾū mā tayassara minhu waʾaqīmū ṣ-ṣalawāta waʾātū z-zakawāta waʾaqriḍū llāha qarḍan ḥasanan wamā tuqaddimū liʾanfusikum min khayrin tajidūhu ʿinda llāhi huwa khayran waʾaʿẓama ʾajran wastaghfirū llāha ʾinna llāha ghafūrun raḥīm | Lo! thy Lord knoweth how thou keepest vigil sometimes nearly two-thirds of the night, or (sometimes) half or a third thereof, as do a party of those with thee. Allah measureth the night and the day. He knoweth that ye count it not, and turneth unto you in mercy. Recite, then, of the Qur'an that which is easy for you. He knoweth that there are sick folk among you, while others travel in the land in search of Allah's bounty, and others (still) are fighting for the cause of Allah. So recite of it that which is easy (for you), and establish worship and pay the poor-due, and (so) lend unto Allah a goodly loan. Whatsoever good ye send before you for your souls, ye will find it with Allah, better and greater in the recompense. And seek forgiveness of Allah. Lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful. |
