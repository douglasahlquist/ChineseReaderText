| 93 فَنُزُلٌ مِّنْ حَمِيمٍ | Fanuzulun min ḥamīm | Then the welcome will be boiling water |
| 94 وَتَصْلِيَةُ جَحِيمٍ | Wataṣliyatu jaḥīm | And roasting at hell-fire. |
| 95 إِنَّ هَـٰذَا لَهُوَ حَقُّ ٱلْيَقِينِ | ʾinna hādhā lahuwa ḥaqqu l-yaqīn | Lo! this is certain truth. |
| 96 فَسَبِّحْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلْعَظِيمِ | Fasabbiḥ bismi rabbika l-ʿaẓīm | Therefor (O Muhammad) praise the name of thy Lord, the Tremendous. |
