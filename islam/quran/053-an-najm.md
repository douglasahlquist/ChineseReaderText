| 22 تِلْكَ إِذًا قِسْمَةٌ ضِيزَىٰٓ | Tilka ʾidhan qismatun ḍīzā | That indeed were an unfair division! |
| 23 إِنْ هِىَ إِلَّآ أَسْمَآءٌ سَمَّيْتُمُوهَآ أَنتُمْ وَءَابَآؤُكُم مَّآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَا مِن سُلْطَـٰنٍ ۚ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّ وَمَا تَهْوَى ٱلْأَنفُسُ ۖ وَلَقَدْ جَآءَهُم مِّن رَّبِّهِمُ ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ | ʾin hiya ʾillā ʾasmāʾun sammaytumūhā ʾantum waʾābāʾukum mā ʾanzala llāhu bihā min sulṭānin ʾin yattabiʿūna ʾillā ẓ-ẓanna wamā tahwā l-ʾanfusu walaqad jāʾahum min rabbihimu l-hudā | They are but names which ye have named, ye and your fathers, for which Allah hath revealed no warrant. They follow but a guess and that which (they) themselves desire. And now the guidance from their Lord hath come unto them. |
| 24 أَمْ لِلْإِنسَـٰنِ مَا تَمَنَّىٰ | ʾam lilʾinsāni mā tamannā | Or shall man have what he coveteth? |
| 25 فَلِلَّهِ ٱلْـَٔاخِرَةُ وَٱلْأُولَىٰ | Falillahi lākhiratu walʾūlā | But unto Allah belongeth the after (life), and the former. |
| 26 ۞ وَكَم مِّن مَّلَكٍ فِى ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ لَا تُغْنِى شَفَـٰعَتُهُمْ شَيْـًٔا إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعْدِ أَن يَأْذَنَ ٱللَّهُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَرْضَىٰٓ | Wakam min malakin fi s-samāwāti lā tughni shafāʿatuhum shayan ʾillā min baʿdi ʾan yaʾdhana llāhu liman yashāʾu wayarḍā | And how many angels are in the heavens whose intercession availeth naught save after Allah giveth leave to whom He chooseth and accepteth. |
| 27 إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ بِٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ لَيُسَمُّونَ ٱلْمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ تَسْمِيَةَ ٱلْأُنثَىٰ | ʾinna ladhīna lā yuʾminūna bilākhirati layusammūna l-malāʾikata tasmiyata l-ʾunthā | Lo! it is those who disbelieve in the Hereafter who name the angels with the names of females. |
| 28 وَمَا لَهُم بِهِۦ مِنْ عِلْمٍ ۖ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّ ۖ وَإِنَّ ٱلظَّنَّ لَا يُغْنِى مِنَ ٱلْحَقِّ شَيْـًٔا | Wamā lahum bihi min ʿilmin ʾin yattabiʿūna ʾillā ẓ-ẓanna waʾinna ẓ-ẓanna lā yughni mina l-ḥaqqi shayā | And they have no knowledge thereof. They follow but a guess, and lo! a guess can never take the place of the truth. |
| 29 فَأَعْرِضْ عَن مَّن تَوَلَّىٰ عَن ذِكْرِنَا وَلَمْ يُرِدْ إِلَّا ٱلْحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنْيَا | Faʾaʿriḍ ʿan man tawallā ʿan dhikrinā walam yurid ʾillā l-ḥayawāta d-dunyā | Then withdraw (O Muhammad) from him who fleeth from Our remembrance and desireth but the life of the world. |
| 30 ذَٰلِكَ مَبْلَغُهُم مِّنَ ٱلْعِلْمِ ۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِمَنِ ٱهْتَدَىٰ | Dhālika mablaghuhum mina l-ʿilmi ʾinna rabbaka huwa ʾaʿlamu biman ḍalla ʿan sabīlihi wahuwa ʾaʿlamu bimani htadā | Such is their sum of knowledge. Lo! thy Lord is Best Aware of him who strayeth, and He is Best Aware of him whom goeth right. |

| 31 وَلِلَّهِ مَا فِى ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ لِيَجْزِىَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسَـٰٓـُٔوا۟ بِمَا عَمِلُوا۟ وَيَجْزِىَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَحْسَنُوا۟ بِٱلْحُسْنَى | Walillahi mā fi s-samāwāti wamā fi l-ʾarḍi liyajziya ladhīna ʾasāw bimā ʿamilū wayajziya ladhīna ʾaḥsanū bilḥusnā | And unto Allah belongeth whatsoever is in the heavens and whatsoever is in the earth, that He may reward those who do evil with that which they have done, and reward those who do good with goodness. |
| 32 ٱلَّذِينَ يَجْتَنِبُونَ كَبَـٰٓئِرَ ٱلْإِثْمِ وَٱلْفَوَٰحِشَ إِلَّا ٱللَّمَمَ ۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ وَٰسِعُ ٱلْمَغْفِرَةِ ۚ هُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِكُمْ إِذْ أَنشَأَكُم مِّنَ ٱلْأَرْضِ وَإِذْ أَنتُمْ أَجِنَّةٌ فِى بُطُونِ أُمَّهَـٰتِكُمْ ۖ فَلَا تُزَكُّوٓا۟ أَنفُسَكُمْ ۖ هُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِمَنِ ٱتَّقَىٰٓ | Alladhīna yajtanibūna kabāʾira l-ʾithmi walfawāḥisha ʾillā l-lamama ʾinna rabbaka wāsiʿu l-maghfirati huwa ʾaʿlamu bikum ʾidh ʾanshaʾakum mina l-ʾarḍi waʾidh ʾantum ʾajinnatun fi buṭūni ʾummahātikum falā tuzakkū ʾanfusakum huwa ʾaʿlamu bimani taqā | Those who avoid enormities of sin and abominations, save the unwilled offences - (for them) lo! thy Lord is of vast mercy. He is Best Aware of you (from the time) when He created you from the earth, and when ye were hidden in the bellies of your mothers. Therefor ascribe not purity unto yourselves. He is Best Aware of him who wardeth off (evil). |
| 33 أَفَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى تَوَلَّىٰ | ʾafaraʾayta ladhi tawallā | Didst thou (O Muhammad) observe him who turned away, |
| 34 وَأَعْطَىٰ قَلِيلًا وَأَكْدَىٰٓ | Waʾaʿṭā qalīlan waʾakdā | And gave a little, then was grudging? |
| 35 أَعِندَهُۥ عِلْمُ ٱلْغَيْبِ فَهُوَ يَرَىٰٓ | ʾaʿindahu ʿilmu l-ghaybi fahuwa yarā | Hath he knowledge of the Unseen so that he seeth? |
| 36 أَمْ لَمْ يُنَبَّأْ بِمَا فِى صُحُفِ مُوسَىٰ | ʾam lam yunabbaʾ bimā fi ṣuḥufi mūsā | Or hath he not had news of what is in the books of Moses |
| 37 وَإِبْرَٰهِيمَ ٱلَّذِى وَفَّىٰٓ | Waʾibrāhīma ladhi waffā | And Abraham who paid his debt: |
| 38 أَلَّا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةٌ وِزْرَ أُخْرَىٰ | ʾallā taziru wāziratun wizra ʾukhrā | That no laden one shall bear another's load, |
| 39 وَأَن لَّيْسَ لِلْإِنسَـٰنِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ | Waʾan laysa lilʾinsāni ʾillā mā saʿā | And that man hath only that for which he maketh effort, |
| 40 وَأَنَّ سَعْيَهُۥ سَوْفَ يُرَىٰ | Waʾanna saʿyahu sawfa yurā | And that his effort will be seen. |
