| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 1 إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنفَطَرَتْ | ʾidhā s-samāʾu nfaṭarat | When the heaven is cleft asunder, |
| 2 وَإِذَا ٱلْكَوَاكِبُ ٱنتَثَرَتْ | Waʾidhā l-kawākibu ntatharat | When the planets are dispersed, |
| 3 وَإِذَا ٱلْبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتْ | Waʾidhā l-biḥāru fujjirat | When the seas are poured forth, |
| 4 وَإِذَا ٱلْقُبُورُ بُعْثِرَتْ | Waʾidhā l-qubūru buʿthirat | And the sepulchres are overturned, |
| 5 عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌ مَّا قَدَّمَتْ وَأَخَّرَتْ | ʿalimat nafsun mā qaddamat waʾakhkharat | A soul will know what it hath sent before (it) and what left behind. |
| 6 يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ ٱلْكَرِيمِ | Yāʾayyuhā l-ʾinsānu mā gharraka birabbika l-karīm | O man! What hath made thee careless concerning thy Lord, the Bountiful, |
| 7 ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّىٰكَ فَعَدَلَكَ | Alladhi khalaqaka fasawwāka faʿadalak | Who created thee, then fashioned, then proportioned thee? |
| 8 فِىٓ أَىِّ صُورَةٍ مَّا شَآءَ رَكَّبَكَ | Fi ʾayyi ṣūratin mā shāʾa rakkabak | Into whatsoever form He will, He casteth thee. |
| 9 كَلَّا بَلْ تُكَذِّبُونَ بِٱلدِّينِ | Kallā bal tukadhdhibūna bilddīn | Nay, but ye deny the Judgment. |
| 10 وَإِنَّ عَلَيْكُمْ لَحَـٰفِظِينَ | Waʾinna ʿalaykum laḥāfiẓīn | Lo! there are above you guardians, |

| 15 يَصْلَوْنَهَا يَوْمَ ٱلدِّينِ | Yaṣlawnahā yawma d-dīn | They will burn therein on the Day of Judgment, |
| 16 وَمَا هُمْ عَنْهَا بِغَآئِبِينَ | Wamā hum ʿanhā bighāʾibīn | And will not be absent thence. |
