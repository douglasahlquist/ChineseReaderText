## Verses 1–10

| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 1 لَآ أُقْسِمُ بِهَـٰذَا ٱلْبَلَدِ | Lā ʾuqsimu bihādhā l-balad | Nay, I swear by this city - |
| 2 وَأَنتَ حِلٌّۢ بِهَـٰذَا ٱلْبَلَدِ | Waʾanta ḥillun bihādhā l-balad | And thou art an indweller of this city - |
| 3 وَوَالِدٍ وَمَا وَلَدَ | Wawālidin wamā walad | And the begetter and that which he begat, |
| 4 لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ فِى كَبَدٍ | Laqad khalaqnā l-ʾinsāna fi kabad | We verily have created man in an atmosphere: |
| 5 أَيَحْسَبُ أَن لَّن يَقْدِرَ عَلَيْهِ أَحَدٌ | ʾayaḥsabu ʾan lan yaqdira ʿalayhi ʾaḥad | Thinketh he that none hath power over him? |
| 6 يَقُولُ أَهْلَكْتُ مَالًا لُّبَدًا | Yaqūlu ʾahlaktu mālan lubadā | And he saith: I have destroyed vast wealth: |
| 7 أَيَحْسَبُ أَن لَّمْ يَرَهُۥٓ أَحَدٌ | ʾayaḥsabu ʾan lam yarahu ʾaḥad | Thinketh he that none beholdeth him? |
| 8 أَلَمْ نَجْعَل لَّهُۥ عَيْنَيْنِ | ʾalam najʿal lahu ʿaynayn | Did We not assign unto him two eyes |
| 9 وَلِسَانًا وَشَفَتَيْنِ | Walisānan washafatayn | And a tongue and two lips, |
| 10 وَهَدَيْنَـٰهُ ٱلنَّجْدَيْنِ | Wahadaynāhu n-najdayn | And guide him to the parting of the mountain ways? |
