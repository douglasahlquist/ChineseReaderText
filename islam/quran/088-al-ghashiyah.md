| 1 هَلْ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلْغَـٰشِيَةِ | Hal ʾatāka ḥadīthu l-ghāshiyat | Hath there come unto thee tidings of the Overwhelming? |
| 2 وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ خَـٰشِعَةٌ | Wujūhun yawmaʾidhin khāshiʿat | On that day (many) faces will be downcast, |
| 3 عَامِلَةٌ نَّاصِبَةٌ | ʿāmilatun nāṣibat | Toiling, weary, |
| 4 تَصْلَىٰ نَارًا حَامِيَةً | Taṣlā nāran ḥāmiyatā | Scorched by burning fire, |
| 5 تُسْقَىٰ مِنْ عَيْنٍ ءَانِيَةٍ | Tusqā min ʿaynin ʾāniyat | Drinking from a boiling spring, |
| 6 لَّيْسَ لَهُمْ طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِن ضَرِيعٍ | Laysa lahum ṭaʿāmun ʾillā min ḍarīʿ | No food for them save bitter thorn-fruit |
| 7 لَّا يُسْمِنُ وَلَا يُغْنِى مِن جُوعٍ | Lā yusminu walā yughni min jūʿ | Which doth not nourish nor release from hunger. |
| 8 وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ نَّاعِمَةٌ | Wujūhun yawmaʾidhin nāʿimat | In that day other faces will be calm, |
| 9 لِّسَعْيِهَا رَاضِيَةٌ | Lisaʿyihā rāḍiyat | Glad for their effort past, |
| 10 فِى جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٍ | Fi jannatin ʿāliyat | In a high Garden |
