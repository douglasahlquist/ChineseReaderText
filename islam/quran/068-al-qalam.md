| 3 وَإِنَّ لَكَ لَأَجْرًا غَيْرَ مَمْنُونٍ | Waʾinna laka laʾajran ghayra mamnūn | And lo! thine verily will be a reward unfailing. |
| 4 وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ | Waʾinnaka laʿalā khuluqin ʿaẓīm | And lo! thou art of a tremendous nature. |
| 5 فَسَتُبْصِرُ وَيُبْصِرُونَ | Fasatubṣiru wayubṣirūn | And thou wilt see and they will see |
| 6 بِأَييِّكُمُ ٱلْمَفْتُونُ | Biʾayyyikumu l-maftūn | Which of you is the demented. |
| 7 إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِٱلْمُهْتَدِينَ | ʾinna rabbaka huwa ʾaʿlamu biman ḍalla ʿan sabīlihi wahuwa ʾaʿlamu bilmuhtadīn | Lo! thy Lord is Best Aware of him who strayeth from His way, and He is Best Aware of those who walk aright. |
| 8 فَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلْمُكَذِّبِينَ | Falā tuṭiʿi l-mukadhdhibīn | Therefor obey not thou the rejecters |
| 9 وَدُّوا۟ لَوْ تُدْهِنُ فَيُدْهِنُونَ | Waddū law tudhinu fayudhinūn | Who would have had thee compromise, that they may compromise. |
| 10 وَلَا تُطِعْ كُلَّ حَلَّافٍ مَّهِينٍ | Walā tuṭiʿ kulla ḥallāfin mahīn | Neither obey thou each feeble oath-monger, |

| 12 مَّنَّاعٍ لِّلْخَيْرِ مُعْتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ | Mannāʿin lilkhayri muʿtadin ʾathīm | Hinderer of the good, transgressor, malefactor |
| 13 عُتُلٍّۭ بَعْدَ ذَٰلِكَ زَنِيمٍ | ʿutullin baʿda dhālika zanīm | Greedy therewithal, intrusive. |
| 14 أَن كَانَ ذَا مَالٍ وَبَنِينَ | ʾan kāna dhā mālin wabanīn | It is because he is possessed of wealth and children |
| 15 إِذَا تُتْلَىٰ عَلَيْهِ ءَايَـٰتُنَا قَالَ أَسَـٰطِيرُ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ | ʾidhā tutlā ʿalayhi ʾāyātunā qāla ʾasāṭīru l-ʾawwalīn | That, when Our revelations are recited unto him, he saith: Mere fables of the men of old. |
| 16 سَنَسِمُهُۥ عَلَى ٱلْخُرْطُومِ | Sanasimuhu ʿalā l-khurṭūm | We shall brand him on the nose. |
| 17 إِنَّا بَلَوْنَـٰهُمْ كَمَا بَلَوْنَآ أَصْحَـٰبَ ٱلْجَنَّةِ إِذْ أَقْسَمُوا۟ لَيَصْرِمُنَّهَا مُصْبِحِينَ | ʾinnā balawnāhum kamā balawnā ʾaṣḥāba l-jannati ʾidh ʾaqsamū layaṣrimunnahā muṣbiḥīn | Lo! We have tried them as We tried the owners of the garden when they vowed that they would pluck its fruit next morning, |
| 18 وَلَا يَسْتَثْنُونَ | Walā yastathnūn | And made no exception (for the Will of Allah); |
| 19 فَطَافَ عَلَيْهَا طَآئِفٌ مِّن رَّبِّكَ وَهُمْ نَآئِمُونَ | Faṭāfa ʿalayhā ṭāʾifun min rabbika wahum nāʾimūn | Then a visitation from thy Lord came upon it while they slept |
| 20 فَأَصْبَحَتْ كَٱلصَّرِيمِ | Faʾaṣbaḥat kalṣṣarīm | And in the morning it was as if plucked. |

| 33 كَذَٰلِكَ ٱلْعَذَابُ ۖ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ أَكْبَرُ ۚ لَوْ كَانُوا۟ يَعْلَمُونَ | Kadhālika l-ʿadhābu walaʿadhābu lākhirati ʾakbaru law kānū yaʿlamūn | Such was the punishment. And verily the punishment of the Hereafter is greater if they did but know. |

| 43 خَـٰشِعَةً أَبْصَـٰرُهُمْ تَرْهَقُهُمْ ذِلَّةٌ ۖ وَقَدْ كَانُوا۟ يُدْعَوْنَ إِلَى ٱلسُّجُودِ وَهُمْ سَـٰلِمُونَ | Khāshiʿatan ʾabṣāruhum tarhaquhum dhillatun waqad kānū yudʿawna ʾilā s-sujūdi wahum sālimūn | With eyes downcast, abasement stupefying them. | 44 فَذَرْنِى وَمَن يُكَذِّبُ بِهَـٰذَا ٱلْحَدِيثِ ۖ سَنَسْتَدْرِجُهُم مِّنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ | Fadharni waman yukadhdhibu bihādhā l-ḥadīthi sanastadrijuhum min ḥaythu lā yaʿlamūn | Leave Me (to deal) with those who give the lie to this pronouncement. | 45 وَأُمْلِى لَهُمْ ۚ إِنَّ كَيْدِى مَتِينٌ | Waʾumli lahum ʾinna kaydi matīn | Yet I bear with them, for lo! My scheme is firm. |
| 46 أَمْ تَسْـَٔلُهُمْ أَجْرًا فَهُم مِّن مَّغْرَمٍ مُّثْقَلُونَ | ʾam tasaluhum ʾajran fahum min maghramin muthqalūn | Or dost thou (Muhammad) ask a fee from them so that they are heavily taxed? |
| 47 أَمْ عِندَهُمُ ٱلْغَيْبُ فَهُمْ يَكْتُبُونَ | ʾam ʿindahumu l-ghaybu fahum yaktubūn | Or is the Unseen theirs that they can write (thereof)? |
| 48 فَٱصْبِرْ لِحُكْمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تَكُن كَصَاحِبِ ٱلْحُوتِ إِذْ نَادَىٰ وَهُوَ مَكْظُومٌ | Faṣbir liḥukmi rabbika walā takun kaṣāḥibi l-ḥūti ʾidh nādā wahuwa makẓūm | But wait thou for thy Lord's decree, and be not like him of the fish, who cried out in despair. |
| 49 لَّوْلَآ أَن تَدَٰرَكَهُۥ نِعْمَةٌ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ لَنُبِذَ بِٱلْعَرَآءِ وَهُوَ مَذْمُومٌ | Lawlā ʾan tadārakahu niʿmatun min rabbihi lanubidha bilʿarāʾi wahuwa madhmūm | Had it not been that favour from his Lord had reached him he surely had been cast into the wilderness while he was reprobate. |
| 50 فَٱجْتَبَـٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَجَعَلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ | Fajtabāhu rabbuhu fajaʿalahu mina ṣ-ṣāliḥīn | But his Lord chose him and placed him among the righteous. |
