| 6 وَكَمْ أَرْسَلْنَا مِن نَّبِىٍّ فِى ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ | Wakam ʾarsalnā min nabiyyin fi l-ʾawwalīn | How many a prophet did We send among the men of old! |
| 7 وَمَا يَأْتِيهِم مِّن نَّبِىٍّ إِلَّا كَانُوا۟ بِهِۦ يَسْتَهْزِءُونَ | Wamā yaʾtīhim min nabiyyin ʾillā kānū bihi yastahziʾūn | And never came there unto them a prophet but they used to mock him. |
| 8 فَأَهْلَكْنَآ أَشَدَّ مِنْهُم بَطْشًا وَمَضَىٰ مَثَلُ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ | Faʾahlaknā ʾashadda minhum baṭshan wamaḍā mathalu l-ʾawwalīn | Then We destroyed men mightier than these in prowess; and the example of the men of old hath gone (before them). |
| 9 وَلَئِن سَأَلْتَهُم مَّنْ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ لَيَقُولُنَّ خَلَقَهُنَّ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْعَلِيمُ | Walaʾin saʾaltahum man khalaqa s-samāwāti walʾarḍa layaqūlunna khalaqahunna l-ʿazīzu l-ʿalīm | And if thou (Muhammad) ask them: Who created the heavens and the earth, they will surely answer: The Mighty, the Knower created them; |
| 10 ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلْأَرْضَ مَهْدًا وَجَعَلَ لَكُمْ فِيهَا سُبُلًا لَّعَلَّكُمْ تَهْتَدُونَ | Alladhi jaʿala lakumu l-ʾarḍa mahdan wajaʿala lakum fīhā subulan laʿallakum tahtadūn | Who made the earth a resting-place for you, and placed roads for you therein, that haply ye may find your way; |

| 13 لِتَسْتَوُۥا۟ عَلَىٰ ظُهُورِهِۦ ثُمَّ تَذْكُرُوا۟ نِعْمَةَ رَبِّكُمْ إِذَا ٱسْتَوَيْتُمْ عَلَيْهِ وَتَقُولُوا۟ سُبْحَـٰنَ ٱلَّذِى سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَـٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُۥ مُقْرِنِينَ | Litastawu ʿalā ẓuhūrihi thumma tadhkurū niʿmata rabbikum ʾidhā stawaytum ʿalayhi wataqūlū subḥāna ladhi sakhkhara lanā hādhā wamā kunnā lahu muqrinīn | That ye may mount upon their backs, and may remember your Lord's favour when ye mount thereon, and may say: Glorified be He Who hath subdued these unto us, and we were not capable (of subduing them); |
| 14 وَإِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ | Waʾinnā ʾilā rabbinā lamunqalibūn | And lo! unto our Lord we surely are returning. |
| 15 وَجَعَلُوا۟ لَهُۥ مِنْ عِبَادِهِۦ جُزْءًا ۚ إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ لَكَفُورٌ مُّبِينٌ | Wajaʿalū lahu min ʿibādihi juzʾan ʾinna l-ʾinsāna lakafūrun mubīn | And they allot to Him a portion of His bondmen! Lo! man is verily a mere ingrate. |
| 16 أَمِ ٱتَّخَذَ مِمَّا يَخْلُقُ بَنَاتٍ وَأَصْفَىٰكُم بِٱلْبَنِينَ | ʾami takhadha mimmā yakhluqu banātin waʾaṣfākum bilbanīn | Or chooseth He daughters of all that He hath created, and honoureth He you with sons? |
| 17 وَإِذَا بُشِّرَ أَحَدُهُم بِمَا ضَرَبَ لِلرَّحْمَـٰنِ مَثَلًا ظَلَّ وَجْهُهُۥ مُسْوَدًّا وَهُوَ كَظِيمٌ | Waʾidhā bushshira ʾaḥaduhum bimā ḍaraba lilrraḥmāni mathalan ẓalla wajhuhu muswaddan wahuwa kaẓīm | And if one of them hath tidings of that which he likeneth to the Beneficent One, his countenance becometh black and he is full of inward rage. |
| 18 أَوَمَن يُنَشَّؤُا۟ فِى ٱلْحِلْيَةِ وَهُوَ فِى ٱلْخِصَامِ غَيْرُ مُبِينٍ | ʾawaman yunashshaʾu fi l-ḥilyati wahuwa fi l-khiṣāmi ghayru mubīn | (Liken they then to Allah) that which is bred up in outward show, and in dispute cannot make itself plain? |
| 19 وَجَعَلُوا۟ ٱلْمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ عِبَـٰدُ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ إِنَـٰثًا ۚ أَشَهِدُوا۟ خَلْقَهُمْ ۚ سَتُكْتَبُ شَهَـٰدَتُهُمْ وَيُسْـَٔلُونَ | Wajaʿalū l-malāʾikata ladhīna hum ʿibādu r-raḥmāni ʾināthan ʾashahidū khalqahum satuktabu shahādatuhum wayusalūn | And they make the angels, who are the slaves of the Beneficent, females. Did they witness their creation? Their testimony will be recorded and they will be questioned. |
| 20 وَقَالُوا۟ لَوْ شَآءَ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنُ مَا عَبَدْنَـٰهُم ۗ مَّا لَهُم بِذَٰلِكَ مِنْ عِلْمٍ ۖ إِنْ هُمْ إِلَّا يَخْرُصُونَ | Waqālū law shāʾa r-raḥmānu mā ʿabadnāhum mā lahum bidhālika min ʿilmin ʾin hum ʾillā yakhruṣūn | And they say: If the Beneficent One had (so) willed, we should not have worshipped them. They have no knowledge whatsoever of that. They do but guess. |

| 23 وَكَذَٰلِكَ مَآ أَرْسَلْنَا مِن قَبْلِكَ فِى قَرْيَةٍ مِّن نَّذِيرٍ إِلَّا قَالَ مُتْرَفُوهَآ إِنَّا وَجَدْنَآ ءَابَآءَنَا عَلَىٰٓ أُمَّةٍ وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَـٰرِهِم مُّقْتَدُونَ | Wakadhālika mā ʾarsalnā min qablika fi qaryatin min nadhīrin ʾillā qāla mutrafūhā ʾinnā wajadnā ʾābāʾanā ʿalā ʾummatin waʾinnā ʿalā ʾāthārihim muqtadūn | And even so We sent not a warner before thee (Muhammad) into any township but its luxurious ones said: Lo! we found our fathers following a religion, and we are following their footprints. |
| 24 ۞ قَـٰلَ أَوَلَوْ جِئْتُكُم بِأَهْدَىٰ مِمَّا وَجَدتُّمْ عَلَيْهِ ءَابَآءَكُمْ ۖ قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّا بِمَآ أُرْسِلْتُم بِهِۦ كَـٰفِرُونَ | Qāla ʾawalaw jiʾtukum biʾahdā mimmā wajadttum ʿalayhi ʾābāʾakum qālū ʾinnā bimā ʾursiltum bihi kāfirūn | (And the warner) said: What! Even though I bring you better guidance than that ye found your fathers following? They answered: Lo! in what ye bring we are disbelievers. |
| 25 فَٱنتَقَمْنَا مِنْهُمْ ۖ فَٱنظُرْ كَيْفَ كَانَ عَـٰقِبَةُ ٱلْمُكَذِّبِينَ | Fantaqamnā minhum fanẓur kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu l-mukadhdhibīn | So We requited them. Then see the nature of the consequence for the rejecters! |
| 26 وَإِذْ قَالَ إِبْرَٰهِيمُ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوْمِهِۦٓ إِنَّنِى بَرَآءٌ مِّمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ | Waʾidh qāla ʾibrāhīmu liʾabīhi waqawmihi ʾinnani barāʾun mimmā taʿbudūn | And when Abraham said unto his father and his folk: Lo! I am innocent of what ye worship |
| 27 إِلَّا ٱلَّذِى فَطَرَنِى فَإِنَّهُۥ سَيَهْدِينِ | ʾillā ladhi faṭarani faʾinnahu sayahdīn | Save Him Who did create me, for He will surely guide me. |
| 28 وَجَعَلَهَا كَلِمَةًۢ بَاقِيَةً فِى عَقِبِهِۦ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَرْجِعُونَ | Wajaʿalahā kalimatan bāqiyatan fi ʿaqibihi laʿallahum yarjiʿūn | And he made it a word enduring among his seed, that haply they might return. |
| 29 بَلْ مَتَّعْتُ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ وَءَابَآءَهُمْ حَتَّىٰ جَآءَهُمُ ٱلْحَقُّ وَرَسُولٌ مُّبِينٌ | Bal mattaʿtu hāʾulāʾi waʾābāʾahum ḥattā jāʾahumu l-ḥaqqu warasūlun mubīn | Nay, but I let these and their fathers enjoy life (only) till there should come unto them the Truth and a messenger making plain. |
| 30 وَلَمَّا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلْحَقُّ قَالُوا۟ هَـٰذَا سِحْرٌ وَإِنَّا بِهِۦ كَـٰفِرُونَ | Walammā jāʾahumu l-ḥaqqu qālū hādhā siḥrun waʾinnā bihi kāfirūn | And now that the Truth hath come unto them they say: This is mere magic, and lo! we are disbelievers therein. |

| 87 وَلَئِن سَأَلْتَهُم مَّنْ خَلَقَهُمْ لَيَقُولُنَّ ٱللَّهُ ۖ فَأَنَّىٰ يُؤْفَكُونَ | Walaʾin saʾaltahum man khalaqahum layaqūlunna llāhu faʾannā yuʾfakūn | And if thou ask them who created them, they will surely say: Allah. How then are they turned away? |
| 88 وَقِيلِهِۦ يَـٰرَبِّ إِنَّ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ قَوْمٌ لَّا يُؤْمِنُونَ | Waqīlihi yārabbi ʾinna hāʾulāʾi qawmun lā yuʾminūn | And he saith: O my Lord! Lo! these are a folk who believe not. |
| 89 فَٱصْفَحْ عَنْهُمْ وَقُلْ سَلَـٰمٌ ۚ فَسَوْفَ يَعْلَمُونَ | Faṣfaḥ ʿanhum waqul salāmun fasawfa yaʿlamūn | Then bear with them (O Muhammad) and say: Peace. But they will come to know. |
