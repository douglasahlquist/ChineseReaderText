| 12 إِنَّا نَحْنُ نُحْىِ ٱلْمَوْتَىٰ وَنَكْتُبُ مَا قَدَّمُوا۟ وَءَاثَـٰرَهُمْ ۚ وَكُلَّ شَىْءٍ أَحْصَيْنَـٰهُ فِىٓ إِمَامٍ مُّبِينٍ | ʾinnā naḥnu nuḥyi l-mawtā wanaktubu mā qaddamū waʾāthārahum wakulla shāʾin ʾaḥṣaynāhu fi ʾimāmin mubīn | Lo! We it is Who bring the dead to life. We record that which they send before (them, and their footprints. And all things We have kept in a clear Register. |
| 13 وَٱضْرِبْ لَهُم مَّثَلًا أَصْحَـٰبَ ٱلْقَرْيَةِ إِذْ جَآءَهَا ٱلْمُرْسَلُونَ | Waḍrib lahum mathalan ʾaṣḥāba l-qaryati ʾidh jāʾahā l-mursalūn | Coin for them a similitude: The people of the city when those sent (from Allah) came unto them; |
| 14 إِذْ أَرْسَلْنَآ إِلَيْهِمُ ٱثْنَيْنِ فَكَذَّبُوهُمَا فَعَزَّزْنَا بِثَالِثٍ فَقَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّآ إِلَيْكُم مُّرْسَلُونَ | ʾidh ʾarsalnā ʾilayhimu thnayni fakadhdhabūhumā faʿazzaznā bithālithin faqālū ʾinnā ʾilaykum mursalūn | When We sent unto them twain, and they denied them both, so We reinforced them with a third, and they said: Lo! we have been sent unto you. |
| 15 قَالُوا۟ مَآ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا بَشَرٌ مِّثْلُنَا وَمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنُ مِن شَىْءٍ إِنْ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا تَكْذِبُونَ | Qālū mā ʾantum ʾillā basharun mithlunā wamā ʾanzala r-raḥmānu min shāʾin ʾin ʾantum ʾillā takdhibūn | They said: Ye are but mortals like unto us. The Beneficent hath naught revealed. Ye do but lie! |
| 16 قَالُوا۟ رَبُّنَا يَعْلَمُ إِنَّآ إِلَيْكُمْ لَمُرْسَلُونَ | Qālū rabbunā yaʿlamu ʾinnā ʾilaykum lamursalūn | They answered: Our Lord knoweth that we are indeed sent unto you, |
| 17 وَمَا عَلَيْنَآ إِلَّا ٱلْبَلَـٰغُ ٱلْمُبِينُ | Wamā ʿalaynā ʾillā l-balāghu l-mubīn | And our duty is but plain conveyance (of the message). |
| 18 قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّا تَطَيَّرْنَا بِكُمْ ۖ لَئِن لَّمْ تَنتَهُوا۟ لَنَرْجُمَنَّكُمْ وَلَيَمَسَّنَّكُم مِّنَّا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ | Qālū ʾinnā taṭayyarnā bikum laʾin lam tantahū lanarjumannakum walayamassannakum minnā ʿadhābun ʾalīm | (The people of the city) said: We augur ill of you. If ye desist not, we shall surely stone you, and grievous torture will befall you at our hands. |
| 19 قَالُوا۟ طَـٰٓئِرُكُم مَّعَكُمْ ۚ أَئِن ذُكِّرْتُم ۚ بَلْ أَنتُمْ قَوْمٌ مُّسْرِفُونَ | Qālū ṭāʾirukum maʿakum ʾaʾin dhukkirtum bal ʾantum qawmun musrifūn | They said: Your evil augury be with you! Is it because ye are reminded (of the truth)? Nay, but ye are froward folk! |
| 20 وَجَآءَ مِنْ أَقْصَا ٱلْمَدِينَةِ رَجُلٌ يَسْعَىٰ قَالَ يَـٰقَوْمِ ٱتَّبِعُوا۟ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ | Wajāʾa min ʾaqṣā l-madīnati rajulun yasʿā qāla yāqawmi tabiʿū l-mursalīn | And there came from the uttermost part of the city a man running. He cried: O my people! Follow those who have been sent! |

| 33 وَءَايَةٌ لَّهُمُ ٱلْأَرْضُ ٱلْمَيْتَةُ أَحْيَيْنَـٰهَا وَأَخْرَجْنَا مِنْهَا حَبًّا فَمِنْهُ يَأْكُلُونَ | Waʾāyatun lahumu l-ʾarḍu l-maytatu ʾaḥyaynāhā waʾakhrajnā minhā ḥabban faminhu yaʾkulūn | A token unto them is the dead earth. | 34 وَجَعَلْنَا فِيهَا جَنَّـٰتٍ مِّن نَّخِيلٍ وَأَعْنَـٰبٍ وَفَجَّرْنَا فِيهَا مِنَ ٱلْعُيُونِ | Wajaʿalnā fīhā jannātin min nakhīlin waʾaʿnābin wafajjarnā fīhā mina l-ʿuyūn | And We have placed therein gardens of the date-palm and grapes, and We have caused springs of water to gush forth therein, |
| 35 لِيَأْكُلُوا۟ مِن ثَمَرِهِۦ وَمَا عَمِلَتْهُ أَيْدِيهِمْ ۖ أَفَلَا يَشْكُرُونَ | Liyaʾkulū min thamarihi wamā ʿamilathu ʾaydīhim ʾafalā yashkurūn | That they may eat of the fruit thereof, and their hands made it not. Will they not, then, give thanks? |
| 36 سُبْحَـٰنَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلْأَزْوَٰجَ كُلَّهَا مِمَّا تُنۢبِتُ ٱلْأَرْضُ وَمِنْ أَنفُسِهِمْ وَمِمَّا لَا يَعْلَمُونَ | Subḥāna ladhi khalaqa l-ʾazwāja kullahā mimmā tunbitu l-ʾarḍu wamin ʾanfusihim wamimmā lā yaʿlamūn | Glory be to Him Who created all the sexual pairs, of that which the earth groweth, and of themselves, and of that which they know not! |
| 37 وَءَايَةٌ لَّهُمُ ٱلَّيْلُ نَسْلَخُ مِنْهُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فَإِذَا هُم مُّظْلِمُونَ | Waʾāyatun lahumu laylu naslakhu minhu n-nahāra faʾidhā hum muẓlimūn | A token unto them is night. We strip it of the day, and lo! they are in darkness. |

| 39 وَٱلْقَمَرَ قَدَّرْنَـٰهُ مَنَازِلَ حَتَّىٰ عَادَ كَٱلْعُرْجُونِ ٱلْقَدِيمِ | Walqamara qaddarnāhu manāzila ḥattā ʿāda kalʿurjūni l-qadīm | And for the moon We have appointed mansions till she return like an old shrivelled palm-leaf. |
| 40 لَا ٱلشَّمْسُ يَنۢبَغِى لَهَآ أَن تُدْرِكَ ٱلْقَمَرَ وَلَا ٱلَّيْلُ سَابِقُ ٱلنَّهَارِ ۚ وَكُلٌّ فِى فَلَكٍ يَسْبَحُونَ | Lā sh-shamsu yanbaghi lahā ʾan tudrika l-qamara walā laylu sābiqu n-nahāri wakullun fi falakin yasbaḥūn | It is not for the sun to overtake the moon, nor doth the night outstrip the day. They float each in an orbit. |
