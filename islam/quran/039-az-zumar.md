| 1 تَنزِيلُ ٱلْكِتَـٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ | Tanzīlu l-kitābi mina llāhi l-ʿazīzi l-ḥakīm | The revelation of the Scripture is from Allah, the Mighty, the Wise. |
| 2 إِنَّآ أَنزَلْنَآ إِلَيْكَ ٱلْكِتَـٰبَ بِٱلْحَقِّ فَٱعْبُدِ ٱللَّهَ مُخْلِصًا لَّهُ ٱلدِّينَ | ʾinnā ʾanzalnā ʾilayka l-kitāba bilḥaqqi faʿbudi llāha mukhliṣan lahu d-dīn | Lo! We have revealed the Scripture unto thee (Muhammad) with truth; so worship Allah, making religion pure for Him (only). |
| 3 أَلَا لِلَّهِ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلْخَالِصُ ۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُوا۟ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوْلِيَآءَ مَا نَعْبُدُهُمْ إِلَّا لِيُقَرِّبُونَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ زُلْفَىٰٓ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَحْكُمُ بَيْنَهُمْ فِى مَا هُمْ فِيهِ يَخْتَلِفُونَ ۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهْدِى مَنْ هُوَ كَـٰذِبٌ كَفَّارٌ | ʾalā lillāhi d-dīnu l-khāliṣu walladhīna takhadhū min dūnihi ʾawliyāʾa mā naʿbuduhum ʾillā liyuqarribūnā ʾilā llāhi zulfā ʾinna llāha yaḥkumu baynahum fi mā hum fīhi yakhtalifūna ʾinna llāha lā yahdi man huwa kādhibun kaffār | Surely pure religion is for Allah only. And those who choose protecting friends beside Him (say): We worship them only that they may bring us near unto Allah. Lo! Allah will judge between them concerning that wherein they differ. Lo! Allah guideth not him who is a liar, an ingrate. |
| 4 لَّوْ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ أَن يَتَّخِذَ وَلَدًا لَّٱصْطَفَىٰ مِمَّا يَخْلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُ ۚ سُبْحَـٰنَهُۥ ۖ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلْوَٰحِدُ ٱلْقَهَّارُ | Law ʾarāda llāhu ʾan yattakhidha waladan laṣṭafā mimmā yakhluqu mā yashāʾu subḥānahu huwa llāhu l-wāḥidu l-qahhār | If Allah had willed to choose a son, He could have chosen what He would of that which He hath created. Be He Glorified! He is Allah, the One, the Absolute. |
| 5 خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ بِٱلْحَقِّ ۖ يُكَوِّرُ ٱلَّيْلَ عَلَى ٱلنَّهَارِ وَيُكَوِّرُ ٱلنَّهَارَ عَلَى ٱلَّيْلِ ۖ وَسَخَّرَ ٱلشَّمْسَ وَٱلْقَمَرَ ۖ كُلٌّ يَجْرِى لِأَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى ۗ أَلَا هُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْغَفَّـٰرُ | Khalaqa s-samāwāti walʾarḍa bilḥaqqi yukawwiru layla ʿalā n-nahāri wayukawwiru n-nahāra ʿalā layli wasakhkhara sh-shamsa walqamara kullun yajri liʾajalin musamman ʾalā huwa l-ʿazīzu l-ghaffār | He hath created the heavens and the earth with truth. He maketh night to succeed day, and He maketh day to succeed night, and He constraineth the sun and the moon to give service, each running on for an appointed term. Is not He the Mighty, the Forgiver? |
| 6 خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفْسٍ وَٰحِدَةٍ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنْهَا زَوْجَهَا وَأَنزَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلْأَنْعَـٰمِ ثَمَـٰنِيَةَ أَزْوَٰجٍ ۚ يَخْلُقُكُمْ فِى بُطُونِ أُمَّهَـٰتِكُمْ خَلْقًا مِّنۢ بَعْدِ خَلْقٍ فِى ظُلُمَـٰتٍ ثَلَـٰثٍ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمْ لَهُ ٱلْمُلْكُ ۖ لَآ إِلَـٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُصْرَفُونَ | Khalaqakum min nafsin wāḥidatin thumma jaʿala minhā zawjahā waʾanzala lakum mina l-ʾanʿāmi thamāniyata ʾazwājin yakhluqukum fi buṭūni ʾummahātikum khalqan min baʿdi khalqin fi ẓulumātin thalāthin dhālikumu llāhu rabbukum lahu l-mulku lā ʾilāha ʾillā huwa faʾannā tuṣrafūn | He created you from one being, then from that (being) He made its mate; and He hath provided for you of cattle eight kinds. He created you in the wombs of your mothers, creation after creation, in a threefold gloom. Such is Allah, your Lord. His is the Sovereignty. There is no Allah save Him. How then are ye turned away? |
| 7 إِن تَكْفُرُوا۟ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِىٌّ عَنكُمْ ۖ وَلَا يَرْضَىٰ لِعِبَادِهِ ٱلْكُفْرَ ۖ وَإِن تَشْكُرُوا۟ يَرْضَهُ لَكُمْ ۗ وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةٌ وِزْرَ أُخْرَىٰ ۗ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُم مَّرْجِعُكُمْ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمٌۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ | ʾin takfurū faʾinna llāha ghaniyyun ʿankum walā yarḍā liʿibādihi l-kufra waʾin tashkurū yarḍahu lakum walā taziru wāziratun wizra ʾukhrā thumma ʾilā rabbikum marjiʿukum fayunabbiʾukum bimā kuntum taʿmalūna ʾinnahu ʿalīmun bidhāti ṣ-ṣudūr | If ye are thankless, yet Allah is Independent of you, though He is not pleased with thanklessness for His bondmen; and if ye are thankful He is pleased therewith for you. No laden soul will bear another's load. Then unto your Lord is your return; and He will tell you what ye used to do. Lo! He knoweth what is in the breasts (of men). |
| 8 ۞ وَإِذَا مَسَّ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ ضُرٌّ دَعَا رَبَّهُۥ مُنِيبًا إِلَيْهِ ثُمَّ إِذَا خَوَّلَهُۥ نِعْمَةً مِّنْهُ نَسِىَ مَا كَانَ يَدْعُوٓا۟ إِلَيْهِ مِن قَبْلُ وَجَعَلَ لِلَّهِ أَندَادًا لِّيُضِلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ ۚ قُلْ تَمَتَّعْ بِكُفْرِكَ قَلِيلًا ۖ إِنَّكَ مِنْ أَصْحَـٰبِ ٱلنَّارِ | Waʾidhā massa l-ʾinsāna ḍurrun daʿā rabbahu munīban ʾilayhi thumma ʾidhā khawwalahu niʿmatan minhu nasiya mā kāna yadʿū ʾilayhi min qablu wajaʿala lillāhi ʾandādan liyuḍilla ʿan sabīlihi qul tamattaʿ bikufrika qalīlan ʾinnaka min ʾaṣḥābi n-nār | And when some hurt toucheth man, he crieth unto his Lord, turning unto Him (repentant). Then, when He granteth him a boon from Him he forgetteth that for which he cried unto Him before, and setteth up rivals to Allah that he may beguile (men) from his way. Say (O Muhammad, unto such an one): Take pleasure in thy disbelief a while. Lo! thou art of the owners of the Fire. |
| 9 أَمَّنْ هُوَ قَـٰنِتٌ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيْلِ سَاجِدًا وَقَآئِمًا يَحْذَرُ ٱلْـَٔاخِرَةَ وَيَرْجُوا۟ رَحْمَةَ رَبِّهِۦ ۗ قُلْ هَلْ يَسْتَوِى ٱلَّذِينَ يَعْلَمُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ ۗ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُو۟لُوا۟ ٱلْأَلْبَـٰبِ | ʾamman huwa qānitun ʾānāʾa layli sājidan waqāʾiman yaḥdharu lākhirata wayarjū raḥmata rabbihi qul hal yastawi ladhīna yaʿlamūna walladhīna lā yaʿlamūna ʾinnamā yatadhakkaru ʾūlū l-ʾalbāb | Is he who payeth adoration in the watches of the night, prostrate and standing, bewaring of the Hereafter and hoping for the mercy of his Lord, (to be accounted equal with a disbeliever)? Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Are those who know equal with those who know not? But only men of understanding will pay heed. |
| 10 قُلْ يَـٰعِبَادِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ ٱتَّقُوا۟ رَبَّكُمْ ۚ لِلَّذِينَ أَحْسَنُوا۟ فِى هَـٰذِهِ ٱلدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةٌ ۗ وَأَرْضُ ٱللَّهِ وَٰسِعَةٌ ۗ إِنَّمَا يُوَفَّى ٱلصَّـٰبِرُونَ أَجْرَهُم بِغَيْرِ حِسَابٍ | Qul yāʿibādi ladhīna ʾāmanū taqū rabbakum lilladhīna ʾaḥsanū fi hādhihi d-dunyā ḥasanatun waʾarḍu llāhi wāsiʿatun ʾinnamā yuwaffā ṣ-ṣābirūna ʾajrahum bighayri ḥisāb | Say: O My bondmen who believe! Observe your duty to your Lord. For those who do good in this world there is good, and Allah's earth is spacious. Verily the steadfast will be paid their wages without stint. |

| 13 قُلْ إِنِّىٓ أَخَافُ إِنْ عَصَيْتُ رَبِّى عَذَابَ يَوْمٍ عَظِيمٍ | Qul ʾinni ʾakhāfu ʾin ʿaṣaytu rabbi ʿadhāba yawmin ʿaẓīm | Say: Lo! if I should disobey my Lord, I fear the doom of a tremendous Day. |

| 15 فَٱعْبُدُوا۟ مَا شِئْتُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ ۗ قُلْ إِنَّ ٱلْخَـٰسِرِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓا۟ أَنفُسَهُمْ وَأَهْلِيهِمْ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَـٰمَةِ ۗ أَلَا ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلْخُسْرَانُ ٱلْمُبِينُ | Faʿbudū mā shiʾtum min dūnihi qul ʾinna l-khāsirīna ladhīna khasirū ʾanfusahum waʾahlīhim yawma l-qiyāmati ʾalā dhālika huwa l-khusrānu l-mubīn | Then worship what ye will beside Him. Say: The losers will be those who lose themselves and their housefolk on the Day of Resurrection. Ah, that will be the manifest loss! |
| 16 لَهُم مِّن فَوْقِهِمْ ظُلَلٌ مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِ وَمِن تَحْتِهِمْ ظُلَلٌ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ يُخَوِّفُ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦ عِبَادَهُۥ ۚ يَـٰعِبَادِ فَٱتَّقُونِ | Lahum min fawqihim ẓulalun mina n-nāri wamin taḥtihim ẓulalun dhālika yukhawwifu llāhu bihi ʿibādahu yāʿibādi fattaqūn | They have an awning of fire above them and beneath them a dais (of fire). With this doth Allah appal His bondmen. O My bondmen, therefor fear Me! |
| 17 وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱجْتَنَبُوا۟ ٱلطَّـٰغُوتَ أَن يَعْبُدُوهَا وَأَنَابُوٓا۟ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ لَهُمُ ٱلْبُشْرَىٰ ۚ فَبَشِّرْ عِبَادِ | Walladhīna jtanabū ṭ-ṭāghūta ʾan yaʿbudūhā waʾanābū ʾilā llāhi lahumu l-bushrā fabashshir ʿibād | And those who put away false gods lest they should worship them and turn to Allah in repentance, for them there are glad tidings. Therefor give good tidings (O Muhammad) to My bondmen |
| 18 ٱلَّذِينَ يَسْتَمِعُونَ ٱلْقَوْلَ فَيَتَّبِعُونَ أَحْسَنَهُۥٓ ۚ أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَدَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ ۖ وَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ هُمْ أُو۟لُوا۟ ٱلْأَلْبَـٰبِ | Alladhīna yastamiʿūna l-qawla fayattabiʿūna ʾaḥsanahu ʾūlāʾika ladhīna hadāhumu llāhu waʾūlāʾika hum ʾūlū l-ʾalbāb | Who hear advice and follow the best thereof. Such are those whom Allah guideth, and such are men of understanding. |
| 19 أَفَمَنْ حَقَّ عَلَيْهِ كَلِمَةُ ٱلْعَذَابِ أَفَأَنتَ تُنقِذُ مَن فِى ٱلنَّارِ | ʾafaman ḥaqqa ʿalayhi kalimatu l-ʿadhābi ʾafaʾanta tunqidhu man fi n-nār | Is he on whom the word of doom is fulfilled (to be helped), and canst thou (O Muhammad) rescue him who is in the Fire? |
| 20 لَـٰكِنِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوْا۟ رَبَّهُمْ لَهُمْ غُرَفٌ مِّن فَوْقِهَا غُرَفٌ مَّبْنِيَّةٌ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَـٰرُ ۖ وَعْدَ ٱللَّهِ ۖ لَا يُخْلِفُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلْمِيعَادَ | Lākini ladhīna taqaw rabbahum lahum ghurafun min fawqihā ghurafun mabniyyatun tajri min taḥtihā l-ʾanhāru waʿda llāhi lā yukhlifu llāhu l-mīʿād | But those who keep their duty to their Lord, for them are lofty halls with lofty halls above them, built (for them), beneath which rivers flow. (It is) a promise of Allah. Allah faileth not His promise. |

| 21 أَلَمْ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءً فَسَلَكَهُۥ يَنَـٰبِيعَ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ ثُمَّ يُخْرِجُ بِهِۦ زَرْعًا مُّخْتَلِفًا أَلْوَٰنُهُۥ ثُمَّ يَهِيجُ فَتَرَىٰهُ مُصْفَرًّا ثُمَّ يَجْعَلُهُۥ حُطَـٰمًا ۚ إِنَّ فِى ذَٰلِكَ لَذِكْرَىٰ لِأُو۟لِى ٱلْأَلْبَـٰبِ | ʾalam tara ʾanna llāha ʾanzala mina s-samāʾi māʾan fasalakahu yanābīʿa fi l-ʾarḍi thumma yukhriju bihi zarʿan mukhtalifan ʾalwānuhu thumma yahīju fatarāhu muṣfarran thumma yajʿaluhu ḥuṭāman ʾinna fi dhālika ladhikrā liʾūli l-ʾalbāb | Hast thou not seen how Allah hath sent down water from the sky and hath caused it to penetrate the earth as watersprings, and afterward thereby produceth crops of divers hues; and afterward they wither and thou seest them turn yellow; then He maketh them chaff. Lo! herein verily is a reminder for men of understanding. |
| 22 أَفَمَن شَرَحَ ٱللَّهُ صَدْرَهُۥ لِلْإِسْلَـٰمِ فَهُوَ عَلَىٰ نُورٍ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ ۚ فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْقَـٰسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُم مِّن ذِكْرِ ٱللَّهِ ۚ أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ فِى ضَلَـٰلٍ مُّبِينٍ | ʾafaman sharaḥa llāhu ṣadrahu lilʾislāmi fahuwa ʿalā nūrin min rabbihi fawaylun lilqāsiyati qulūbuhum min dhikri llāhi ʾūlāʾika fi ḍalālin mubīn | Is he whose bosom Allah hath expanded for Al-Islam, so that he followeth a light from his Lord, (as he who disbelieveth)? Then woe unto those whose hearts are hardened against remembrance of Allah. Such are in plain error. |

| 54 وَأَنِيبُوٓا۟ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمْ وَأَسْلِمُوا۟ لَهُۥ مِن قَبْلِ أَن يَأْتِيَكُمُ ٱلْعَذَابُ ثُمَّ لَا تُنصَرُونَ | Waʾanībū ʾilā rabbikum waʾaslimū lahu min qabli ʾan yaʾtiyakumu l-ʿadhābu thumma lā tunṣarūn | Turn unto your Lord repentant, and surrender unto Him, before there come unto you the doom, when ye cannot be helped. |

| 56 أَن تَقُولَ نَفْسٌ يَـٰحَسْرَتَىٰ عَلَىٰ مَا فَرَّطتُ فِى جَنۢبِ ٱللَّهِ وَإِن كُنتُ لَمِنَ ٱلسَّـٰخِرِينَ | ʾan taqūla nafsun yāḥasratā ʿalā mā farraṭtu fi janbi llāhi waʾin kuntu lamina s-sākhirīn | Lest any soul should say: Alas, my grief that I was unmindful of Allah, and I was indeed among the scoffers! |
| 57 أَوْ تَقُولَ لَوْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هَدَىٰنِى لَكُنتُ مِنَ ٱلْمُتَّقِينَ | ʾaw taqūla law ʾanna llāha hadāni lakuntu mina l-muttaqīn | Or should say: If Allah had but guided me I should have been among the dutiful! |
| 58 أَوْ تَقُولَ حِينَ تَرَى ٱلْعَذَابَ لَوْ أَنَّ لِى كَرَّةً فَأَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلْمُحْسِنِينَ | ʾaw taqūla ḥīna tarā l-ʿadhāba law ʾanna li karratan faʾakūna mina l-muḥsinīn | Or should say, when it seeth the doom: Oh, that I had but a second chance that I might be among the righteous! |
| 59 بَلَىٰ قَدْ جَآءَتْكَ ءَايَـٰتِى فَكَذَّبْتَ بِهَا وَٱسْتَكْبَرْتَ وَكُنتَ مِنَ ٱلْكَـٰفِرِينَ | Balā qad jāʾatka ʾāyāti fakadhdhabta bihā wastakbarta wakunta mina l-kāfirīn | (But now the answer will be): Nay, for My revelations came unto thee, but thou didst deny them and wast scornful and wast among the disbelievers. |
| 60 وَيَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَـٰمَةِ تَرَى ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَبُوا۟ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ وُجُوهُهُم مُّسْوَدَّةٌ ۚ أَلَيْسَ فِى جَهَنَّمَ مَثْوًى لِّلْمُتَكَبِّرِينَ | Wayawma l-qiyāmati tarā ladhīna kadhabū ʿalā llāhi wujūhuhum muswaddatun ʾalaysa fi jahannama mathwan lilmutakabbirīn | And on the Day of Resurrection thou (Muhammad) seest those who lied concerning Allah with their faces blackened. Is not the home of the scorners in hell? |
