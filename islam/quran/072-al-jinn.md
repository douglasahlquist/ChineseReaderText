# 72. Al-Jinn

> Arabic (Uthmani script) · Romanization (generated, approximate) · Pickthall, The Meaning of the Glorious Koran (1930)

## Verses 1–10

| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 1 قُلْ أُوحِىَ إِلَىَّ أَنَّهُ ٱسْتَمَعَ نَفَرٌ مِّنَ ٱلْجِنِّ فَقَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّا سَمِعْنَا قُرْءَانًا عَجَبًا | Qul ʾūḥiya ʾilayya ʾannahu stamaʿa nafarun mina l-jinni faqālū ʾinnā samiʿnā qurʾānan ʿajabā | Say (O Muhammad): It is revealed unto me that a company of the Jinn gave ear, and they said: Lo! we have heard a marvellous Qur'an, |
| 2 يَهْدِىٓ إِلَى ٱلرُّشْدِ فَـَٔامَنَّا بِهِۦ ۖ وَلَن نُّشْرِكَ بِرَبِّنَآ أَحَدًا | Yahdi ʾilā r-rushdi fāmannā bihi walan nushrika birabbinā ʾaḥadā | Which guideth unto righteousness, so we believe in it and we ascribe no partner unto our Lord. |
| 3 وَأَنَّهُۥ تَعَـٰلَىٰ جَدُّ رَبِّنَا مَا ٱتَّخَذَ صَـٰحِبَةً وَلَا وَلَدًا | Waʾannahu taʿālā jaddu rabbinā mā takhadha ṣāḥibatan walā waladā | And (we believe) that He - exalted be the glory of our Lord! - hath taken neither wife nor son, |
| 4 وَأَنَّهُۥ كَانَ يَقُولُ سَفِيهُنَا عَلَى ٱللَّهِ شَطَطًا | Waʾannahu kāna yaqūlu safīhunā ʿalā llāhi shaṭaṭā | And that the foolish one among us used to speak concerning Allah an atrocious lie. |
| 5 وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّآ أَن لَّن تَقُولَ ٱلْإِنسُ وَٱلْجِنُّ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًا | Waʾannā ẓanannā ʾan lan taqūla l-ʾinsu waljinnu ʿalā llāhi kadhibā | And lo! we had supposed that humankind and jinn would not speak a lie concerning Allah - |
| 6 وَأَنَّهُۥ كَانَ رِجَالٌ مِّنَ ٱلْإِنسِ يَعُوذُونَ بِرِجَالٍ مِّنَ ٱلْجِنِّ فَزَادُوهُمْ رَهَقًا | Waʾannahu kāna rijālun mina l-ʾinsi yaʿūdhūna birijālin mina l-jinni fazādūhum rahaqā | And indeed (O Muhammad) individuals of humankind used to invoke the protection of individuals of the jinn, so that they increased them in revolt against Allah); |
| 7 وَأَنَّهُمْ ظَنُّوا۟ كَمَا ظَنَنتُمْ أَن لَّن يَبْعَثَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدًا | Waʾannahum ẓannū kamā ẓanantum ʾan lan yabʿatha llāhu ʾaḥadā | And indeed they supposed, even as ye suppose, that Allah would not raise anyone (from the dead) - |
| 8 وَأَنَّا لَمَسْنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ فَوَجَدْنَـٰهَا مُلِئَتْ حَرَسًا شَدِيدًا وَشُهُبًا | Waʾannā lamasnā s-samāʾa fawajadnāhā muliʾat ḥarasan shadīdan washuhubā | And (the Jinn who had listened to the Qur'an said): We had sought the heaven but had found it filled with strong warders and meteors. |
| 9 وَأَنَّا كُنَّا نَقْعُدُ مِنْهَا مَقَـٰعِدَ لِلسَّمْعِ ۖ فَمَن يَسْتَمِعِ ٱلْـَٔانَ يَجِدْ لَهُۥ شِهَابًا رَّصَدًا | Waʾannā kunnā naqʿudu minhā maqāʿida lilssamʿi faman yastamiʿi lāna yajid lahu shihāban raṣadā | And we used to sit on places (high) therein to listen. But he who listeneth now findeth a flame in wait for him; |
| 10 وَأَنَّا لَا نَدْرِىٓ أَشَرٌّ أُرِيدَ بِمَن فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ أَمْ أَرَادَ بِهِمْ رَبُّهُمْ رَشَدًا | Waʾannā lā nadri ʾasharrun ʾurīda biman fi l-ʾarḍi ʾam ʾarāda bihim rabbuhum rashadā | And we know not whether harm is boded unto all who are in the earth, or whether their Lord intendeth guidance for them. |

| 12 وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّآ أَن لَّن نُّعْجِزَ ٱللَّهَ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ وَلَن نُّعْجِزَهُۥ هَرَبًا | Waʾannā ẓanannā ʾan lan nuʿjiza llāha fi l-ʾarḍi walan nuʿjizahu harabā | And we know that we cannot escape from Allah in the earth, nor can we escape by flight. |
| 13 وَأَنَّا لَمَّا سَمِعْنَا ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ ۖ فَمَن يُؤْمِنۢ بِرَبِّهِۦ فَلَا يَخَافُ بَخْسًا وَلَا رَهَقًا | Waʾannā lammā samiʿnā l-hudā ʾāmannā bihi faman yuʾmin birabbihi falā yakhāfu bakhsan walā rahaqā | And when we heard the guidance, we believed therein, and whoso believeth in his Lord, he feareth neither loss nor oppression. |
| 14 وَأَنَّا مِنَّا ٱلْمُسْلِمُونَ وَمِنَّا ٱلْقَـٰسِطُونَ ۖ فَمَنْ أَسْلَمَ فَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ تَحَرَّوْا۟ رَشَدًا | Waʾannā minnā l-muslimūna waminnā l-qāsiṭūna faman ʾaslama faʾūlāʾika taḥarraw rashadā | And there are among us some who have surrendered (to Allah) and there are among us some who are unjust. And whoso hath surrendered to Allah, such have taken the right path purposefully. |
| 15 وَأَمَّا ٱلْقَـٰسِطُونَ فَكَانُوا۟ لِجَهَنَّمَ حَطَبًا | Waʾammā l-qāsiṭūna fakānū lijahannama ḥaṭabā | And as for those who are unjust, they are firewood for hell. |
| 16 وَأَلَّوِ ٱسْتَقَـٰمُوا۟ عَلَى ٱلطَّرِيقَةِ لَأَسْقَيْنَـٰهُم مَّآءً غَدَقًا | Waʾallawi staqāmū ʿalā ṭ-ṭarīqati laʾasqaynāhum māʾan ghadaqā | If they (the idolaters) tread the right path, We shall give them to drink of water in abundance |
| 17 لِّنَفْتِنَهُمْ فِيهِ ۚ وَمَن يُعْرِضْ عَن ذِكْرِ رَبِّهِۦ يَسْلُكْهُ عَذَابًا صَعَدًا | Linaftinahum fīhi waman yuʿriḍ ʿan dhikri rabbihi yaslukhu ʿadhāban ṣaʿadā | That We may test them thereby, and whoso turneth away from the remembrance of his Lord; He will thrust him into ever-growing torment. |
| 18 وَأَنَّ ٱلْمَسَـٰجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدْعُوا۟ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدًا | Waʾanna l-masājida lillāhi falā tadʿū maʿa llāhi ʾaḥadā | And the places of worship are only for Allah, so pray not unto anyone along with Allah. |
| 19 وَأَنَّهُۥ لَمَّا قَامَ عَبْدُ ٱللَّهِ يَدْعُوهُ كَادُوا۟ يَكُونُونَ عَلَيْهِ لِبَدًا | Waʾannahu lammā qāma ʿabdu llāhi yadʿūhu kādū yakūnūna ʿalayhi libadā | And when the slave of Allah stood up in prayer to Him, they crowded on him, almost stifling. |
| 20 قُلْ إِنَّمَآ أَدْعُوا۟ رَبِّى وَلَآ أُشْرِكُ بِهِۦٓ أَحَدًا | Qul ʾinnamā ʾadʿū rabbi walā ʾushriku bihi ʾaḥadā | Say (unto them, O Muhammad): I pray unto Allah only, and ascribe unto Him no partner. |

| 23 إِلَّا بَلَـٰغًا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرِسَـٰلَـٰتِهِۦ ۚ وَمَن يَعْصِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ نَارَ جَهَنَّمَ خَـٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدًا | ʾillā balāghan mina llāhi warisālātihi waman yaʿṣi llāha warasūlahu faʾinna lahu nāra jahannama khālidīna fīhā ʾabadā | (Mine is) but conveyance (of the Truth) from Allah, and His messages; and whoso disobeyeth Allah and His messenger, lo! his is fire of hell, wherein such dwell for ever. |
| 24 حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا رَأَوْا۟ مَا يُوعَدُونَ فَسَيَعْلَمُونَ مَنْ أَضْعَفُ نَاصِرًا وَأَقَلُّ عَدَدًا | Ḥattā ʾidhā raʾaw mā yūʿadūna fasayaʿlamūna man ʾaḍʿafu nāṣiran waʾaqallu ʿadadā | Till (the day) when they shall behold that which they are promised (they may doubt); but then they will know (for certain) who is weaker in allies and less in multitude. |
| 25 قُلْ إِنْ أَدْرِىٓ أَقَرِيبٌ مَّا تُوعَدُونَ أَمْ يَجْعَلُ لَهُۥ رَبِّىٓ أَمَدًا | Qul ʾin ʾadri ʾaqarībun mā tūʿadūna ʾam yajʿalu lahu rabbi ʾamadā | Say (O Muhammad, unto the disbelievers): I know not whether that which ye are promised is nigh, or if my Lord hath set a distant term for it. |
| 26 عَـٰلِمُ ٱلْغَيْبِ فَلَا يُظْهِرُ عَلَىٰ غَيْبِهِۦٓ أَحَدًا | ʿālimu l-ghaybi falā yuẓhiru ʿalā ghaybihi ʾaḥadā | (He is) the Knower of the Unseen, and He revealeth unto none His secret, |
| 27 إِلَّا مَنِ ٱرْتَضَىٰ مِن رَّسُولٍ فَإِنَّهُۥ يَسْلُكُ مِنۢ بَيْنِ يَدَيْهِ وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِۦ رَصَدًا | ʾillā mani rtaḍā min rasūlin faʾinnahu yasluku min bayni yadayhi wamin khalfihi raṣadā | Save unto every messenger whom He hath chosen, and then He maketh a guard to go before him and a guard behind him |
| 28 لِّيَعْلَمَ أَن قَدْ أَبْلَغُوا۟ رِسَـٰلَـٰتِ رَبِّهِمْ وَأَحَاطَ بِمَا لَدَيْهِمْ وَأَحْصَىٰ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ عَدَدًۢا | Liyaʿlama ʾan qad ʾablaghū risālāti rabbihim waʾaḥāṭa bimā ladayhim waʾaḥṣā kulla shāʾin ʿadadā | That He may know that they have indeed conveyed the messages of their Lord. He surroundeth all their doings, and He keepeth count of all things. |
