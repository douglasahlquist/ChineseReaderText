| 2 بَلْ عَجِبُوٓا۟ أَن جَآءَهُم مُّنذِرٌ مِّنْهُمْ فَقَالَ ٱلْكَـٰفِرُونَ هَـٰذَا شَىْءٌ عَجِيبٌ | Bal ʿajibū ʾan jāʾahum mundhirun minhum faqāla l-kāfirūna hādhā shāʾun ʿajīb | Nay, but they marvel that a warner of their own hath come unto them; and the disbelievers say: This is a strange thing: |
| 3 أَءِذَا مِتْنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابًا ۖ ذَٰلِكَ رَجْعٌۢ بَعِيدٌ | ʾaʾidhā mitnā wakunnā turāban dhālika rajʿun baʿīd | When we are dead and have become dust (shall we be brought back again)? That would be a far return! |
| 4 قَدْ عَلِمْنَا مَا تَنقُصُ ٱلْأَرْضُ مِنْهُمْ ۖ وَعِندَنَا كِتَـٰبٌ حَفِيظٌۢ | Qad ʿalimnā mā tanquṣu l-ʾarḍu minhum waʿindanā kitābun ḥafīẓ | We know that which the earth taketh of them, and with Us is a recording Book. |
| 5 بَلْ كَذَّبُوا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمْ فَهُمْ فِىٓ أَمْرٍ مَّرِيجٍ | Bal kadhdhabū bilḥaqqi lammā jāʾahum fahum fi ʾamrin marīj | Nay, but they have denied the truth when it came unto them, therefor they are now in troubled case. |
| 6 أَفَلَمْ يَنظُرُوٓا۟ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَوْقَهُمْ كَيْفَ بَنَيْنَـٰهَا وَزَيَّنَّـٰهَا وَمَا لَهَا مِن فُرُوجٍ | ʾafalam yanẓurū ʾilā s-samāʾi fawqahum kayfa banaynāhā wazayyannāhā wamā lahā min furūj | Have they not then observed the sky above them, how We have constructed it and beautified it, and how there are no rifts therein? |
| 7 وَٱلْأَرْضَ مَدَدْنَـٰهَا وَأَلْقَيْنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِىَ وَأَنۢبَتْنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوْجٍۭ بَهِيجٍ | Walʾarḍa madadnāhā waʾalqaynā fīhā rawāsiya waʾanbatnā fīhā min kulli zawjin bahīj | And the earth have We spread out, and have flung firm hills therein, and have caused of every lovely kind to grow thereon, |
| 8 تَبْصِرَةً وَذِكْرَىٰ لِكُلِّ عَبْدٍ مُّنِيبٍ | Tabṣiratan wadhikrā likulli ʿabdin munīb | A vision and a reminder for every penitent slave. |
| 9 وَنَزَّلْنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءً مُّبَـٰرَكًا فَأَنۢبَتْنَا بِهِۦ جَنَّـٰتٍ وَحَبَّ ٱلْحَصِيدِ | Wanazzalnā mina s-samāʾi māʾan mubārakan faʾanbatnā bihi jannātin waḥabba l-ḥaṣīd | And We send down from the sky blessed water whereby We give growth unto gardens and the grain of crops, |
| 10 وَٱلنَّخْلَ بَاسِقَـٰتٍ لَّهَا طَلْعٌ نَّضِيدٌ | Walnnakhla bāsiqātin lahā ṭalʿun naḍīd | And lofty date-palms with ranged clusters, |

| 13 وَعَادٌ وَفِرْعَوْنُ وَإِخْوَٰنُ لُوطٍ | Waʿādun wafirʿawnu waʾikhwānu lūṭ | And (the tribe of) A'ad, and Pharaoh, and the brethren of Lot, |
| 14 وَأَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلْأَيْكَةِ وَقَوْمُ تُبَّعٍ ۚ كُلٌّ كَذَّبَ ٱلرُّسُلَ فَحَقَّ وَعِيدِ | Waʾaṣḥābu l-ʾaykati waqawmu tubbaʿin kullun kadhdhaba r-rusula faḥaqqa waʿīd | And the dwellers in the wood, and the folk of Tubb'a: every one denied their messengers, therefor My threat took effect. |
| 15 أَفَعَيِينَا بِٱلْخَلْقِ ٱلْأَوَّلِ ۚ بَلْ هُمْ فِى لَبْسٍ مِّنْ خَلْقٍ جَدِيدٍ | ʾafaʿayīnā bilkhalqi l-ʾawwali bal hum fi labsin min khalqin jadīd | Were We then worn out by the first creation? Yet they are in doubt about a new creation. |
| 16 وَلَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ وَنَعْلَمُ مَا تُوَسْوِسُ بِهِۦ نَفْسُهُۥ ۖ وَنَحْنُ أَقْرَبُ إِلَيْهِ مِنْ حَبْلِ ٱلْوَرِيدِ | Walaqad khalaqnā l-ʾinsāna wanaʿlamu mā tuwaswisu bihi nafsuhu wanaḥnu ʾaqrabu ʾilayhi min ḥabli l-warīd | We verily created man and We know what his soul whispereth to him, and We are nearer to him than his jugular vein. |
| 17 إِذْ يَتَلَقَّى ٱلْمُتَلَقِّيَانِ عَنِ ٱلْيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ قَعِيدٌ | ʾidh yatalaqqā l-mutalaqqiyāni ʿani l-yamīni waʿani sh-shimāli qaʿīd | When the two Receivers receive (him), seated on the right hand and on the left, |
| 18 مَّا يَلْفِظُ مِن قَوْلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيْهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدٌ | Mā yalfiẓu min qawlin ʾillā ladayhi raqībun ʿatīd | He uttereth no word but there is with him an observer ready. |
| 19 وَجَآءَتْ سَكْرَةُ ٱلْمَوْتِ بِٱلْحَقِّ ۖ ذَٰلِكَ مَا كُنتَ مِنْهُ تَحِيدُ | Wajāʾat sakratu l-mawti bilḥaqqi dhālika mā kunta minhu taḥīd | And the agony of death cometh in truth. (And it is said unto him): This is that which thou wast wont to shun. |
| 20 وَنُفِخَ فِى ٱلصُّورِ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ يَوْمُ ٱلْوَعِيدِ | Wanufikha fi ṣ-ṣūri dhālika yawmu l-waʿīd | And the trumpet is blown. This is the threatened Day. |

| 30 يَوْمَ نَقُولُ لِجَهَنَّمَ هَلِ ٱمْتَلَأْتِ وَتَقُولُ هَلْ مِن مَّزِيدٍ | Yawma naqūlu lijahannama hali mtalaʾti wataqūlu hal min mazīd | On the day when We say unto hell: Art thou filled? and it saith: Can there be more to come? |

| 35 لَهُم مَّا يَشَآءُونَ فِيهَا وَلَدَيْنَا مَزِيدٌ | Lahum mā yashāʾūna fīhā waladaynā mazīd | There they have all that they desire, and there is more with Us. |
| 36 وَكَمْ أَهْلَكْنَا قَبْلَهُم مِّن قَرْنٍ هُمْ أَشَدُّ مِنْهُم بَطْشًا فَنَقَّبُوا۟ فِى ٱلْبِلَـٰدِ هَلْ مِن مَّحِيصٍ | Wakam ʾahlaknā qablahum min qarnin hum ʾashaddu minhum baṭshan fanaqqabū fi l-bilādi hal min maḥīṣ | And how many a generation We destroyed before them, who were mightier than these in prowess so that they overran the lands! Had they any place of refuge (when the judgment came)? |
| 37 إِنَّ فِى ذَٰلِكَ لَذِكْرَىٰ لِمَن كَانَ لَهُۥ قَلْبٌ أَوْ أَلْقَى ٱلسَّمْعَ وَهُوَ شَهِيدٌ | ʾinna fi dhālika ladhikrā liman kāna lahu qalbun ʾaw ʾalqā s-samʿa wahuwa shahīd | Lo! therein verily is a reminder for him who hath a heart, or giveth ear with full intelligence. |
| 38 وَلَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا فِى سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍ وَمَا مَسَّنَا مِن لُّغُوبٍ | Walaqad khalaqnā s-samāwāti walʾarḍa wamā baynahumā fi sittati ʾayyāmin wamā massanā min lughūb | And verily We created the heavens and the earth, and all that is between them, in six Days, and naught of weariness touched Us. |
| 39 فَٱصْبِرْ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ قَبْلَ طُلُوعِ ٱلشَّمْسِ وَقَبْلَ ٱلْغُرُوبِ | Faṣbir ʿalā mā yaqūlūna wasabbiḥ biḥamdi rabbika qabla ṭulūʿi sh-shamsi waqabla l-ghurūb | Therefor (O Muhammad) bear with what they say, and hymn the praise of thy Lord before the rising and before the setting of the sun; |
| 40 وَمِنَ ٱلَّيْلِ فَسَبِّحْهُ وَأَدْبَـٰرَ ٱلسُّجُودِ | Wamina layli fasabbiḥhu waʾadbāra s-sujūd | And in the night-time hymn His praise, and after the (prescribed) prostrations. |
