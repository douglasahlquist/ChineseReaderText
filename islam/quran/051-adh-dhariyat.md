| 19 وَفِىٓ أَمْوَٰلِهِمْ حَقٌّ لِّلسَّآئِلِ وَٱلْمَحْرُومِ | Wafi ʾamwālihim ḥaqqun lilssāʾili walmaḥrūm | And in their wealth the beggar and the outcast had due share. |
| 20 وَفِى ٱلْأَرْضِ ءَايَـٰتٌ لِّلْمُوقِنِينَ | Wafi l-ʾarḍi ʾāyātun lilmūqinīn | And in the earth are portents for those whose faith is sure. |
