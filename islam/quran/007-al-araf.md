| 3 ٱتَّبِعُوا۟ مَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيْكُم مِّن رَّبِّكُمْ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُوا۟ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوْلِيَآءَ ۗ قَلِيلًا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ | Ittabiʿū mā ʾunzila ʾilaykum min rabbikum walā tattabiʿū min dūnihi ʾawliyāʾa qalīlan mā tadhakkarūn | (Saying): Follow that which is sent down unto you from your Lord, and follow no protecting friends beside Him. Little do ye recollect! |
| 4 وَكَم مِّن قَرْيَةٍ أَهْلَكْنَـٰهَا فَجَآءَهَا بَأْسُنَا بَيَـٰتًا أَوْ هُمْ قَآئِلُونَ | Wakam min qaryatin ʾahlaknāhā fajāʾahā baʾsunā bayātan ʾaw hum qāʾilūn | How many a township have We destroyed! As a raid by night, or while they slept at noon, Our terror came unto them. |
| 5 فَمَا كَانَ دَعْوَىٰهُمْ إِذْ جَآءَهُم بَأْسُنَآ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّا كُنَّا ظَـٰلِمِينَ | Famā kāna daʿwāhum ʾidh jāʾahum baʾsunā ʾillā ʾan qālū ʾinnā kunnā ẓālimīn | No plea had they, when Our terror came unto them, save that they said: Lo! We were wrong-doers. |
| 6 فَلَنَسْـَٔلَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أُرْسِلَ إِلَيْهِمْ وَلَنَسْـَٔلَنَّ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ | Falanasalanna ladhīna ʾursila ʾilayhim walanasalanna l-mursalīn | Then verily We shall question those unto whom (Our message) hath been sent, and verily We shall question the messengers. |
| 7 فَلَنَقُصَّنَّ عَلَيْهِم بِعِلْمٍ ۖ وَمَا كُنَّا غَآئِبِينَ | Falanaquṣṣanna ʿalayhim biʿilmin wamā kunnā ghāʾibīn | Then verily We shall narrate unto them (the event) with knowledge, for We were not absent (when it came to pass). |
| 8 وَٱلْوَزْنُ يَوْمَئِذٍ ٱلْحَقُّ ۚ فَمَن ثَقُلَتْ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْمُفْلِحُونَ | Walwaznu yawmaʾidhin l-ḥaqqu faman thaqulat mawāzīnuhu faʾūlāʾika humu l-mufliḥūn | The weighing on that day is the true (weighing). As for those whose scale is heavy, they are the successful. |
| 9 وَمَنْ خَفَّتْ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓا۟ أَنفُسَهُم بِمَا كَانُوا۟ بِـَٔايَـٰتِنَا يَظْلِمُونَ | Waman khaffat mawāzīnuhu faʾūlāʾika ladhīna khasirū ʾanfusahum bimā kānū biyātinā yaẓlimūn | And as for those whose scale is light: those are they who lose their souls because they used to wrong Our revelations. |
| 10 وَلَقَدْ مَكَّنَّـٰكُمْ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ وَجَعَلْنَا لَكُمْ فِيهَا مَعَـٰيِشَ ۗ قَلِيلًا مَّا تَشْكُرُونَ | Walaqad makkannākum fi l-ʾarḍi wajaʿalnā lakum fīhā maʿāyisha qalīlan mā tashkurūn | And We have given you (mankind) power in the earth, and appointed for you therein livelihoods. Little give ye thanks! |

| 12 قَالَ مَا مَنَعَكَ أَلَّا تَسْجُدَ إِذْ أَمَرْتُكَ ۖ قَالَ أَنَا۠ خَيْرٌ مِّنْهُ خَلَقْتَنِى مِن نَّارٍ وَخَلَقْتَهُۥ مِن طِينٍ | Qāla mā manaʿaka ʾallā tasjuda ʾidh ʾamartuka qāla ʾanā khayrun minhu khalaqtani min nārin wakhalaqtahu min ṭīn | He said: What hindered thee that thou didst not fall prostrate when I bade thee? | 15 قَالَ إِنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلْمُنظَرِينَ | Qāla ʾinnaka mina l-munẓarīn | He said: Lo! thou art of those reprieved. |
| 16 قَالَ فَبِمَآ أَغْوَيْتَنِى لَأَقْعُدَنَّ لَهُمْ صِرَٰطَكَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ | Qāla fabimā ʾaghwaytani laʾaqʿudanna lahum ṣirāṭaka l-mustaqīm | He said: Now, because Thou hast sent me astray, verily I shall lurk in ambush for them on Thy Right Path. |
| 17 ثُمَّ لَـَٔاتِيَنَّهُم مِّنۢ بَيْنِ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ وَعَنْ أَيْمَـٰنِهِمْ وَعَن شَمَآئِلِهِمْ ۖ وَلَا تَجِدُ أَكْثَرَهُمْ شَـٰكِرِينَ | Thumma lātiyannahum min bayni ʾaydīhim wamin khalfihim waʿan ʾaymānihim waʿan shamāʾilihim walā tajidu ʾaktharahum shākirīn | Then I shall come upon them from before them and from behind them and from their right hands and from their left hands, and Thou wilt not find most of them beholden (unto Thee). |
| 18 قَالَ ٱخْرُجْ مِنْهَا مَذْءُومًا مَّدْحُورًا ۖ لَّمَن تَبِعَكَ مِنْهُمْ لَأَمْلَأَنَّ جَهَنَّمَ مِنكُمْ أَجْمَعِينَ | Qāla khruj minhā madhʾūman madḥūran laman tabiʿaka minhum laʾamlaʾanna jahannama minkum ʾajmaʿīn | He said: Go forth from hence, degraded, banished. As for such of them as follow thee, surely I will fill hell with all of you. |
| 19 وَيَـٰٓـَٔادَمُ ٱسْكُنْ أَنتَ وَزَوْجُكَ ٱلْجَنَّةَ فَكُلَا مِنْ حَيْثُ شِئْتُمَا وَلَا تَقْرَبَا هَـٰذِهِ ٱلشَّجَرَةَ فَتَكُونَا مِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ | Wayādamu skun ʾanta wazawjuka l-jannata fakulā min ḥaythu shiʾtumā walā taqrabā hādhihi sh-shajarata fatakūnā mina ẓ-ẓālimīn | And (unto man): O Adam! Dwell thou and thy wife in the Garden and eat from whence ye will, but come not nigh this tree lest ye become wrong-doers. |
| 20 فَوَسْوَسَ لَهُمَا ٱلشَّيْطَـٰنُ لِيُبْدِىَ لَهُمَا مَا وُۥرِىَ عَنْهُمَا مِن سَوْءَٰتِهِمَا وَقَالَ مَا نَهَىٰكُمَا رَبُّكُمَا عَنْ هَـٰذِهِ ٱلشَّجَرَةِ إِلَّآ أَن تَكُونَا مَلَكَيْنِ أَوْ تَكُونَا مِنَ ٱلْخَـٰلِدِينَ | Fawaswasa lahumā sh-shayṭānu liyubdiya lahumā mā wuriya ʿanhumā min sawʾātihimā waqāla mā nahākumā rabbukumā ʿan hādhihi sh-shajarati ʾillā ʾan takūnā malakayni ʾaw takūnā mina l-khālidīn | Then Satan whispered to them that he might manifest unto them that which was hidden from them of their shame, and he said: Your Lord forbade you from this tree only lest ye should become angels or become of the immortals. |

| 23 قَالَا رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَآ أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلْخَـٰسِرِينَ | Qālā rabbanā ẓalamnā ʾanfusanā waʾin lam taghfir lanā watarḥamnā lanakūnanna mina l-khāsirīn | They said: Our Lord! | 24 قَالَ ٱهْبِطُوا۟ بَعْضُكُمْ لِبَعْضٍ عَدُوٌّ ۖ وَلَكُمْ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ مُسْتَقَرٌّ وَمَتَـٰعٌ إِلَىٰ حِينٍ | Qāla hbiṭū baʿḍukum libaʿḍin ʿaduwwun walakum fi l-ʾarḍi mustaqarrun wamatāʿun ʾilā ḥīn | He said: Go down (from hence), one of you a foe unto the other. There will be for you on earth a habitation and provision for a while. |
| 25 قَالَ فِيهَا تَحْيَوْنَ وَفِيهَا تَمُوتُونَ وَمِنْهَا تُخْرَجُونَ | Qāla fīhā taḥyawna wafīhā tamūtūna waminhā tukhrajūn | He said: There shall ye live, and there shall ye die, and thence shall ye be brought forth. |
| 26 يَـٰبَنِىٓ ءَادَمَ قَدْ أَنزَلْنَا عَلَيْكُمْ لِبَاسًا يُوَٰرِى سَوْءَٰتِكُمْ وَرِيشًا ۖ وَلِبَاسُ ٱلتَّقْوَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ خَيْرٌ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ مِنْ ءَايَـٰتِ ٱللَّهِ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَذَّكَّرُونَ | Yābani ʾādama qad ʾanzalnā ʿalaykum libāsan yuwāri sawʾātikum warīshan walibāsu t-taqwā dhālika khayrun dhālika min ʾāyāti llāhi laʿallahum yadhdhakkarūn | O Children of Adam! We have revealed unto you raiment to conceal your shame, and splendid vesture, but the raiment of restraint from evil, that is best. This is of the revelations of Allah, that they may remember. |
| 27 يَـٰبَنِىٓ ءَادَمَ لَا يَفْتِنَنَّكُمُ ٱلشَّيْطَـٰنُ كَمَآ أَخْرَجَ أَبَوَيْكُم مِّنَ ٱلْجَنَّةِ يَنزِعُ عَنْهُمَا لِبَاسَهُمَا لِيُرِيَهُمَا سَوْءَٰتِهِمَآ ۗ إِنَّهُۥ يَرَىٰكُمْ هُوَ وَقَبِيلُهُۥ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا تَرَوْنَهُمْ ۗ إِنَّا جَعَلْنَا ٱلشَّيَـٰطِينَ أَوْلِيَآءَ لِلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ | Yābani ʾādama lā yaftinannakumu sh-shayṭānu kamā ʾakhraja ʾabawaykum mina l-jannati yanziʿu ʿanhumā libāsahumā liyuriyahumā sawʾātihimā ʾinnahu yarākum huwa waqabīluhu min ḥaythu lā tarawnahum ʾinnā jaʿalnā sh-shayāṭīna ʾawliyāʾa lilladhīna lā yuʾminūn | O Children of Adam! Lo! he seeth you, he and his tribe, from whence ye see him not. Lo! We have made the devils protecting friends for those who believe not. |
| 28 وَإِذَا فَعَلُوا۟ فَـٰحِشَةً قَالُوا۟ وَجَدْنَا عَلَيْهَآ ءَابَآءَنَا وَٱللَّهُ أَمَرَنَا بِهَا ۗ قُلْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَأْمُرُ بِٱلْفَحْشَآءِ ۖ أَتَقُولُونَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعْلَمُونَ | Waʾidhā faʿalū fāḥishatan qālū wajadnā ʿalayhā ʾābāʾanā walllahu ʾamaranā bihā qul ʾinna llāha lā yaʾmuru bilfaḥshāʾi ʾataqūlūna ʿalā llāhi mā lā taʿlamūn | And when they do some lewdness they say: We found our fathers doing it and Allah hath enjoined it on us. Say: Allah, verily, enjoineth not lewdness. Tell ye concerning Allah that which ye know not? |
| 29 قُلْ أَمَرَ رَبِّى بِٱلْقِسْطِ ۖ وَأَقِيمُوا۟ وُجُوهَكُمْ عِندَ كُلِّ مَسْجِدٍ وَٱدْعُوهُ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ ۚ كَمَا بَدَأَكُمْ تَعُودُونَ | Qul ʾamara rabbi bilqisṭi waʾaqīmū wujūhakum ʿinda kulli masjidin wadʿūhu mukhliṣīna lahu d-dīna kamā badaʾakum taʿūdūn | Say: My Lord enjoineth justice. | 30 فَرِيقًا هَدَىٰ وَفَرِيقًا حَقَّ عَلَيْهِمُ ٱلضَّلَـٰلَةُ ۗ إِنَّهُمُ ٱتَّخَذُوا۟ ٱلشَّيَـٰطِينَ أَوْلِيَآءَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَحْسَبُونَ أَنَّهُم مُّهْتَدُونَ | Farīqan hadā wafarīqan ḥaqqa ʿalayhimu ḍ-ḍalālatu ʾinnahumu takhadhū sh-shayāṭīna ʾawliyāʾa min dūni llāhi wayaḥsabūna ʾannahum muhtadūn | A party hath He led aright, while error hath just hold over (another) party, for lo! they choose the devils for protecting supporters instead of Allah and deem that they are rightly guided. |

| 203 وَإِذَا لَمْ تَأْتِهِم بِـَٔايَةٍ قَالُوا۟ لَوْلَا ٱجْتَبَيْتَهَا ۚ قُلْ إِنَّمَآ أَتَّبِعُ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَىَّ مِن رَّبِّى ۚ هَـٰذَا بَصَآئِرُ مِن رَّبِّكُمْ وَهُدًى وَرَحْمَةٌ لِّقَوْمٍ يُؤْمِنُونَ | Waʾidhā lam taʾtihim biyatin qālū lawlā jtabaytahā qul ʾinnamā ʾattabiʿu mā yūḥā ʾilayya min rabbi hādhā baṣāʾiru min rabbikum wahudan waraḥmatun liqawmin yuʾminūn | And when thou bringest not a verse for them they say: Why hast thou not chosen it? Say: I follow only that which is inspired in me from my Lord. This (Qur'an) is insight from your Lord, and a guidance and a mercy for a people that believe. |
| 204 وَإِذَا قُرِئَ ٱلْقُرْءَانُ فَٱسْتَمِعُوا۟ لَهُۥ وَأَنصِتُوا۟ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُرْحَمُونَ | Waʾidhā quriʾa l-qurʾānu fastamiʿū lahu waʾanṣitū laʿallakum turḥamūn | And when the Qur'an is recited, give ear to it and pay heed, that ye may obtain mercy. |
| 205 وَٱذْكُر رَّبَّكَ فِى نَفْسِكَ تَضَرُّعًا وَخِيفَةً وَدُونَ ٱلْجَهْرِ مِنَ ٱلْقَوْلِ بِٱلْغُدُوِّ وَٱلْـَٔاصَالِ وَلَا تَكُن مِّنَ ٱلْغَـٰفِلِينَ | Wadhkur rabbaka fi nafsika taḍarruʿan wakhīfatan wadūna l-jahri mina l-qawli bilghuduwwi walāṣāli walā takun mina l-ghāfilīn | And do thou (O Muhammad) remember thy Lord within thyself humbly and with awe, below thy breath, at morn and evening. And be not thou of the neglectful. |
| 206 إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ عِندَ رَبِّكَ لَا يَسْتَكْبِرُونَ عَنْ عِبَادَتِهِۦ وَيُسَبِّحُونَهُۥ وَلَهُۥ يَسْجُدُونَ ۩ | ʾinna ladhīna ʿinda rabbika lā yastakbirūna ʿan ʿibādatihi wayusabbiḥūnahu walahu yasjudūn | Lo! those who are with thy Lord are not too proud to do Him service, but they praise Him and prostrate before Him. |
