| 14 هَـٰذِهِ ٱلنَّارُ ٱلَّتِى كُنتُم بِهَا تُكَذِّبُونَ | Hādhihi n-nāru lati kuntum bihā tukadhdhibūn | (And it is said unto them): This is the Fire which ye were wont to deny. |
| 15 أَفَسِحْرٌ هَـٰذَآ أَمْ أَنتُمْ لَا تُبْصِرُونَ | ʾafasiḥrun hādhā ʾam ʾantum lā tubṣirūn | Is this magic, or do ye not see? |
| 16 ٱصْلَوْهَا فَٱصْبِرُوٓا۟ أَوْ لَا تَصْبِرُوا۟ سَوَآءٌ عَلَيْكُمْ ۖ إِنَّمَا تُجْزَوْنَ مَا كُنتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ | Iṣlawhā faṣbirū ʾaw lā taṣbirū sawāʾun ʿalaykum ʾinnamā tujzawna mā kuntum taʿmalūn | Endure the heat thereof, and whether ye are patient of it or impatient of it is all one for you. | 17 إِنَّ ٱلْمُتَّقِينَ فِى جَنَّـٰتٍ وَنَعِيمٍ | ʾinna l-muttaqīna fi jannātin wanaʿīm | Lo! those who kept their duty dwell in gardens and delight, |
| 18 فَـٰكِهِينَ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمْ رَبُّهُمْ وَوَقَىٰهُمْ رَبُّهُمْ عَذَابَ ٱلْجَحِيمِ | Fākihīna bimā ʾātāhum rabbuhum wawaqāhum rabbuhum ʿadhāba l-jaḥīm | Happy because of what their Lord hath given them, and (because) their Lord hath warded off from them the torment of hell-fire. |
| 19 كُلُوا۟ وَٱشْرَبُوا۟ هَنِيٓـًٔۢا بِمَا كُنتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ | Kulū washrabū haniyan bimā kuntum taʿmalūn | (And it is said unto them): Eat and drink in health (as a reward) for what ye used to do, |
| 20 مُتَّكِـِٔينَ عَلَىٰ سُرُرٍ مَّصْفُوفَةٍ ۖ وَزَوَّجْنَـٰهُم بِحُورٍ عِينٍ | Muttakīna ʿalā sururin maṣfūfatin wazawwajnāhum biḥūrin ʿīn | Reclining on ranged couches. And we wed them unto fair ones with wide, lovely eyes. |

| 25 وَأَقْبَلَ بَعْضُهُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ | Waʾaqbala baʿḍuhum ʿalā baʿḍin yatasāʾalūn | And some of them draw near unto others, questioning, |

| 28 إِنَّا كُنَّا مِن قَبْلُ نَدْعُوهُ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلْبَرُّ ٱلرَّحِيمُ | ʾinnā kunnā min qablu nadʿūhu ʾinnahu huwa l-barru r-raḥīm | Lo! we used to pray unto Him of old. Lo! He is the Benign, the Merciful. |

| 34 فَلْيَأْتُوا۟ بِحَدِيثٍ مِّثْلِهِۦٓ إِن كَانُوا۟ صَـٰدِقِينَ | Falyaʾtū biḥadīthin mithlihi ʾin kānū ṣādiqīn | Then let them produce speech the like thereof, if they are truthful. |
| 35 أَمْ خُلِقُوا۟ مِنْ غَيْرِ شَىْءٍ أَمْ هُمُ ٱلْخَـٰلِقُونَ | ʾam khuliqū min ghayri shāʾin ʾam humu l-khāliqūn | Or were they created out of naught? Or are they the creators? |
| 36 أَمْ خَلَقُوا۟ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ ۚ بَل لَّا يُوقِنُونَ | ʾam khalaqū s-samāwāti walʾarḍa bal lā yūqinūn | Or did they create the heavens and the earth? Nay, but they are sure of nothing! |
| 37 أَمْ عِندَهُمْ خَزَآئِنُ رَبِّكَ أَمْ هُمُ ٱلْمُصَۣيْطِرُونَ | ʾam ʿindahum khazāʾinu rabbika ʾam humu l-muṣayṭirūn | Or do they own the treasures of thy Lord? Or have they been given charge (thereof)? |
| 38 أَمْ لَهُمْ سُلَّمٌ يَسْتَمِعُونَ فِيهِ ۖ فَلْيَأْتِ مُسْتَمِعُهُم بِسُلْطَـٰنٍ مُّبِينٍ | ʾam lahum sullamun yastamiʿūna fīhi falyaʾti mustamiʿuhum bisulṭānin mubīn | Or have they any stairway (unto heaven) by means of which they overhear (decrees). Then let their listener produce some warrant manifest! |
| 39 أَمْ لَهُ ٱلْبَنَـٰتُ وَلَكُمُ ٱلْبَنُونَ | ʾam lahu l-banātu walakumu l-banūn | Or hath He daughters whereas ye have sons? |
| 40 أَمْ تَسْـَٔلُهُمْ أَجْرًا فَهُم مِّن مَّغْرَمٍ مُّثْقَلُونَ | ʾam tasaluhum ʾajran fahum min maghramin muthqalūn | Or askest thou (Muhammad) a fee from them so that they are plunged in debt? |

| 45 فَذَرْهُمْ حَتَّىٰ يُلَـٰقُوا۟ يَوْمَهُمُ ٱلَّذِى فِيهِ يُصْعَقُونَ | Fadharhum ḥattā yulāqū yawmahumu ladhi fīhi yuṣʿaqūn | Then let them be (O Muhammad), till they meet their day, in which they will be thunder-stricken, |

| 47 وَإِنَّ لِلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوا۟ عَذَابًا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ وَلَـٰكِنَّ أَكْثَرَهُمْ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ | Waʾinna lilladhīna ẓalamū ʿadhāban dūna dhālika walākinna ʾaktharahum lā yaʿlamūn | And verily, for those who do wrong, there is a punishment beyond that. But most of them know not. |
