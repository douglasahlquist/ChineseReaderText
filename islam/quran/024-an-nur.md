| 1 سُورَةٌ أَنزَلْنَـٰهَا وَفَرَضْنَـٰهَا وَأَنزَلْنَا فِيهَآ ءَايَـٰتٍۭ بَيِّنَـٰتٍ لَّعَلَّكُمْ تَذَكَّرُونَ | Sūratun ʾanzalnāhā wafaraḍnāhā waʾanzalnā fīhā ʾāyātin bayyinātin laʿallakum tadhakkarūn | (Here is) a surah which We have revealed and enjoined, and wherein We have revealed plain tokens, that haply ye may take heed. |
| 2 ٱلزَّانِيَةُ وَٱلزَّانِى فَٱجْلِدُوا۟ كُلَّ وَٰحِدٍ مِّنْهُمَا مِا۟ئَةَ جَلْدَةٍ ۖ وَلَا تَأْخُذْكُم بِهِمَا رَأْفَةٌ فِى دِينِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتُمْ تُؤْمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلْيَوْمِ ٱلْـَٔاخِرِ ۖ وَلْيَشْهَدْ عَذَابَهُمَا طَآئِفَةٌ مِّنَ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ | Az-zāniyatu walzzāni fajlidū kulla wāḥidin minhumā miʾata jaldatin walā taʾkhudhkum bihimā raʾfatun fi dīni llāhi ʾin kuntum tuʾminūna billlahi walyawmi lākhiri walyashhad ʿadhābahumā ṭāʾifatun mina l-muʾminīn | The adulterer and the adulteress, scourge ye each one of them (with) a hundred stripes. And let not pity for the twain withhold you from obedience to Allah, if ye believe in Allah and the Last Day. And let a party of believers witness their punishment. |
| 3 ٱلزَّانِى لَا يَنكِحُ إِلَّا زَانِيَةً أَوْ مُشْرِكَةً وَٱلزَّانِيَةُ لَا يَنكِحُهَآ إِلَّا زَانٍ أَوْ مُشْرِكٌ ۚ وَحُرِّمَ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ | Az-zāni lā yankiḥu ʾillā zāniyatan ʾaw mushrikatan walzzāniyatu lā yankiḥuhā ʾillā zānin ʾaw mushrikun waḥurrima dhālika ʿalā l-muʾminīn | The adulterer shall not marry save an adulteress or an idolatress, and the adulteress none shall marry save an adulterer or an idolater. All that is forbidden unto believers. |
| 4 وَٱلَّذِينَ يَرْمُونَ ٱلْمُحْصَنَـٰتِ ثُمَّ لَمْ يَأْتُوا۟ بِأَرْبَعَةِ شُهَدَآءَ فَٱجْلِدُوهُمْ ثَمَـٰنِينَ جَلْدَةً وَلَا تَقْبَلُوا۟ لَهُمْ شَهَـٰدَةً أَبَدًا ۚ وَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْفَـٰسِقُونَ | Walladhīna yarmūna l-muḥṣanāti thumma lam yaʾtū biʾarbaʿati shuhadāʾa fajlidūhum thamānīna jaldatan walā taqbalū lahum shahādatan ʾabadan waʾūlāʾika humu l-fāsiqūn | And those who accuse honourable women but bring not four witnesses, scourge them (with) eighty stripes and never (afterward) accept their testimony - They indeed are evil-doers - |
| 5 إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ تَابُوا۟ مِنۢ بَعْدِ ذَٰلِكَ وَأَصْلَحُوا۟ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ | ʾillā ladhīna tābū min baʿdi dhālika waʾaṣlaḥū faʾinna llāha ghafūrun raḥīm | Save those who afterward repent and make amends. (For such) lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful. |
| 6 وَٱلَّذِينَ يَرْمُونَ أَزْوَٰجَهُمْ وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُمْ شُهَدَآءُ إِلَّآ أَنفُسُهُمْ فَشَهَـٰدَةُ أَحَدِهِمْ أَرْبَعُ شَهَـٰدَٰتٍۭ بِٱللَّهِ ۙ إِنَّهُۥ لَمِنَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ | Walladhīna yarmūna ʾazwājahum walam yakun lahum shuhadāʾu ʾillā ʾanfusuhum fashahādatu ʾaḥadihim ʾarbaʿu shahādātin billlahi ʾinnahu lamina ṣ-ṣādiqīn | As for those who accuse their wives but have no witnesses except themselves; let the testimony of one of them be four testimonies, (swearing) by Allah that he is of those who speak the truth; |
| 7 وَٱلْخَـٰمِسَةُ أَنَّ لَعْنَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلْكَـٰذِبِينَ | Walkhāmisatu ʾanna laʿnata llāhi ʿalayhi ʾin kāna mina l-kādhibīn | And yet a fifth, invoking the curse of Allah on him if he is of those who lie. |
| 8 وَيَدْرَؤُا۟ عَنْهَا ٱلْعَذَابَ أَن تَشْهَدَ أَرْبَعَ شَهَـٰدَٰتٍۭ بِٱللَّهِ ۙ إِنَّهُۥ لَمِنَ ٱلْكَـٰذِبِينَ | Wayadraʾu ʿanhā l-ʿadhāba ʾan tashhada ʾarbaʿa shahādātin billlahi ʾinnahu lamina l-kādhibīn | And it shall avert the punishment from her if she bear witness before Allah four times that the thing he saith is indeed false, |
| 9 وَٱلْخَـٰمِسَةَ أَنَّ غَضَبَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيْهَآ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ | Walkhāmisata ʾanna ghaḍaba llāhi ʿalayhā ʾin kāna mina ṣ-ṣādiqīn | And a fifth (time) that the wrath of Allah be upon her if he speaketh truth. |
| 10 وَلَوْلَا فَضْلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَتُهُۥ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ تَوَّابٌ حَكِيمٌ | Walawlā faḍlu llāhi ʿalaykum waraḥmatuhu waʾanna llāha tawwābun ḥakīm | And had it not been for the grace of Allah and His mercy unto you, and that Allah is Clement, Wise, (ye had been undone). |

| 11 إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ جَآءُو بِٱلْإِفْكِ عُصْبَةٌ مِّنكُمْ ۚ لَا تَحْسَبُوهُ شَرًّا لَّكُم ۖ بَلْ هُوَ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ ۚ لِكُلِّ ٱمْرِئٍ مِّنْهُم مَّا ٱكْتَسَبَ مِنَ ٱلْإِثْمِ ۚ وَٱلَّذِى تَوَلَّىٰ كِبْرَهُۥ مِنْهُمْ لَهُۥ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٌ | ʾinna ladhīna jāʾū bilʾifki ʿuṣbatun minkum lā taḥsabūhu sharran lakum bal huwa khayrun lakum likulli mriʾin minhum mā ktasaba mina l-ʾithmi walladhi tawallā kibrahu minhum lahu ʿadhābun ʿaẓīm | Lo! they who spread the slander are a gang among you. Deem it not a bad thing for you; nay, it is good for you. Unto every man of them (will be paid) that which he hath earned of the sin; and as for him among them who had the greater share therein, his will be an awful doom. |
| 12 لَّوْلَآ إِذْ سَمِعْتُمُوهُ ظَنَّ ٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ وَٱلْمُؤْمِنَـٰتُ بِأَنفُسِهِمْ خَيْرًا وَقَالُوا۟ هَـٰذَآ إِفْكٌ مُّبِينٌ | Lawlā ʾidh samiʿtumūhu ẓanna l-muʾminūna walmuʾminātu biʾanfusihim khayran waqālū hādhā ʾifkun mubīn | Why did not the believers, men and women, when ye heard it, think good of their own own folk, and say: It is a manifest untruth? |
| 13 لَّوْلَا جَآءُو عَلَيْهِ بِأَرْبَعَةِ شُهَدَآءَ ۚ فَإِذْ لَمْ يَأْتُوا۟ بِٱلشُّهَدَآءِ فَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ هُمُ ٱلْكَـٰذِبُونَ | Lawlā jāʾū ʿalayhi biʾarbaʿati shuhadāʾa faʾidh lam yaʾtū bilshshuhadāʾi faʾūlāʾika ʿinda llāhi humu l-kādhibūn | Why did they not produce four witnesses? Since they produce not witnesses, they verily are liars in the sight of Allah. |
| 14 وَلَوْلَا فَضْلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَتُهُۥ فِى ٱلدُّنْيَا وَٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ لَمَسَّكُمْ فِى مَآ أَفَضْتُمْ فِيهِ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٌ | Walawlā faḍlu llāhi ʿalaykum waraḥmatuhu fi d-dunyā walākhirati lamassakum fi mā ʾafaḍtum fīhi ʿadhābun ʿaẓīm | Had it not been for the grace of Allah and His mercy unto you in the world and the Hereafter an awful doom had overtaken you for that whereof ye murmured. |
| 15 إِذْ تَلَقَّوْنَهُۥ بِأَلْسِنَتِكُمْ وَتَقُولُونَ بِأَفْوَاهِكُم مَّا لَيْسَ لَكُم بِهِۦ عِلْمٌ وَتَحْسَبُونَهُۥ هَيِّنًا وَهُوَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ عَظِيمٌ | ʾidh talaqqawnahu biʾalsinatikum wataqūlūna biʾafwāhikum mā laysa lakum bihi ʿilmun wataḥsabūnahu hayyinan wahuwa ʿinda llāhi ʿaẓīm | When ye welcomed it with your tongues, and uttered with your mouths that whereof ye had no knowledge, ye counted it a trifle. In the sight of Allah it is very great. |
| 16 وَلَوْلَآ إِذْ سَمِعْتُمُوهُ قُلْتُم مَّا يَكُونُ لَنَآ أَن نَّتَكَلَّمَ بِهَـٰذَا سُبْحَـٰنَكَ هَـٰذَا بُهْتَـٰنٌ عَظِيمٌ | Walawlā ʾidh samiʿtumūhu qultum mā yakūnu lanā ʾan natakallama bihādhā subḥānaka hādhā buhtānun ʿaẓīm | Wherefor, when ye heard it, said ye not: It is not for us to speak of this. Glory be to Thee (O Allah)! This is awful calumny. |
| 17 يَعِظُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ أَن تَعُودُوا۟ لِمِثْلِهِۦٓ أَبَدًا إِن كُنتُم مُّؤْمِنِينَ | Yaʿiẓukumu llāhu ʾan taʿūdū limithlihi ʾabadan ʾin kuntum muʾminīn | Allah admonisheth you that ye repeat not the like thereof ever, if ye are (in truth) believers. |
| 18 وَيُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمُ ٱلْـَٔايَـٰتِ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ | Wayubayyinu llāhu lakumu lāyāti walllahu ʿalīmun ḥakīm | And He expoundeth unto you the revelations. Allah is Knower, Wise. |
| 19 إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُحِبُّونَ أَن تَشِيعَ ٱلْفَـٰحِشَةُ فِى ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ لَهُمْ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ فِى ٱلدُّنْيَا وَٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ وَأَنتُمْ لَا تَعْلَمُونَ | ʾinna ladhīna yuḥibbūna ʾan tashīʿa l-fāḥishatu fi ladhīna ʾāmanū lahum ʿadhābun ʾalīmun fi d-dunyā walākhirati walllahu yaʿlamu waʾantum lā taʿlamūn | Lo! those who love that slander should be spread concerning those who believe, theirs will be a painful punishment in the world and the Hereafter. Allah knoweth. Ye know not. |
| 20 وَلَوْلَا فَضْلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَتُهُۥ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ رَءُوفٌ رَّحِيمٌ | Walawlā faḍlu llāhi ʿalaykum waraḥmatuhu waʾanna llāha raʾūfun raḥīm | Had it not been for the grace of Allah and His mercy unto you, and that Allah is Clement, Merciful, (ye had been undone). |

| 21 ۞ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ لَا تَتَّبِعُوا۟ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيْطَـٰنِ ۚ وَمَن يَتَّبِعْ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيْطَـٰنِ فَإِنَّهُۥ يَأْمُرُ بِٱلْفَحْشَآءِ وَٱلْمُنكَرِ ۚ وَلَوْلَا فَضْلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَتُهُۥ مَا زَكَىٰ مِنكُم مِّنْ أَحَدٍ أَبَدًا وَلَـٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُزَكِّى مَن يَشَآءُ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ | Yāʾayyuhā ladhīna ʾāmanū lā tattabiʿū khuṭuwāti sh-shayṭāni waman yattabiʿ khuṭuwāti sh-shayṭāni faʾinnahu yaʾmuru bilfaḥshāʾi walmunkari walawlā faḍlu llāhi ʿalaykum waraḥmatuhu mā zakā minkum min ʾaḥadin ʾabadan walākinna llāha yuzakki man yashāʾu walllahu samīʿun ʿalīm | O ye who believe! Follow not the footsteps of the devil. Unto whomsoever followeth the footsteps of the devil, lo! he commandeth filthiness and wrong. Had it not been for the grace of Allah and His mercy unto you, not one of you would ever have grown pure. But Allah causeth whom He will to grow. And Allah is Hearer, Knower. |
| 22 وَلَا يَأْتَلِ أُو۟لُوا۟ ٱلْفَضْلِ مِنكُمْ وَٱلسَّعَةِ أَن يُؤْتُوٓا۟ أُو۟لِى ٱلْقُرْبَىٰ وَٱلْمَسَـٰكِينَ وَٱلْمُهَـٰجِرِينَ فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ۖ وَلْيَعْفُوا۟ وَلْيَصْفَحُوٓا۟ ۗ أَلَا تُحِبُّونَ أَن يَغْفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمْ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ | Walā yaʾtali ʾūlū l-faḍli minkum walssaʿati ʾan yuʾtū ʾūli l-qurbā walmasākīna walmuhājirīna fi sabīli llāhi walyaʿfū walyaṣfaḥū ʾalā tuḥibbūna ʾan yaghfira llāhu lakum walllahu ghafūrun raḥīm | And let not those who possess dignity and ease among you swear not to give to the near of kin and to the needy, and to fugitives for the cause of Allah. Let them forgive and show indulgence. Yearn ye not that Allah may forgive you? Allah is Forgiving, Merciful. |
| 23 إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَرْمُونَ ٱلْمُحْصَنَـٰتِ ٱلْغَـٰفِلَـٰتِ ٱلْمُؤْمِنَـٰتِ لُعِنُوا۟ فِى ٱلدُّنْيَا وَٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ وَلَهُمْ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٌ | ʾinna ladhīna yarmūna l-muḥṣanāti l-ghāfilāti l-muʾmināti luʿinū fi d-dunyā walākhirati walahum ʿadhābun ʿaẓīm | Lo! as for those who traduce virtuous, believing women (who are) careless, cursed are they in the world and the Hereafter. Theirs will be an awful doom |
| 24 يَوْمَ تَشْهَدُ عَلَيْهِمْ أَلْسِنَتُهُمْ وَأَيْدِيهِمْ وَأَرْجُلُهُم بِمَا كَانُوا۟ يَعْمَلُونَ | Yawma tashhadu ʿalayhim ʾalsinatuhum waʾaydīhim waʾarjuluhum bimā kānū yaʿmalūn | On the day when their tongues and their hands and their feet testify against them as to what they used to do, |
| 25 يَوْمَئِذٍ يُوَفِّيهِمُ ٱللَّهُ دِينَهُمُ ٱلْحَقَّ وَيَعْلَمُونَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلْحَقُّ ٱلْمُبِينُ | Yawmaʾidhin yuwaffīhimu llāhu dīnahumu l-ḥaqqa wayaʿlamūna ʾanna llāha huwa l-ḥaqqu l-mubīn | On that day Allah will pay them their just due, and they will know that Allah, He is the Manifest Truth. |
| 26 ٱلْخَبِيثَـٰتُ لِلْخَبِيثِينَ وَٱلْخَبِيثُونَ لِلْخَبِيثَـٰتِ ۖ وَٱلطَّيِّبَـٰتُ لِلطَّيِّبِينَ وَٱلطَّيِّبُونَ لِلطَّيِّبَـٰتِ ۚ أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ مُبَرَّءُونَ مِمَّا يَقُولُونَ ۖ لَهُم مَّغْفِرَةٌ وَرِزْقٌ كَرِيمٌ | Al-khabīthātu lilkhabīthīna walkhabīthūna lilkhabīthāti walṭṭayyibātu lilṭṭayyibīna walṭṭayyibūna lilṭṭayyibāti ʾūlāʾika mubarraʾūna mimmā yaqūlūna lahum maghfiratun warizqun karīm | Vile women are for vile men, and vile men for vile women. Good women are for good men, and good men for good women; such are innocent of that which people say: For them is pardon and a bountiful provision. |

| 61 لَّيْسَ عَلَى ٱلْأَعْمَىٰ حَرَجٌ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلْأَعْرَجِ حَرَجٌ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلْمَرِيضِ حَرَجٌ وَلَا عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَن تَأْكُلُوا۟ مِنۢ بُيُوتِكُمْ أَوْ بُيُوتِ ءَابَآئِكُمْ أَوْ بُيُوتِ أُمَّهَـٰتِكُمْ أَوْ بُيُوتِ إِخْوَٰنِكُمْ أَوْ بُيُوتِ أَخَوَٰتِكُمْ أَوْ بُيُوتِ أَعْمَـٰمِكُمْ أَوْ بُيُوتِ عَمَّـٰتِكُمْ أَوْ بُيُوتِ أَخْوَٰلِكُمْ أَوْ بُيُوتِ خَـٰلَـٰتِكُمْ أَوْ مَا مَلَكْتُم مَّفَاتِحَهُۥٓ أَوْ صَدِيقِكُمْ ۚ لَيْسَ عَلَيْكُمْ جُنَاحٌ أَن تَأْكُلُوا۟ جَمِيعًا أَوْ أَشْتَاتًا ۚ فَإِذَا دَخَلْتُم بُيُوتًا فَسَلِّمُوا۟ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمْ تَحِيَّةً مِّنْ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ مُبَـٰرَكَةً طَيِّبَةً ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمُ ٱلْـَٔايَـٰتِ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَعْقِلُونَ | Laysa ʿalā l-ʾaʿmā ḥarajun walā ʿalā l-ʾaʿraji ḥarajun walā ʿalā l-marīḍi ḥarajun walā ʿalā ʾanfusikum ʾan taʾkulū min buyūtikum ʾaw buyūti ʾābāʾikum ʾaw buyūti ʾummahātikum ʾaw buyūti ʾikhwānikum ʾaw buyūti ʾakhawātikum ʾaw buyūti ʾaʿmāmikum ʾaw buyūti ʿammātikum ʾaw buyūti ʾakhwālikum ʾaw buyūti khālātikum ʾaw mā malaktum mafātiḥahu ʾaw ṣadīqikum laysa ʿalaykum junāḥun ʾan taʾkulū jamīʿan ʾaw ʾashtātan faʾidhā dakhaltum buyūtan fasallimū ʿalā ʾanfusikum taḥiyyatan min ʿindi llāhi mubārakatan ṭayyibatan kadhālika yubayyinu llāhu lakumu lāyāti laʿallakum taʿqilūn | No blame is there upon the blind nor any blame upon the lame nor any blame upon the sick nor on yourselves if ye eat from your houses, or the houses of your fathers, or the houses of your mothers, or the houses of your brothers, or the houses of your sisters, or the houses of your fathers' brothers, or the houses of your fathers' sisters, or the houses of your mothers' brothers, or the houses of your mothers' sisters, or (from that) whereof ye hold the keys, or (from the house) of a friend. No sin shall it be for you whether ye eat together or apart. But when ye enter houses, salute one another with a greeting from Allah, blessed and sweet. Thus Allah maketh clear His revelations for you, that haply ye may understand. |
| 62 إِنَّمَا ٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَإِذَا كَانُوا۟ مَعَهُۥ عَلَىٰٓ أَمْرٍ جَامِعٍ لَّمْ يَذْهَبُوا۟ حَتَّىٰ يَسْتَـْٔذِنُوهُ ۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسْتَـْٔذِنُونَكَ أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤْمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ ۚ فَإِذَا ٱسْتَـْٔذَنُوكَ لِبَعْضِ شَأْنِهِمْ فَأْذَن لِّمَن شِئْتَ مِنْهُمْ وَٱسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُمُ ٱللَّهَ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ | ʾinnamā l-muʾminūna ladhīna ʾāmanū billlahi warasūlihi waʾidhā kānū maʿahu ʿalā ʾamrin jāmiʿin lam yadhhabū ḥattā yastadhinūhu ʾinna ladhīna yastadhinūnaka ʾūlāʾika ladhīna yuʾminūna billlahi warasūlihi faʾidhā stadhanūka libaʿḍi shaʾnihim faʾdhan liman shiʾta minhum wastaghfir lahumu llāha ʾinna llāha ghafūrun raḥīm | They only are the true believers who believe in Allah and His messenger and, when they are with him on some common errand, go not away until they have asked leave of him. Lo! those who ask leave of thee, those are they who believe in Allah and His messenger. So, if they ask thy leave for some affair of theirs, give leave to whom thou wilt of them, and ask for them forgiveness of Allah. Lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful. |
| 63 لَّا تَجْعَلُوا۟ دُعَآءَ ٱلرَّسُولِ بَيْنَكُمْ كَدُعَآءِ بَعْضِكُم بَعْضًا ۚ قَدْ يَعْلَمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَتَسَلَّلُونَ مِنكُمْ لِوَاذًا ۚ فَلْيَحْذَرِ ٱلَّذِينَ يُخَالِفُونَ عَنْ أَمْرِهِۦٓ أَن تُصِيبَهُمْ فِتْنَةٌ أَوْ يُصِيبَهُمْ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ | Lā tajʿalū duʿāʾa r-rasūli baynakum kaduʿāʾi baʿḍikum baʿḍan qad yaʿlamu llāhu ladhīna yatasallalūna minkum liwādhan falyaḥdhari ladhīna yukhālifūna ʿan ʾamrihi ʾan tuṣībahum fitnatun ʾaw yuṣībahum ʿadhābun ʾalīm | Make not the calling of the messenger among you as your calling one of another. Allah knoweth those of you who steal away, hiding themselves. And let those who conspire to evade orders beware lest grief or painful punishment befall them. |
| 64 أَلَآ إِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَا فِى ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۖ قَدْ يَعْلَمُ مَآ أَنتُمْ عَلَيْهِ وَيَوْمَ يُرْجَعُونَ إِلَيْهِ فَيُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا عَمِلُوا۟ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَىْءٍ عَلِيمٌۢ | ʾalā ʾinna lillāhi mā fi s-samāwāti walʾarḍi qad yaʿlamu mā ʾantum ʿalayhi wayawma yurjaʿūna ʾilayhi fayunabbiʾuhum bimā ʿamilū walllahu bikulli shāʾin ʿalīm | Lo! verily unto Allah belongeth whatsoever is in the heavens and the earth. He knoweth your condition. And (He knoweth) the Day when they are returned unto Him so that He may inform them of what they did. Allah is Knower of all things. |
