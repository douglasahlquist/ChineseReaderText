| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |

| 1 هَلْ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلْإِنسَـٰنِ حِينٌ مِّنَ ٱلدَّهْرِ لَمْ يَكُن شَيْـًٔا مَّذْكُورًا | Hal ʾatā ʿalā l-ʾinsāni ḥīnun mina d-dahri lam yakun shayan madhkūrā | Hath there come upon man (ever) any period of time in which he was a thing unremembered? |
| 2 إِنَّا خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مِن نُّطْفَةٍ أَمْشَاجٍ نَّبْتَلِيهِ فَجَعَلْنَـٰهُ سَمِيعًۢا بَصِيرًا | ʾinnā khalaqnā l-ʾinsāna min nuṭfatin ʾamshājin nabtalīhi fajaʿalnāhu samīʿan baṣīrā | Lo! We create man from a drop of thickened fluid to test him; so We make him hearing, knowing. |
| 3 إِنَّا هَدَيْنَـٰهُ ٱلسَّبِيلَ إِمَّا شَاكِرًا وَإِمَّا كَفُورًا | ʾinnā hadaynāhu s-sabīla ʾimmā shākiran waʾimmā kafūrā | Lo! We have shown him the way, whether he be grateful or disbelieving. |
| 4 إِنَّآ أَعْتَدْنَا لِلْكَـٰفِرِينَ سَلَـٰسِلَا۟ وَأَغْلَـٰلًا وَسَعِيرًا | ʾinnā ʾaʿtadnā lilkāfirīna salāsilā waʾaghlālan wasaʿīrā | Lo! We have prepared for disbelievers manacles and carcans and a raging fire. |
| 5 إِنَّ ٱلْأَبْرَارَ يَشْرَبُونَ مِن كَأْسٍ كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا كَافُورًا | ʾinna l-ʾabrāra yashrabūna min kaʾsin kāna mizājuhā kāfūrā | Lo! the righteous shall drink of a cup whereof the mixture is of Kafur, |
| 6 عَيْنًا يَشْرَبُ بِهَا عِبَادُ ٱللَّهِ يُفَجِّرُونَهَا تَفْجِيرًا | ʿaynan yashrabu bihā ʿibādu llāhi yufajjirūnahā tafjīrā | A spring wherefrom the slaves of Allah drink, making it gush forth abundantly, |
| 7 يُوفُونَ بِٱلنَّذْرِ وَيَخَافُونَ يَوْمًا كَانَ شَرُّهُۥ مُسْتَطِيرًا | Yūfūna bilnnadhri wayakhāfūna yawman kāna sharruhu mustaṭīrā | (Because) they perform the vow and fear a day whereof the evil is wide-spreading, |
| 8 وَيُطْعِمُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ مِسْكِينًا وَيَتِيمًا وَأَسِيرًا | Wayuṭʿimūna ṭ-ṭaʿāma ʿalā ḥubbihi miskīnan wayatīman waʾasīrā | And feed with food the needy wretch, the orphan and the prisoner, for love of Him, |
| 9 إِنَّمَا نُطْعِمُكُمْ لِوَجْهِ ٱللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمْ جَزَآءً وَلَا شُكُورًا | ʾinnamā nuṭʿimukum liwajhi llāhi lā nurīdu minkum jazāʾan walā shukūrā | (Saying): We feed you, for the sake of Allah only. We wish for no reward nor thanks from you; |
| 10 إِنَّا نَخَافُ مِن رَّبِّنَا يَوْمًا عَبُوسًا قَمْطَرِيرًا | ʾinnā nakhāfu min rabbinā yawman ʿabūsan qamṭarīrā | Lo! we fear from our Lord a day of frowning and of fate. |

| 11 فَوَقَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ شَرَّ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْيَوْمِ وَلَقَّىٰهُمْ نَضْرَةً وَسُرُورًا | Fawaqāhumu llāhu sharra dhālika l-yawmi walaqqāhum naḍratan wasurūrā | Therefor Allah hath warded off from them the evil of that day, and hath made them find brightness and joy; |
| 12 وَجَزَىٰهُم بِمَا صَبَرُوا۟ جَنَّةً وَحَرِيرًا | Wajazāhum bimā ṣabarū jannatan waḥarīrā | And hath awarded them for all that they endured, a Garden and silk attire; |
| 13 مُّتَّكِـِٔينَ فِيهَا عَلَى ٱلْأَرَآئِكِ ۖ لَا يَرَوْنَ فِيهَا شَمْسًا وَلَا زَمْهَرِيرًا | Muttakīna fīhā ʿalā l-ʾarāʾiki lā yarawna fīhā shamsan walā zamharīrā | Reclining therein upon couches, they will find there neither (heat of) a sun nor bitter cold. |
| 14 وَدَانِيَةً عَلَيْهِمْ ظِلَـٰلُهَا وَذُلِّلَتْ قُطُوفُهَا تَذْلِيلًا | Wadāniyatan ʿalayhim ẓilāluhā wadhullilat quṭūfuhā tadhlīlā | The shade thereof is close upon them and the clustered fruits thereof bow down. |
| 15 وَيُطَافُ عَلَيْهِم بِـَٔانِيَةٍ مِّن فِضَّةٍ وَأَكْوَابٍ كَانَتْ قَوَارِيرَا۠ | Wayuṭāfu ʿalayhim biniyatin min fiḍḍatin waʾakwābin kānat qawārīrā | Goblets of silver are brought round for them, and beakers (as) of glass |
| 16 قَوَارِيرَا۟ مِن فِضَّةٍ قَدَّرُوهَا تَقْدِيرًا | Qawārīrā min fiḍḍatin qaddarūhā taqdīrā | (Bright as) glass but (made) of silver, which they (themselves) have measured to the measure (of their deeds). |
| 17 وَيُسْقَوْنَ فِيهَا كَأْسًا كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا زَنجَبِيلًا | Wayusqawna fīhā kaʾsan kāna mizājuhā zanjabīlā | There are they watered with a cup whereof the mixture is of Zanjabil, |
| 18 عَيْنًا فِيهَا تُسَمَّىٰ سَلْسَبِيلًا | ʿaynan fīhā tusammā salsabīlā | (The water of) a spring therein, named Salsabil. |
| 19 ۞ وَيَطُوفُ عَلَيْهِمْ وِلْدَٰنٌ مُّخَلَّدُونَ إِذَا رَأَيْتَهُمْ حَسِبْتَهُمْ لُؤْلُؤًا مَّنثُورًا | Wayaṭūfu ʿalayhim wildānun mukhalladūna ʾidhā raʾaytahum ḥasibtahum luʾluʾan manthūrā | There wait on them immortal youths, whom, when thou seest, thou wouldst take for scattered pearls. |
| 20 وَإِذَا رَأَيْتَ ثَمَّ رَأَيْتَ نَعِيمًا وَمُلْكًا كَبِيرًا | Waʾidhā raʾayta thamma raʾayta naʿīman wamulkan kabīrā | When thou seest, thou wilt see there bliss and high estate. |

| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |

| 21 عَـٰلِيَهُمْ ثِيَابُ سُندُسٍ خُضْرٌ وَإِسْتَبْرَقٌ ۖ وَحُلُّوٓا۟ أَسَاوِرَ مِن فِضَّةٍ وَسَقَىٰهُمْ رَبُّهُمْ شَرَابًا طَهُورًا | ʿāliyahum thiyābu sundusin khuḍrun waʾistabraqun waḥullū ʾasāwira min fiḍḍatin wasaqāhum rabbuhum sharāban ṭahūrā | Their raiment will be fine green silk and gold embroidery. Bracelets of silver will they wear. Their Lord will slake their thirst with a pure drink. |
| 22 إِنَّ هَـٰذَا كَانَ لَكُمْ جَزَآءً وَكَانَ سَعْيُكُم مَّشْكُورًا | ʾinna hādhā kāna lakum jazāʾan wakāna saʿyukum mashkūrā | (And it will be said unto them): Lo! this is a reward for you. Your endeavour (upon earth) hath found acceptance. |
| 23 إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا عَلَيْكَ ٱلْقُرْءَانَ تَنزِيلًا | ʾinnā naḥnu nazzalnā ʿalayka l-qurʾāna tanzīlā | Lo! We, even We, have revealed unto thee the Qur'an, a revelation; |
| 24 فَٱصْبِرْ لِحُكْمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تُطِعْ مِنْهُمْ ءَاثِمًا أَوْ كَفُورًا | Faṣbir liḥukmi rabbika walā tuṭiʿ minhum ʾāthiman ʾaw kafūrā | So submit patiently to thy Lord's command, and obey not of them any guilty one or disbeliever. |
| 25 وَٱذْكُرِ ٱسْمَ رَبِّكَ بُكْرَةً وَأَصِيلًا | Wadhkuri sma rabbika bukratan waʾaṣīlā | Remember the name of thy Lord at morn and evening. |
| 26 وَمِنَ ٱلَّيْلِ فَٱسْجُدْ لَهُۥ وَسَبِّحْهُ لَيْلًا طَوِيلًا | Wamina layli fasjud lahu wasabbiḥhu laylan ṭawīlā | And worship Him (a portion) of the night. And glorify Him through the livelong night. |
| 27 إِنَّ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ يُحِبُّونَ ٱلْعَاجِلَةَ وَيَذَرُونَ وَرَآءَهُمْ يَوْمًا ثَقِيلًا | ʾinna hāʾulāʾi yuḥibbūna l-ʿājilata wayadharūna warāʾahum yawman thaqīlā | Lo! these love fleeting life, and put behind them (the remembrance of) a grievous day. |
| 28 نَّحْنُ خَلَقْنَـٰهُمْ وَشَدَدْنَآ أَسْرَهُمْ ۖ وَإِذَا شِئْنَا بَدَّلْنَآ أَمْثَـٰلَهُمْ تَبْدِيلًا | Naḥnu khalaqnāhum washadadnā ʾasrahum waʾidhā shiʾnā baddalnā ʾamthālahum tabdīlā | We, even We, created them, and strengthened their frame. And when We will, We can replace them, bringing others like them in their stead. |
| 29 إِنَّ هَـٰذِهِۦ تَذْكِرَةٌ ۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلًا | ʾinna hādhihi tadhkiratun faman shāʾa takhadha ʾilā rabbihi sabīlā | Lo! this is an Admonishment, that whosoever will may choose a way unto his Lord. |
| 30 وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمًا | Wamā tashāʾūna ʾillā ʾan yashāʾa llāhu ʾinna llāha kāna ʿalīman ḥakīmā | Yet ye will not, unless Allah willeth. Lo! Allah is Knower, Wise. |
