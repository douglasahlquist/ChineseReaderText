| 1 وَيْلٌ لِّلْمُطَفِّفِينَ | Waylun lilmuṭaffifīn | Woe unto the defrauders: |
| 2 ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ٱكْتَالُوا۟ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ يَسْتَوْفُونَ | Alladhīna ʾidhā ktālū ʿalā n-nāsi yastawfūn | Those who when they take the measure from mankind demand it full, |
| 3 وَإِذَا كَالُوهُمْ أَو وَّزَنُوهُمْ يُخْسِرُونَ | Waʾidhā kālūhum ʾaw wazanūhum yukhsirūn | But if they measure unto them or weight for them, they cause them loss. |
| 4 أَلَا يَظُنُّ أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ أَنَّهُم مَّبْعُوثُونَ | ʾalā yaẓunnu ʾūlāʾika ʾannahum mabʿūthūn | Do such (men) not consider that they will be raised again |
| 5 لِيَوْمٍ عَظِيمٍ | Liyawmin ʿaẓīm | Unto an Awful Day, |
| 6 يَوْمَ يَقُومُ ٱلنَّاسُ لِرَبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ | Yawma yaqūmu n-nāsu lirabbi l-ʿālamīn | The day when (all) mankind stand before the Lord of the Worlds? |
| 7 كَلَّآ إِنَّ كِتَـٰبَ ٱلْفُجَّارِ لَفِى سِجِّينٍ | Kallā ʾinna kitāba l-fujjāri lafi sijjīn | Nay, but the record of the vile is in Sijjin - |
| 8 وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا سِجِّينٌ | Wamā ʾadrāka mā sijjīn | Ah! what will convey unto thee what Sijjin is! - |
| 9 كِتَـٰبٌ مَّرْقُومٌ | Kitābun marqūm | A written record. |
| 10 وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ | Waylun yawmaʾidhin lilmukadhdhibīn | Woe unto the repudiators on that day! |

| 11 ٱلَّذِينَ يُكَذِّبُونَ بِيَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ | Alladhīna yukadhdhibūna biyawmi d-dīn | Those who deny the Day of Judgment |
| 12 وَمَا يُكَذِّبُ بِهِۦٓ إِلَّا كُلُّ مُعْتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ | Wamā yukadhdhibu bihi ʾillā kullu muʿtadin ʾathīm | Which none denieth save each criminal transgressor, |
| 13 إِذَا تُتْلَىٰ عَلَيْهِ ءَايَـٰتُنَا قَالَ أَسَـٰطِيرُ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ | ʾidhā tutlā ʿalayhi ʾāyātunā qāla ʾasāṭīru l-ʾawwalīn | Who, when thou readest unto him Our revelations, saith: (Mere) fables of the men of old. |
| 14 كَلَّا ۖ بَلْ ۜ رَانَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِم مَّا كَانُوا۟ يَكْسِبُونَ | Kallā bal rāna ʿalā qulūbihim mā kānū yaksibūn | Nay, but that which they have earned is rust upon their hearts. |
| 15 كَلَّآ إِنَّهُمْ عَن رَّبِّهِمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ لَّمَحْجُوبُونَ | Kallā ʾinnahum ʿan rabbihim yawmaʾidhin lamaḥjūbūn | Nay, but surely on that day they will be covered from (the mercy of) their Lord. |
| 16 ثُمَّ إِنَّهُمْ لَصَالُوا۟ ٱلْجَحِيمِ | Thumma ʾinnahum laṣālū l-jaḥīm | Then lo! they verily will burn in hell, |
| 17 ثُمَّ يُقَالُ هَـٰذَا ٱلَّذِى كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ | Thumma yuqālu hādhā ladhi kuntum bihi tukadhdhibūn | And it will be said (unto them): This is that which ye used to deny. |
| 18 كَلَّآ إِنَّ كِتَـٰبَ ٱلْأَبْرَارِ لَفِى عِلِّيِّينَ | Kallā ʾinna kitāba l-ʾabrāri lafi ʿilliyyīn | Nay, but the record of the righteous is in 'Illiyin - |
| 19 وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا عِلِّيُّونَ | Wamā ʾadrāka mā ʿilliyyūn | Ah, what will convey unto thee what 'Illiyin is! - |
| 20 كِتَـٰبٌ مَّرْقُومٌ | Kitābun marqūm | A written record, |

| 21 يَشْهَدُهُ ٱلْمُقَرَّبُونَ | Yashhaduhu l-muqarrabūn | Attested by those who are brought near (unto their Lord). |
| 22 إِنَّ ٱلْأَبْرَارَ لَفِى نَعِيمٍ | ʾinna l-ʾabrāra lafi naʿīm | Lo! the righteous verily are in delight, |
| 23 عَلَى ٱلْأَرَآئِكِ يَنظُرُونَ | ʿalā l-ʾarāʾiki yanẓurūn | On couches, gazing, |
| 24 تَعْرِفُ فِى وُجُوهِهِمْ نَضْرَةَ ٱلنَّعِيمِ | Taʿrifu fi wujūhihim naḍrata n-naʿīm | Thou wilt know in their faces the radiance of delight. |
| 25 يُسْقَوْنَ مِن رَّحِيقٍ مَّخْتُومٍ | Yusqawna min raḥīqin makhtūm | They are given to drink of a pure wine, sealed, |
| 26 خِتَـٰمُهُۥ مِسْكٌ ۚ وَفِى ذَٰلِكَ فَلْيَتَنَافَسِ ٱلْمُتَنَـٰفِسُونَ | Khitāmuhu miskun wafi dhālika falyatanāfasi l-mutanāfisūn | Whose seal is musk - for this let (all) those strive who strive for bliss - |
| 27 وَمِزَاجُهُۥ مِن تَسْنِيمٍ | Wamizājuhu min tasnīm | And mixed with water of Tasnim, |
| 28 عَيْنًا يَشْرَبُ بِهَا ٱلْمُقَرَّبُونَ | ʿaynan yashrabu bihā l-muqarrabūn | A spring whence those brought near (to Allah) drink. |
| 29 إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أَجْرَمُوا۟ كَانُوا۟ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ يَضْحَكُونَ | ʾinna ladhīna ʾajramū kānū mina ladhīna ʾāmanū yaḍḥakūn | Lo! the guilty used to laugh at those who believed, |
| 30 وَإِذَا مَرُّوا۟ بِهِمْ يَتَغَامَزُونَ | Waʾidhā marrū bihim yataghāmazūn | And wink one to another when they passed them; |
