| 5 وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتْ | Waʾadhinat lirabbihā waḥuqqat | And attentive to her Lord in fear! |
| 6 يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدْحًا فَمُلَـٰقِيهِ | Yāʾayyuhā l-ʾinsānu ʾinnaka kādiḥun ʾilā rabbika kadḥan famulāqīh | Thou, verily, O man, art working toward thy Lord a work which thou wilt meet (in His presence). |

| 9 وَيَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰٓ أَهْلِهِۦ مَسْرُورًا | Wayanqalibu ʾilā ʾahlihi masrūrā | And will return unto his folk in joy. |
| 10 وَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِىَ كِتَـٰبَهُۥ وَرَآءَ ظَهْرِهِۦ | Waʾammā man ʾūtiya kitābahu warāʾa ẓahrih | But whoso is given his account behind his back, |

| 14 إِنَّهُۥ ظَنَّ أَن لَّن يَحُورَ | ʾinnahu ẓanna ʾan lan yaḥūr | He verily deemed that he would never return (unto Allah). |
| 15 بَلَىٰٓ إِنَّ رَبَّهُۥ كَانَ بِهِۦ بَصِيرًا | Balā ʾinna rabbahu kāna bihi baṣīrā | Nay, but lo! his Lord is ever looking on him! |
| 16 فَلَآ أُقْسِمُ بِٱلشَّفَقِ | Falā ʾuqsimu bilshshafaq | Oh, I swear by the afterglow of sunset, |
| 17 وَٱلَّيْلِ وَمَا وَسَقَ | Wallayli wamā wasaq | And by the night and all that it enshroudeth, |
| 18 وَٱلْقَمَرِ إِذَا ٱتَّسَقَ | Walqamari ʾidhā tasaq | And by the moon when she is at the full, |
| 19 لَتَرْكَبُنَّ طَبَقًا عَن طَبَقٍ | Latarkabunna ṭabaqan ʿan ṭabaq | That ye shall journey on from plane to plane. |
| 20 فَمَا لَهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ | Famā lahum lā yuʾminūn | What aileth them, then, that they believe not |
