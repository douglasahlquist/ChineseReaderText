| 2 وَإِن يَرَوْا۟ ءَايَةً يُعْرِضُوا۟ وَيَقُولُوا۟ سِحْرٌ مُّسْتَمِرٌّ | Waʾin yaraw ʾāyatan yuʿriḍū wayaqūlū siḥrun mustamirr | And if they behold a portent they turn away and say: Prolonged illusion. |
| 3 وَكَذَّبُوا۟ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓا۟ أَهْوَآءَهُمْ ۚ وَكُلُّ أَمْرٍ مُّسْتَقِرٌّ | Wakadhdhabū wattabaʿū ʾahwāʾahum wakullu ʾamrin mustaqirr | They denied (the Truth) and followed their own lusts. Yet everything will come to a decision |
| 4 وَلَقَدْ جَآءَهُم مِّنَ ٱلْأَنۢبَآءِ مَا فِيهِ مُزْدَجَرٌ | Walaqad jāʾahum mina l-ʾanbāʾi mā fīhi muzdajar | And surely there hath come unto them news whereof the purport should deter, |
| 5 حِكْمَةٌۢ بَـٰلِغَةٌ ۖ فَمَا تُغْنِ ٱلنُّذُرُ | Ḥikmatun bālighatun famā tughni n-nudhur | Effective wisdom; but warnings avail not. |
| 6 فَتَوَلَّ عَنْهُمْ ۘ يَوْمَ يَدْعُ ٱلدَّاعِ إِلَىٰ شَىْءٍ نُّكُرٍ | Fatawalla ʿanhum yawma yadʿu d-dāʿi ʾilā shāʾin nukur | So withdraw from them (O Muhammad) on the day when the Summoner summoneth unto a painful thing. |
| 7 خُشَّعًا أَبْصَـٰرُهُمْ يَخْرُجُونَ مِنَ ٱلْأَجْدَاثِ كَأَنَّهُمْ جَرَادٌ مُّنتَشِرٌ | Khushshaʿan ʾabṣāruhum yakhrujūna mina l-ʾajdāthi kaʾannahum jarādun muntashir | With downcast eyes, they come forth from the graves as they were locusts spread abroad, |
| 8 مُّهْطِعِينَ إِلَى ٱلدَّاعِ ۖ يَقُولُ ٱلْكَـٰفِرُونَ هَـٰذَا يَوْمٌ عَسِرٌ | Muhṭiʿīna ʾilā d-dāʿi yaqūlu l-kāfirūna hādhā yawmun ʿasir | Hastening toward the summoner; the disbelievers say: This is a hard day. |
| 9 ۞ كَذَّبَتْ قَبْلَهُمْ قَوْمُ نُوحٍ فَكَذَّبُوا۟ عَبْدَنَا وَقَالُوا۟ مَجْنُونٌ وَٱزْدُجِرَ | Kadhdhabat qablahum qawmu nūḥin fakadhdhabū ʿabdanā waqālū majnūnun wazdujir | The folk of Noah denied before them, yea, they denied Our slave and said: A madman; and he was repulsed. |
| 10 فَدَعَا رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّى مَغْلُوبٌ فَٱنتَصِرْ | Fadaʿā rabbahu ʾanni maghlūbun fantaṣir | So he cried unto his Lord, saying: I am vanquished, so give help. |

| 15 وَلَقَد تَّرَكْنَـٰهَآ ءَايَةً فَهَلْ مِن مُّدَّكِرٍ | Walaqad taraknāhā ʾāyatan fahal min muddakir | And verily We left it as a token; but is there any that remembereth? |

| 18 كَذَّبَتْ عَادٌ فَكَيْفَ كَانَ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ | Kadhdhabat ʿādun fakayfa kāna ʿadhābi wanudhur | (The tribe of) A'ad rejected warnings. Then how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings. |
| 19 إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ رِيحًا صَرْصَرًا فِى يَوْمِ نَحْسٍ مُّسْتَمِرٍّ | ʾinnā ʾarsalnā ʿalayhim rīḥan ṣarṣaran fi yawmi naḥsin mustamirr | Lo! We let loose on them a raging wind on a day of constant calamity, |
| 20 تَنزِعُ ٱلنَّاسَ كَأَنَّهُمْ أَعْجَازُ نَخْلٍ مُّنقَعِرٍ | Tanziʿu n-nāsa kaʾannahum ʾaʿjāzu nakhlin munqaʿir | Sweeping men away as though they were uprooted trunks of palm-trees. |

| 24 فَقَالُوٓا۟ أَبَشَرًا مِّنَّا وَٰحِدًا نَّتَّبِعُهُۥٓ إِنَّآ إِذًا لَّفِى ضَلَـٰلٍ وَسُعُرٍ | Faqālū ʾabasharan minnā wāḥidan nattabiʿuhu ʾinnā ʾidhan lafi ḍalālin wasuʿur | For they said; Is it a mortal man, alone among us, that we are to follow? | 28 وَنَبِّئْهُمْ أَنَّ ٱلْمَآءَ قِسْمَةٌۢ بَيْنَهُمْ ۖ كُلُّ شِرْبٍ مُّحْتَضَرٌ | Wanabbiʾhum ʾanna l-māʾa qismatun baynahum kullu shirbin muḥtaḍar | And inform them that the water is to be shared between (her and) them. Every drinking will be witnessed. |
| 29 فَنَادَوْا۟ صَاحِبَهُمْ فَتَعَاطَىٰ فَعَقَرَ | Fanādaw ṣāḥibahum fataʿāṭā faʿaqar | But they call their comrade and he took and hamstrung (her). |

| 34 إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ حَاصِبًا إِلَّآ ءَالَ لُوطٍ ۖ نَّجَّيْنَـٰهُم بِسَحَرٍ | ʾinnā ʾarsalnā ʿalayhim ḥāṣiban ʾillā ʾāla lūṭin najjaynāhum bisaḥar | Lo! We sent a storm of stones upon them (all) save the family of Lot, whom We rescued in the last watch of the night, |

| 36 وَلَقَدْ أَنذَرَهُم بَطْشَتَنَا فَتَمَارَوْا۟ بِٱلنُّذُرِ | Walaqad ʾandharahum baṭshatanā fatamāraw bilnnudhur | And he indeed had warned them of Our blow, but they did doubt the warnings. |
| 37 وَلَقَدْ رَٰوَدُوهُ عَن ضَيْفِهِۦ فَطَمَسْنَآ أَعْيُنَهُمْ فَذُوقُوا۟ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ | Walaqad rāwadūhu ʿan ḍayfihi faṭamasnā ʾaʿyunahum fadhūqū ʿadhābi wanudhur | They even asked of him his guests for an ill purpose. | 50 وَمَآ أَمْرُنَآ إِلَّا وَٰحِدَةٌ كَلَمْحٍۭ بِٱلْبَصَرِ | Wamā ʾamrunā ʾillā wāḥidatun kalamḥin bilbaṣar | And Our commandment is but one (commandment), as the twinkling of an eye. |
