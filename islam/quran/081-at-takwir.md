| 1 إِذَا ٱلشَّمْسُ كُوِّرَتْ | ʾidhā sh-shamsu kuwwirat | When the sun is overthrown, |
| 2 وَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ ٱنكَدَرَتْ | Waʾidhā n-nujūmu nkadarat | And when the stars fall, |
| 3 وَإِذَا ٱلْجِبَالُ سُيِّرَتْ | Waʾidhā l-jibālu suyyirat | And when the hills are moved, |
| 4 وَإِذَا ٱلْعِشَارُ عُطِّلَتْ | Waʾidhā l-ʿishāru ʿuṭṭilat | And when the camels big with young are abandoned, |
| 5 وَإِذَا ٱلْوُحُوشُ حُشِرَتْ | Waʾidhā l-wuḥūshu ḥushirat | And when the wild beasts are herded together, |
| 6 وَإِذَا ٱلْبِحَارُ سُجِّرَتْ | Waʾidhā l-biḥāru sujjirat | And when the seas rise, |
| 7 وَإِذَا ٱلنُّفُوسُ زُوِّجَتْ | Waʾidhā n-nufūsu zuwwijat | And when souls are reunited, |
| 8 وَإِذَا ٱلْمَوْءُۥدَةُ سُئِلَتْ | Waʾidhā l-mawʾudatu suʾilat | And when the girl-child that was buried alive is asked |
| 9 بِأَىِّ ذَنۢبٍ قُتِلَتْ | Biʾayyi dhanbin qutilat | For what sin she was slain, |
| 10 وَإِذَا ٱلصُّحُفُ نُشِرَتْ | Waʾidhā ṣ-ṣuḥufu nushirat | And when the pages are laid open, |

| 12 وَإِذَا ٱلْجَحِيمُ سُعِّرَتْ | Waʾidhā l-jaḥīmu suʿʿirat | And when hell is lighted, |
| 13 وَإِذَا ٱلْجَنَّةُ أُزْلِفَتْ | Waʾidhā l-jannatu ʾuzlifat | And when the Garden is brought nigh, |
| 14 عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌ مَّآ أَحْضَرَتْ | ʿalimat nafsun mā ʾaḥḍarat | (Then) every soul will know what it hath made ready. |
| 15 فَلَآ أُقْسِمُ بِٱلْخُنَّسِ | Falā ʾuqsimu bilkhunnas | Oh, but I call to witness the planets, |
| 16 ٱلْجَوَارِ ٱلْكُنَّسِ | Al-jawāri l-kunnas | The stars which rise and set, |

| 20 ذِى قُوَّةٍ عِندَ ذِى ٱلْعَرْشِ مَكِينٍ | Dhi quwwatin ʿinda dhi l-ʿarshi makīn | Mighty, established in the presence of the Lord of the Throne, |

## Verses 21–29

| Arabic | Romanization | Pickthall |
|---|---|---|
| 21 مُّطَاعٍ ثَمَّ أَمِينٍ | Muṭāʿin thamma ʾamīn | (One) to be obeyed, and trustworthy; |
| 22 وَمَا صَاحِبُكُم بِمَجْنُونٍ | Wamā ṣāḥibukum bimajnūn | And your comrade is not mad. |
| 23 وَلَقَدْ رَءَاهُ بِٱلْأُفُقِ ٱلْمُبِينِ | Walaqad raʾāhu bilʾufuqi l-mubīn | Surely he beheld Him on the clear horizon. |
| 24 وَمَا هُوَ عَلَى ٱلْغَيْبِ بِضَنِينٍ | Wamā huwa ʿalā l-ghaybi biḍanīn | And he is not avid of the Unseen. |
| 25 وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوْلِ شَيْطَـٰنٍ رَّجِيمٍ | Wamā huwa biqawli shayṭānin rajīm | Nor is this the utterance of a devil worthy to be stoned. |
| 26 فَأَيْنَ تَذْهَبُونَ | Faʾayna tadhhabūn | Whither then go ye? |
| 27 إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكْرٌ لِّلْعَـٰلَمِينَ | ʾin huwa ʾillā dhikrun lilʿālamīn | This is naught else than a reminder unto creation, |
| 28 لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمْ أَن يَسْتَقِيمَ | Liman shāʾa minkum ʾan yastaqīm | Unto whomsoever of you willeth to walk straight. |
| 29 وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ | Wamā tashāʾūna ʾillā ʾan yashāʾa llāhu rabbu l-ʿālamīn | And ye will not, unless (it be) that Allah willeth, the Lord of Creation. |
