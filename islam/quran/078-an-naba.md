| 12 وَبَنَيْنَا فَوْقَكُمْ سَبْعًا شِدَادًا | Wabanaynā fawqakum sabʿan shidādā | And We have built above you seven strong (heavens), |

| 15 لِّنُخْرِجَ بِهِۦ حَبًّا وَنَبَاتًا | Linukhrija bihi ḥabban wanabātā | Thereby to produce grain and plant, |

| 20 وَسُيِّرَتِ ٱلْجِبَالُ فَكَانَتْ سَرَابًا | Wasuyyirati l-jibālu fakānat sarābā | And the hills are set in motion and become as a mirage. |

| 21 إِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ كَانَتْ مِرْصَادًا | ʾinna jahannama kānat mirṣādā | Lo! hell lurketh in ambush, |
| 22 لِّلطَّـٰغِينَ مَـَٔابًا | Lilṭṭāghīna mābā | A home for the rebellious. |
| 23 لَّـٰبِثِينَ فِيهَآ أَحْقَابًا | Lābithīna fīhā ʾaḥqābā | They will abide therein for ages. |
| 24 لَّا يَذُوقُونَ فِيهَا بَرْدًا وَلَا شَرَابًا | Lā yadhūqūna fīhā bardan walā sharābā | Therein taste they neither coolness nor (any) drink |
| 25 إِلَّا حَمِيمًا وَغَسَّاقًا | ʾillā ḥamīman waghassāqā | Save boiling water and a paralysing cold: |
| 26 جَزَآءً وِفَاقًا | Jazāʾan wifāqā | Reward proportioned (to their evil deeds). |
| 27 إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا۟ لَا يَرْجُونَ حِسَابًا | ʾinnahum kānū lā yarjūna ḥisābā | For lo! they looked not for a reckoning; |
| 28 وَكَذَّبُوا۟ بِـَٔايَـٰتِنَا كِذَّابًا | Wakadhdhabū biyātinā kidhdhābā | They called Our revelations false with strong denial. |
| 29 وَكُلَّ شَىْءٍ أَحْصَيْنَـٰهُ كِتَـٰبًا | Wakulla shāʾin ʾaḥṣaynāhu kitābā | Everything have We recorded in a Book. |
| 30 فَذُوقُوا۟ فَلَن نَّزِيدَكُمْ إِلَّا عَذَابًا | Fadhūqū falan nazīdakum ʾillā ʿadhābā | So taste (of that which ye have earned). No increase do We give you save of torment. |

| 36 جَزَآءً مِّن رَّبِّكَ عَطَآءً حِسَابًا | Jazāʾan min rabbika ʿaṭāʾan ḥisābā | Requital from thy Lord - a gift in payment - |
| 37 رَّبِّ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ۖ لَا يَمْلِكُونَ مِنْهُ خِطَابًا | Rabbi s-samāwāti walʾarḍi wamā baynahumā r-raḥmāni lā yamlikūna minhu khiṭābā | Lord of the heavens and the earth, and (all) that is between them, the Beneficent; with Whom none can converse. |
| 38 يَوْمَ يَقُومُ ٱلرُّوحُ وَٱلْمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ صَفًّا ۖ لَّا يَتَكَلَّمُونَ إِلَّا مَنْ أَذِنَ لَهُ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنُ وَقَالَ صَوَابًا | Yawma yaqūmu r-rūḥu walmalāʾikatu ṣaffan lā yatakallamūna ʾillā man ʾadhina lahu r-raḥmānu waqāla ṣawābā | On the day when the angels and the Spirit stand arrayed, they speak not, saving him whom the Beneficent alloweth and who speaketh right. |
| 39 ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْيَوْمُ ٱلْحَقُّ ۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ مَـَٔابًا | Dhālika l-yawmu l-ḥaqqu faman shāʾa takhadha ʾilā rabbihi mābā | That is the True Day. So whoso will should seek recourse unto his Lord. |
| 40 إِنَّآ أَنذَرْنَـٰكُمْ عَذَابًا قَرِيبًا يَوْمَ يَنظُرُ ٱلْمَرْءُ مَا قَدَّمَتْ يَدَاهُ وَيَقُولُ ٱلْكَافِرُ يَـٰلَيْتَنِى كُنتُ تُرَٰبًۢا | ʾinnā ʾandharnākum ʿadhāban qarīban yawma yanẓuru l-marʾu mā qaddamat yadāhu wayaqūlu l-kāfiru yālaytani kuntu turābā | Lo! We warn you of a doom at hand, a day whereon a man will look on that which his own hands have sent before, and the disbeliever will cry: "Would that I were dust!" |
