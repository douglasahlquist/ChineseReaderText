# Hadith · What Was Downloaded

> The hadith collections downloaded for this library, and what is still missing. Updated 2026-11-18.

## Downloaded

10 collections, 36,512 hadith, from [fawazahmed0/hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api) (public domain).

- [Sahih al-Bukhari](01-bukhari.md): 7,589 hadith, 9 with missing text
- [Sahih Muslim](02-muslim.md): 7,563 hadith, 203 with missing text
- [Sunan Abu Dawud](03-abudawud.md): 5,274 hadith, 5,274 graded, 2 with missing text
- [Jami' at-Tirmidhi](04-tirmidhi.md): 3,998 hadith, 3,954 graded, 74 with missing text
- [Sunan an-Nasa'i](05-nasai.md): 5,765 hadith, 5,758 graded, 86 with missing text
- [Sunan Ibn Majah](06-ibnmajah.md): 4,343 hadith, 4,341 graded, 5 with missing text
- [Muwatta Malik](07-malik.md): 1,858 hadith, 1,858 graded, 40 with missing text
- [Forty Hadith of an-Nawawi](08-nawawi.md): 42 hadith
- [Forty Hadith Qudsi](09-qudsi.md): 40 hadith
- [Forty Hadith of Shah Waliullah Dehlawi](10-dehlawi.md): 40 hadith

Languages: Arabic (with diacritics), Arabic (without diacritics), English.

## Not in this source

- Musnad Ahmad and Sunan al-Darimi, the last two of the traditional Nine Books
- Other collections found on sunnah.com, such as Riyad as-Salihin, al-Adab al-Mufrad, Bulugh al-Maram, Mishkat al-Masabih and Shama'il Muhammadiyah

## Where the gaps might be filled

- Missing hadith text: read on sunnah.com (each collection's notes page links the numbers). Scraping sunnah.com is not allowed.
- Musnad Ahmad and al-Darimi: quranlab/hadith on Hugging Face (not there yet, checked 18 Nov 2026)
- More collections: AhmedBaset/hadith-json on GitHub (17 books, Arabic and English); check translation licensing
- Every sunnah.com collection: the sunnah.com API, which needs a key from sunnah.com
