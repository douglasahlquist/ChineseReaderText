## 语法：病 | Yǔ fǎ : bìng | Grammar: 病, 生病, 病人 and 看病

> **病了** or **生病了** is “to have got sick”. A **病人** is a patient. **看病** is to see a doctor (said of the patient) or to treat patients (said of the doctor).

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 小明 生病 了。 | Xiǎo míng shēng bìng le . | Xiaoming is sick. |
| 我 明天 去 看病。 | Wǒ míng tiān qù kàn bìng . | I'm seeing the doctor tomorrow. |

## 语法：小时 | Yǔ fǎ : xiǎo shí | Grammar: How long: 个小时

> Hours are counted with 个: 两**个小时**. How long something lasts goes after the verb: 我学习了三个小时 “I studied for three hours”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 等 了 两 个 小时。 | Wǒ men děng le liǎng gè xiǎo shí . | We waited two hours. |

## 语法：点分 | Yǔ fǎ : diǎn fēn | Grammar: Telling the time: 点 and 分

> Hours are 点 and minutes 分: 三点十分 “3:10”, 八点二十分 “8:20”. For one to nine minutes add 零: 十二点零五分 “12:05”. Ask 几点几分？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 现在 八 点 二十 分。 | Xiàn zài bā diǎn èr shí fēn . | It's 8:20. |
