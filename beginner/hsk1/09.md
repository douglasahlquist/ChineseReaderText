## 语法：几月几号 | Yǔ fǎ : jǐ yuè jǐ hào | Grammar: Dates: big to small

> Chinese dates go from the biggest unit to the smallest: month, then day, then the day of the week: **十月五号 星期天** “Sunday, October 5th”. Months are numbers + 月: 一月 January … 十二月 December.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天 是 十月 五 号。 | Jīn tiān shì shí yuè wǔ hào . | Today is October 5th. |
| 十二月 二十五 号。 | Shí èr yuè èr shí wǔ hào . | December 25th. |

## 语法：号日 | Yǔ fǎ : hào rì | Grammar: 号 and 日

> For the day of the month, people say **号** (hào); **日** (rì) is the written form, on calendars and in letters.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 五月 四 日。 | Wǔ yuè sì rì . | May 4th (written). |
| 五月 四 号。 | Wǔ yuè sì hào . | May 4th (spoken). |

## 语法：星期几 | Yǔ fǎ : xīng qī jǐ | Grammar: Days of the week

> Monday to Saturday are **星期** + a number: 星期一 Monday, 星期二 Tuesday … 星期六 Saturday. Sunday is **星期天** (spoken) or **星期日** (written). Ask with 星期几？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天 星期 几？ | Jīn tiān xīng qī jǐ ? | What day is it today? |
| 今天 星期四。 | Jīn tiān xīng qī sì . | Today is Thursday. |

## 语法：今天 | Yǔ fǎ : jīn tiān | Grammar: Time words come early

> Time words such as **今天**, **明天**, **昨天** go before the verb, either before or after the subject: 我今天在家 or 今天我在家. There is no past tense: 昨天我在家 “I was at home yesterday”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 明天 我 去 教会。 | Míng tiān wǒ qù jiào huì . | I'm going to church tomorrow. |
| 昨天 我 在 家。 | Zuó tiān wǒ zài jiā . | I was at home yesterday. |
