# 第十六课：认识你很高兴 | Dì shí liù kè : rèn shi nǐ hěn gāo xìng | HSK 1 · Lesson 16: Nice to meet you

> **Goal:** Meet new people politely: introduce people, say “nice to meet you”, ask with 请问, invite people to sit, and use 先生.

## 语法：认识 | Yǔ fǎ : rèn shi | Grammar: 认识你很高兴: nice to meet you

> **认识** (rèn shi) means to know a person, or to get to know them. **认识你很高兴** (or 很高兴认识你) is “Nice to meet you”; answer 认识你我也很高兴 “nice to meet you too”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 认识 你 很 高兴！ | Rèn shi nǐ hěn gāo xìng ! | Nice to meet you! |

## 语法：请 | Yǔ fǎ : qǐng | Grammar: 请: please, and inviting

> **请** + verb is a polite “please”: 请坐, 请喝茶. **请问** “may I ask” opens a question to someone you don't know.

> 请 + person + verb means “invite someone to” or “treat someone to”: 我请你吃饭 “I'll treat you to a meal”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 请问， 教会 在 哪儿？ | Qǐng wèn , jiào huì zài nǎ r ? | Excuse me, where's the church? |
| 我 请 你 吃饭。 | Wǒ qǐng nǐ chī fàn . | I'll treat you to a meal. |

## 语法：见 | Yǔ fǎ : jiàn | Grammar: 见 and 见面

> **见** (jiàn) is to see someone: 明天见 “see you tomorrow”, 教会见 “see you at church”.

> **见面** is “meet up”. It already contains its object (面 “face”), so say 和你见面 “meet with you”, not 见面你.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 很 高兴 和 你 见面。 | Hěn gāo xìng hé nǐ jiàn miàn . | I'm glad to meet you. |

## 语法：先生 | Yǔ fǎ : xiān sheng | Grammar: 先生: Mr., and husband

> Family name + **先生** is “Mr.”: 王先生. Titles always come after the name. 我先生 / 她先生 means “my / her husband”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 王 先生， 你 好！ | Wáng xiān sheng , nǐ hǎo ! | Hello, Mr. Wang! |
