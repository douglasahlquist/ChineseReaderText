## 语法：数字 | Yǔ fǎ : shù zì | Grammar: Counting from 1 to 99

> The numbers 1–10 build all the others: **十一** 11 (10 + 1), **二十** 20 (2 × 10), **二十八** 28 (2 × 10 + 8), **九十九** 99.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 十二。 | Shí èr . | Twelve. |
| 三十。 | Sān shí . | Thirty. |
| 四十五。 | Sì shí wǔ . | Forty-five. |

## 语法：一 | Yǔ fǎ : yī | Grammar: The tone of 一

> Counting or at the end of a number, **一** is said yī: 一、二、三, 十一. In front of other words its tone changes (yí / yì); you'll see this from lesson 6 on, and the pinyin always shows it.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一、 二、 三。 | Yī , èr , sān . | One, two, three. |

## 语法：大数字 | Yǔ fǎ : dà shù zì | Grammar: Bigger numbers: coming in lesson 6

> This lesson counts up to 99. **Lesson 6** goes on to the bigger numbers: 百 (100), 千 (1,000), 万 (10,000) and up to 亿 (100,000,000) and beyond, with 零 for the gaps, as in 一百零五 (105).

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 十、 二十、 三十、 四十、 五十。 | Shí , èr shí , sān shí , sì shí , wǔ shí . | Ten, twenty, thirty, forty, fifty. |

## 语法：电话 | Yǔ fǎ : diàn huà | Grammar: Phone numbers

> Phone numbers are read digit by digit: 八二七五 = 8-2-7-5. (In phone numbers 一 is often said yāo; these lessons keep yī.)

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 的 电话 是 八 二 七 五。 | Wǒ de diàn huà shì bā èr qī wǔ . | My number is 8275. |
