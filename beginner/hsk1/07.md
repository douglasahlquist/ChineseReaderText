## 语法：大小 | Yǔ fǎ : dà xiǎo | Grammar: 大 and 小 for size and age

> **大** (dà) “big” and **小** (xiǎo) “small” also describe age in a family: 大儿子 “oldest son”, 小女儿 “youngest daughter”, 他大 “he's older”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 是 我 的 大 儿子。 | Zhè shì wǒ de dà ér zi . | This is my oldest son. |
| 小明 是 我 的 小 儿子。 | Xiǎo míng shì wǒ de xiǎo ér zi . | Xiaoming is my youngest son. |

## 语法：爷爷奶奶 | Yǔ fǎ : yé ye nǎi nai | Grammar: Grandparents

> **爷爷** and **奶奶** are your father's parents. (Your mother's parents have other names, 姥爷 and 姥姥 or 外公 and 外婆, which come later.)

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 爷爷 奶奶 都 很 好。 | Yé ye nǎi nai dōu hěn hǎo . | Grandpa and Grandma are both well. |

## 语法：想 | Yǔ fǎ : xiǎng | Grammar: 想: to miss, to think

> **想** (xiǎng) with a person or a place means “to miss”: 我想妈妈 “I miss Mom”; 我想家 “I'm homesick”. On its own it means “to think”: 你想什么？ (In lesson 8 you'll meet 想 + verb: “want to”.)

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 很 想 我 的 家人。 | Wǒ hěn xiǎng wǒ de jiā rén . | I miss my family very much. |

## 语法：小朋友 | Yǔ fǎ : xiǎo péng you | Grammar: Talking to children

> Adults call a child they don't know **小朋友** “little friend”: 小朋友，你几岁？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 小朋友， 你 叫 什么 名字？ | Xiǎo péng you , nǐ jiào shén me míng zi ? | What's your name, little one? |
