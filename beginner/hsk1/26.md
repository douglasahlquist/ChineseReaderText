## 语法：坐飞机 | Yǔ fǎ : zuò fēi jī | Grammar: How you travel goes before the verb

> Ride a vehicle with **坐**: 坐飞机, 坐火车, 坐车. On foot is **走路**. The way you travel comes before 去 or 来: 我坐火车去 “I'm going by train”, 我走路去学校 “I walk to school”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 坐 火车 去。 | Wǒ men zuò huǒ chē qù . | We're going by train. |
| 我 走路 去 学校。 | Wǒ zǒu lù qù xué xiào . | I walk to school. |

## 语法：远 | Yǔ fǎ : yuǎn | Grammar: 远不远？ Asking with “A not A”

> Instead of 吗 you can ask yes-or-no by saying the word, then 不, then the word again: 远不远？ “Is it far?”, 去不去？ “Are you going?”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 学校 远 不 远？ | Xué xiào yuǎn bù yuǎn ? | Is the school far? |

## 语法：票 | Yǔ fǎ : piào | Grammar: 票: tickets

> **票** is a ticket. It combines with what it's for: 车票 (bus or train), 机票 (plane), 电影票 (movie), 火车票 (train).

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 火车 票 多少 钱？ | Huǒ chē piào duō shǎo qián ? | How much is a train ticket? |
