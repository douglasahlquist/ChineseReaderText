## 语法：穿 | Yǔ fǎ : chuān | Grammar: 穿: wear, and put on

> **穿** is both “wear” and “put on” for clothes: 你穿什么？ 穿好了 means “dressed and ready”. 多穿一些 is “wear more (layers)”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天 很 冷， 多 穿 一些。 | Jīn tiān hěn lěng , duō chuān yì xiē . | It's cold today, wear more. |

## 语法：坏了 | Yǔ fǎ : huài le | Grammar: 坏了: broken or gone bad

> **坏了** says something has broken or spoiled: 手机坏了, 鸡蛋坏了. The 了 shows it is a change. Before a noun, 坏 means “bad”: 坏人.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 的 手机 坏 了。 | Wǒ de shǒu jī huài le . | My phone is broken. |

## 语法：口 | Yǔ fǎ : kǒu | Grammar: 口: mouth, and a measure word

> **口** is “mouth”. As a measure word it counts mouthfuls (一口水, 吃一口) and the members of a family: 我家有五口人.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 家 有 几 口 人？ | Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén ? | How many people are in your family? |

## 语法：身体 | Yǔ fǎ : shēn tǐ | Grammar: 身体好吗？ Asking after someone's health

> **身体好吗？** literally “is your body well?” is the polite way to ask after someone's health, especially an older person's. Answer 很好 or 不太好.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 奶奶 身体 好 吗？ | Nǎi nai shēn tǐ hǎo ma ? | How is Grandma's health? |
