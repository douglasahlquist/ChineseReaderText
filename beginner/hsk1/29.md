## 语法：上里 | Yǔ fǎ : shàng lǐ | Grammar: Noun + 上 / 里: on and in

> Where English puts a word in front (“on the table”, “in the bag”), Chinese puts **上** or **里** after the noun: 桌子上, 书包里. With 在: 手机在书包里 “the phone is in the bag”.

> 里边, 外边, 上边, 下边 are the fuller forms and can also stand alone: 他在里边.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 手机 在 书包 里。 | Shǒu jī zài shū bāo lǐ . | The phone is in the schoolbag. |
| 你 的 书 在 桌子 上。 | Nǐ de shū zài zhuō zi shàng . | Your book is on the desk. |

## 语法：上下 | Yǔ fǎ : shàng xià | Grammar: 上 and 下: last and next

> Before 星期 or 个月, **上** means “last” and **下** “next”: 上星期 “last week”, 下个月 “next month”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 上 星期 我 去 了 机场。 | Shàng xīng qī wǒ qù le jī chǎng . | Last week I went to the airport. |
| 下 个 月 安娜 来。 | Xià gè yuè Ān nà lái . | Anna is coming next month. |

## 语法：楼 | Yǔ fǎ : lóu | Grammar: 楼: buildings and floors

> **楼** is a building, and number + 楼 is a floor: 三楼 “the third floor”. **楼上** and **楼下** are “upstairs” and “downstairs”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 家 在 三 楼。 | Wǒ jiā zài sān lóu . | My home is on the third floor. |
