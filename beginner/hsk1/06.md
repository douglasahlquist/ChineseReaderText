## 语法：个 | Yǔ fǎ : gè | Grammar: Number + measure word + noun

> Between a number and a noun Chinese needs a **measure word**. **个** (gè) is the most common one: 一个人 “one person”, 三个朋友 “three friends”. 这个 / 那个 mean “this one / that one”.

> 一 before 个 is said yí: 一个 yí gè.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一 个 朋友 | Yí gè péng you | a friend |
| 这 个 孩子 | Zhè gè hái zi | this child |

## 语法：两 | Yǔ fǎ : liǎng | Grammar: 两 or 二?

> For “two of something” use **两** (liǎng): 两个人. Use 二 when counting or in bigger numbers: 一、二、三; 十二; 二十.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 两 个 人 | Liǎng gè rén | two people |
| 十二 个 人 | Shí èr gè rén | twelve people |

## 语法：几 | Yǔ fǎ : jǐ | Grammar: 几 and 多少: how many?

> **几** (jǐ) asks about small numbers you expect to be under ten, and needs a measure word: 几个人？ **多少** (duō shao) asks about any amount, and the measure word is optional: 多少人？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 有 几 个 哥哥？ | Nǐ yǒu jǐ gè gē ge ? | How many older brothers do you have? |
| 教会 有 多少 人？ | Jiào huì yǒu duō shǎo rén ? | How many people does the church have? |

## 语法：岁 | Yǔ fǎ : suì | Grammar: Ages with 岁

> Give an age with a number + **岁** (suì); no 是 is needed: 我十岁. Ask a child 你几岁？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 妹妹 五 岁。 | Wǒ mèi mei wǔ suì . | My younger sister is five. |
