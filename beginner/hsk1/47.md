## 语法：比 | Yǔ fǎ : bǐ | Grammar: A 比 B + adjective: comparing

> **A 比 B** + adjective says A is more so than B: 哥哥比我高 “my brother is taller than me”. Don't add 很: not 哥哥比我很高.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天 比 昨天 冷。 | Jīn tiān bǐ zuó tiān lěng . | Today is colder than yesterday. |

## 语法：最 | Yǔ fǎ : zuì | Grammar: 最: the most

> **最** before an adjective or verb means “the most”: 最大 “the biggest”, 我最喜欢唱歌 “I like singing best”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 哪 个 最 大？ | Nǎ gè zuì dà ? | Which is the biggest? |

## 语法：第 | Yǔ fǎ : dì | Grammar: 第 + number: first, second …

> **第** before a number makes it an order: 第一 “first”, 第二 “second”, 第三课 “lesson three”. Ask 第几？ “which number?”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 是 第 一 个！ | Wǒ shì dì yī gè ! | I'm first! |
| 第二 天。 | Dì èr tiān . | The next day. |

## 语法：毛半 | Yǔ fǎ : máo bàn | Grammar: Money and halves: 块, 毛, 半

> One 块 is ten **毛**: 一块五毛 “one yuan fifty”, often shortened to 一块五. **半** is “half”: 三点半 “half past three”, 半个小时 “half an hour”, 一个半小时 “an hour and a half”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一 块 五 毛。 | Yí kuài wǔ máo . | One yuan fifty. |
| 现在 七 点 半。 | Xiàn zài qī diǎn bàn . | It's half past seven. |

## 语法：一点儿 | Yǔ fǎ : yì diǎn r | Grammar: 一点儿: a little

> **一点儿** “a little” goes before a noun (喝一点儿水) or after an adjective (大了一点儿 “a bit big”). **一点儿也不** means “not at all”: 一点儿也不难.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一点儿 也 不 难。 | Yì diǎn r yě bù nán . | It's not hard at all. |
