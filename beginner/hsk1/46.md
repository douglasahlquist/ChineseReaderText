## 语法：这些 | Yǔ fǎ : zhè xiē | Grammar: 这些, 那些 and 哪些

> **这些** “these”, **那些** “those” and **哪些** “which (ones)” are the plurals of 这, 那 and 哪. They need no measure word: 这些人, 哪些字.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这些 是 我 的 书。 | Zhè xiē shì wǒ de shū . | These are my books. |
| 你 要 哪些？ | Nǐ yào nǎ xiē ? | Which ones do you want? |

## 语法：哪 | Yǔ fǎ : nǎ | Grammar: 哪 + measure word: which

> **哪** (nǎ) “which” goes before a measure word or a word like 天 or 国: 哪个？ 哪天？ 你是哪国人？ “Which country are you from?”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 要 哪 个？ | Nǐ yào nǎ gè ? | Which one do you want? |
| 你 是 哪 国 人？ | Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén ? | Which country are you from? |

## 语法：这边 | Yǔ fǎ : zhè bian | Grammar: 这边 and 那边

> **这边** “this side, over here” and **那边** “that side, over there” work like 这儿 and 那儿. 这边请！ is a polite “this way, please!”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这边 请！ | Zhè bian qǐng ! | This way, please! |

## 语法：子 | Yǔ fǎ : zi | Grammar: 子 at the end of nouns

> Many nouns end in **子**, usually toneless: 杯子 bēi zi, 桌子, 本子, 包子, 孩子, 房子. On its own the character is read zǐ.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 杯子 的 子。 | Bēi zi de zi . | The 子 in 杯子. |

## 对话一 | Duì huà yī | Dialog 1 · Buying flowers

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| Yue：我 想 给 奶奶 买 花。 | Wǒ xiǎng gěi nǎi nai mǎi huā . | I want to buy Grandma some flowers. |
| Shop：你 要 哪些？ | Nǐ yào nǎ xiē ? | Which ones would you like? |
| Yue：这些 白 花 多少 钱？ | Zhè xiē bái huā duō shǎo qián ? | How much are these white flowers? |

| Yue：好， 我 要 这些。 | Hǎo , wǒ yào zhè xiē . | OK, I'll take these. |
