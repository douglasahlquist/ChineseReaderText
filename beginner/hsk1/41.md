## 语法：觉得 | Yǔ fǎ : jué de | Grammar: 觉得: think, feel

> **觉得** gives an opinion or a feeling: 我觉得汉语很好玩儿 “I think Chinese is fun”, 我不觉得冷 “I don't feel cold”. Ask someone's view with 你觉得呢？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 觉得 呢？ | Nǐ jué de ne ? | What do you think? |

## 语法：最好 | Yǔ fǎ : zuì hǎo | Grammar: 最好: had better, best

> **最好** before a verb is advice, “you'd better”: 你最好去看病. Before a noun with 的 it is “best”: 我最好的朋友 “my best friend”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 最好 去 看病。 | Nǐ zuì hǎo qù kàn bìng . | You'd better see a doctor. |

## 语法：难 | Yǔ fǎ : nán | Grammar: 难 + verb: hard to

> **难** before a verb means “hard to …”, the opposite of 好 + verb: 难写 “hard to write”, compare 好看 “good to look at”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 字 很 难 写。 | Zhè gè zì hěn nán xiě . | This character is hard to write. |

## 语法：差 | Yǔ fǎ : chà | Grammar: 差: poor, and short of

> **差** (chà) describes something poor: 他的汉语很差. It also means “short of”: 还差十块钱 “still ten yuan short”, 差五分十点 “five (minutes) short of ten”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 差 五 分 十 点。 | Chà wǔ fēn shí diǎn . | Five to ten. |
