# HSK 1 介绍 | HSK yī jiè shào | About HSK 1

> **Draft** — the lessons are not yet reviewed by a native speaker.

## 目标 | Mù biāo | The goal

> HSK 1 is the first level of the HSK 3.0 standard (2021). This course teaches all **506 HSK 1 words** in **52 lessons** of about ten new words each, in an order that lets every sentence use only what you have already learned.

> By the end you can talk simply about yourself, your family and friends; count, give your age and phone number; say dates and the time; say where people are and where they are going; eat, drink and shop; get around town; talk about the weather, health, work and school; and follow short everyday conversations at home, at church and in town.

## 每课 | Měi kè | How each lesson works

> **Goal and new words.** Each lesson starts with what it sets out to teach and its word list, with Hanzi, pinyin and English all shown.

> **Sentences that build up.** Each new word then gets about ten sentences, from the word on its own to full sentences, using only words already taught. Only the Hanzi show: **tap** a word to see its pinyin and hear it, **double-tap** a line for its English.

> **Grammar notes** explain the lesson's new patterns, with examples you can tap.

> **Dialogs** close each lesson: short conversations that use everything learned so far.

> **补充 Extra** words are not on the HSK 1 list but are needed for the settings, such as 教会 (church). They are marked wherever they appear.
