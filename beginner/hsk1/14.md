## 语法：多少 | Yǔ fǎ : duō shǎo | Grammar: 很多 and 很少

> **多** “many” and **少** “few” almost always take 很 when they describe: 人很多 “there are lots of people”. Before a noun, use 很多: 很多人. (很少 before a noun is unusual; say 人很少.)

> On their own before a verb they mean “more” and “less”: 多吃菜, 少喝茶.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 商场 人 很 多。 | Shāng chǎng rén hěn duō . | The mall is crowded. |
| 多 吃 菜！ | Duō chī cài ! | Eat more vegetables! |

## 语法：太 | Yǔ fǎ : tài | Grammar: 太……了: too, or really

> **太** + adjective + **了** means “too …”: 太贵了 “too expensive”. With good things it's just enthusiasm: 太好了！ “Great!”

> **不太** softens a negative: 不太贵 “not very expensive”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 太 贵 了！ | Tài guì le ! | Too expensive! |
| 不 太 好。 | Bú tài hǎo . | Not very good. |

## 语法：还再 | Yǔ fǎ : hái zài | Grammar: 还 and 再

> **还** (hái) means “still”, “also” or “more” about the present: 还有吗？ “Is there any more?”, 他还在家 “he's still at home”. 还没有 = “not yet”.

> **再** (zài) means “again, once more” about something that hasn't happened yet: 明天再来 “come again tomorrow”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 还 有 吗？ | Hái yǒu ma ? | Is there any more? |
| 明天 再 来。 | Míng tiān zài lái . | Come again tomorrow. |

## 语法：一些 | Yǔ fǎ : yì xiē | Grammar: 一些 and 有些: some

> **一些** “some, a few” goes before a noun, usually after the verb: 买一些水果. **有些** “some (of them)” usually starts the sentence, setting some apart from the rest: 有些人不吃肉.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 买 了 一些 东西。 | Wǒ mǎi le yì xiē dōng xi . | I bought a few things. |
| 有些 人 不 吃 肉。 | Yǒu xiē rén bù chī ròu . | Some people don't eat meat. |
