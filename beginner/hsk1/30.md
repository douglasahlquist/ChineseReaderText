## 语法：天气 | Yǔ fǎ : tiān qì | Grammar: Talking about the weather

> Describe the weather with an adjective, usually with 很: 天气很好, 今天很冷. **下雨** is “to rain”; 雨很大 “it's raining hard”, 风很大 “it's very windy”. 下雨了 “it's started raining”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天 天气 很 好。 | Jīn tiān tiān qì hěn hǎo . | The weather's nice today. |
| 下雨 了！ | Xià yǔ le ! | It's raining! |

## 语法：常常 | Yǔ fǎ : cháng cháng | Grammar: 常 and 常常: often

> **常** and **常常** “often” go before the verb: 我常常去教会. The negative is usually 不常: 我不常看电视.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 常常 去 商场。 | Wǒ cháng cháng qù shāng chǎng . | I often go to the mall. |
| 我 不 常 看 电视。 | Wǒ bù cháng kàn diàn shì . | I don't watch TV much. |

## 语法：有时候 | Yǔ fǎ : yǒu shí hou | Grammar: 有时 and 有时候: sometimes

> **有时候** (or the shorter **有时**) goes at the start or before the verb, and is often used in pairs: 有时候很冷，有时候很热.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 有时 走路， 有时 坐 车。 | Wǒ yǒu shí zǒu lù , yǒu shí zuò chē . | Sometimes I walk and sometimes I take the bus. |

## 对话一 | Duì huà yī | Dialog 1 · It's raining

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| Xiao：妈妈， 外边 下雨 了！ | Mā ma , wài bian xià yǔ le ! | Mom, it's raining outside! |
| Mama：雨 大 吗？ | Yǔ dà ma ? | Is it raining hard? |
| Xiao：很 大， 风 也 很 大。 | Hěn dà , fēng yě hěn dà . | Very, and it's really windy too. |
