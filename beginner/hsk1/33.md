## 语法：上班 | Yǔ fǎ : shàng bān | Grammar: 上班, 下班 and 班

> **上班** is to go to work or be at work; **下班** is to finish work. Like 上课 and 下课. **班** on its own is a class (a group of students) or a shift: 我们班.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 爸爸 八 点 上班。 | Bà ba bā diǎn shàng bān . | Dad starts work at eight. |
| 我们 班 有 三十 个 学生。 | Wǒ men bān yǒu sān shí gè xué sheng . | Our class has thirty students. |

## 语法：做 | Yǔ fǎ : zuò | Grammar: 做: do and make

> **做** is “do” or “make”: 你在做什么？ “What are you doing?”, 做饭 “cook”, 做包子 “make buns”. 你做什么工作？ asks what someone does for a living.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 在 做 什么？ | Nǐ zài zuò shén me ? | What are you doing? |
| 我 在 做 饭。 | Wǒ zài zuò fàn . | I'm cooking. |

## 语法：事 | Yǔ fǎ : shì | Grammar: 有事 and 没事儿

> **有事** means “to have something on” or “something's up”: 我有事 “I'm busy”, 有事吗？ “What's up?” **没事儿** means “it's nothing”, “no problem”, or “free”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 有 事 吗？ | Yǒu shì ma ? | What's up? |
| 没事儿。 | Méi shì r . | It's nothing. |
