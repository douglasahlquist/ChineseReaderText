## 语法：前天 | Yǔ fǎ : qián tiān | Grammar: From 前天 to 后天

> The days around today: **前天** “the day before yesterday”, 昨天, 今天, 明天, **后天** “the day after tomorrow”. Like other time words they go before the verb.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 前天 下雨 了。 | Qián tiān xià yǔ le . | It rained the day before yesterday. |
| 后天 我们 去 北京。 | Hòu tiān wǒ men qù Běi jīng . | We're going to Beijing the day after tomorrow. |

## 语法：次 | Yǔ fǎ : cì | Grammar: 次: times

> Number + **次** counts how many times, after the verb: 我去了三次北京. For “how often”, put the period first: 一个星期一次 “once a week”. 上次 and 下次 are “last time” and “next time”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 去 了 三 次 北京。 | Wǒ qù le sān cì Běi jīng . | I've been to Beijing three times. |
| 一 个 星期 一 次。 | Yí gè xīng qī yī cì . | Once a week. |

## 语法：一会儿 | Yǔ fǎ : yí huì r | Grammar: 一会儿 and 半天: how long

> **一会儿** is “a little while”, **半天** “half a day”, or in everyday talk “ages”. Both go after the verb: 休息一会儿, 等了半天. 一会儿 also comes first meaning “in a moment”: 一会儿见！

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 等 了 半天！ | Wǒ děng le bàn tiān ! | I waited for ages! |
| 我 休息 一会儿。 | Wǒ xiū xi yí huì r . | I'll rest for a bit. |

## 语法：放假 | Yǔ fǎ : fàng jià | Grammar: 放假 and 请假

> **放假** is when everyone has a holiday (school or work closes). **请假** is asking for time off yourself: 我明天请假.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 学校 什么 时候 放假？ | Xué xiào shén me shí hou fàng jià ? | When do school holidays start? |
| 我 明天 请假。 | Wǒ míng tiān qǐng jià . | I'm taking tomorrow off. |
