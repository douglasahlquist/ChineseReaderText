## 语法：怎么 | Yǔ fǎ : zěn me | Grammar: 怎么: how, and how come

> **怎么** + verb asks “how”: 这个字怎么读？ “How do you read this character?”

> **怎么** + 不 asks “how come / why not”: 小明怎么不吃饭？ And **怎么了？** means “what's wrong?”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 怎么 去 教会？ | Wǒ men zěn me qù jiào huì ? | How do we get to church? |
| 你 怎么 了？ | Nǐ zěn me le ? | What's wrong? |

## 语法：坐 | Yǔ fǎ : zuò | Grammar: 坐, 开 and 打: getting around

> You ride in a vehicle with **坐** “sit”: 坐车. You drive with **开**: 开车. **打车** is to take a taxi.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 坐 车 去。 | Wǒ men zuò chē qù . | We'll go by bus. |
| 爸爸 开车。 | Bà ba kāi chē . | Dad drives. |

## 语法：车上 | Yǔ fǎ : chē shàng | Grammar: 上车, 下车 and 车上

> **上车** is to get on or into a vehicle, **下车** to get off or out. **车上** “on the vehicle” is a place: 车上有很多人.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 在 这儿 下车。 | Wǒ zài zhè r xià chē . | I'm getting off here. |
| 车上 有 很 多 人。 | Chē shàng yǒu hěn duō rén . | There are lots of people on the bus. |
