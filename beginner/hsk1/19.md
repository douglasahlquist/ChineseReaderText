## 语法：上课 | Yǔ fǎ : shàng kè | Grammar: 上 and 下: starting and finishing

> **上课** is to start class or be in class, **下课** is to finish class. In the same way **上学** is to go to school, and **放学** is when school lets out.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 八 点 上课。 | Bā diǎn shàng kè . | Class starts at eight. |
| 下午 四 点 放学。 | Xià wǔ sì diǎn fàng xué . | School finishes at four. |

## 语法：教 | Yǔ fǎ : jiāo | Grammar: 教 + person + what

> **教** (jiāo) “teach” can take two objects, the person and then what is taught: 妈妈教我中文 “Mom teaches me Chinese”. (In 教会 the same character is read jiào.)

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 教 小明 汉字。 | Wǒ jiāo Xiǎo míng Hàn zì . | I teach Xiaoming characters. |

## 语法：的 | Yǔ fǎ : de | Grammar: Verb + 的 describing a noun

> A verb phrase + **的** can describe a noun, like an English “that …” clause: 你写的字 “the characters (that) you write”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 写 的 字 很 好。 | Nǐ xiě de zì hěn hǎo . | Your characters look nice. |
