## 语法：一样 | Yǔ fǎ : yí yàng | Grammar: A 和 B 一样: the same

> **A 和 B 一样** means “A and B are the same”; **不一样** is “different”. Add an adjective for “as … as”: 你和你哥哥一样大吗？ “Are you as old as your brother?”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 两 个 字 不 一样。 | Zhè liǎng gè zì bù yí yàng . | These two characters are different. |

## 语法：先 | Yǔ fǎ : xiān | Grammar: 先……再……: first … then …

> **先** “first” and **再** “then” order two actions: 先洗手，再吃饭 “wash your hands first, then eat”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 先 去 书店， 再 去 饭店。 | Wǒ men xiān qù shū diàn , zài qù fàn diàn . | Let's go to the bookshop first, then the restaurant. |

## 语法：最后 | Yǔ fǎ : zuì hòu | Grammar: 最后 and 还有

> **最后** is “last” or “finally”: 最后一个 “the last one”, 最后，我们唱歌 “finally, we sing”. **还有** is “there's still …” or, at the start, “and also …”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 是 最后 一 个 包子。 | Zhè shì zuì hòu yí gè bāo zi . | This is the last bun. |
| 还有， 别 忘记 你 的 书。 | Hái yǒu , bié wàng jì nǐ de shū . | And also, don't forget your book. |

## 语法：好听 | Yǔ fǎ : hǎo tīng | Grammar: 好听: lovely to hear

> **好听** is “nice to listen to”, for songs, names and voices; it joins 好看, 好吃 and 好玩儿.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 歌 很 好听。 | Zhè gè gē hěn hǎo tīng . | This song is lovely. |

## 对话一 | Duì huà yī | Dialog 1 · A new classmate

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
