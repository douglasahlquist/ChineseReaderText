## 语法：过 | Yǔ fǎ : guo | Grammar: Verb + 过: have ever done

> **过** after a verb says something has happened at some time in your life: 我去过北京 “I've been to Beijing”. The negative is 没 (or 没有) + verb + 过: 我没去过中国 “I've never been to China”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 去 过 北京 吗？ | Nǐ qù guo Běi jīng ma ? | Have you ever been to Beijing? |
| 我 没 去 过 中国。 | Wǒ méi qù guo Zhōng guó . | I've never been to China. |

## 语法：正在 | Yǔ fǎ : zhèng zài | Grammar: 正在 and 在: right now

> **正在** before a verb stresses that something is happening right now: 我正在吃饭. It's a stronger form of the 在 you met in lesson 18.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 外边 正在 下雨。 | Wài bian zhèng zài xià yǔ . | It's raining outside right now. |

## 语法：着 | Yǔ fǎ : zhe | Grammar: Verb + 着: a lasting state

> **着** after a verb describes a state that continues: 门开着 “the door is open”, 他站着 “he's standing”. Verb + 着 + another verb says how something is done: 笑着说 “say with a smile”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 门 开 着。 | Mén kāi zhe . | The door is open. |
| 他 笑 着 说。 | Tā xiào zhe shuō . | He said with a smile. |

## 语法：从 | Yǔ fǎ : cóng | Grammar: 从……到……: from … to …

> **从** A **到** B means “from A to B”, for places or times: 从我家到教会, 从八点到十点.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 从 八 点 到 十 点 上课。 | Cóng bā diǎn dào shí diǎn shàng kè . | Class is from eight to ten. |
