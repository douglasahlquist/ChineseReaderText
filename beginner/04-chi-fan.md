# 第4课：吃饭 | Dì 4 kè : chī fàn | Lesson 4: Meals

## 第一组：吃早饭 | Dì yī zǔ : chī zǎo fàn | Group 1: Eating breakfast

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| A：你早饭吃了吗？ | Nǐ zǎo fàn chī le ma ? | Have you eaten breakfast? |
| B：吃了。 | Chī le . | Yes, I have. |
| A：你吃了什么？ | Nǐ chī le shén me ? | What did you eat? |
| B：我吃了鸡蛋和面包。 | Wǒ chī le jī dàn hé miàn bāo . | I ate eggs and bread. |
| A：你喝牛奶吗？ | Nǐ hē niú nǎi ma ? | Do you drink milk? |
| B：喝，我喜欢喝牛奶。 | Hē , wǒ xǐ huan hē niú nǎi . | Yes, I like drinking milk. |
| A：你喜欢吃包子吗？ | Nǐ xǐ huan chī bāo zi ma ? | Do you like steamed buns? |
| B：喜欢。 | Xǐ huan . | Yes, I do. |

## 第二组：吃午饭 | Dì èr zǔ : chī wǔ fàn | Group 2: Eating lunch

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| A：你饿了吗？ | Nǐ è le ma ? | Are you hungry? |
| B：我饿了。 | Wǒ è le . | I'm hungry. |
| A：你想吃什么？ | Nǐ xiǎng chī shén me ? | What do you want to eat? |
| B：我想吃汉堡。 | Wǒ xiǎng chī hàn bǎo . | I want a hamburger. |
| A：你想喝什么？ | Nǐ xiǎng hē shén me ? | What do you want to drink? |
| B：我想喝果汁。 | Wǒ xiǎng hē guǒ zhī . | I want juice. |
| A：你喜欢吃薯条吗？ | Nǐ xǐ huan chī shǔ tiáo ma ? | Do you like French fries? |
| B：喜欢，我很喜欢！ | Xǐ huan , wǒ hěn xǐ huan ! | Yes, I really like them! |

## 第三组：吃晚饭 | Dì sān zǔ : chī wǎn fàn | Group 3: Eating dinner

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| A：你晚饭吃什么？ | Nǐ wǎn fàn chī shén me ? | What are you having for dinner? |
| B：我吃米饭和牛肉。 | Wǒ chī mǐ fàn hé niú ròu . | I'm having rice and beef. |
| A：你喜欢吃鱼吗？ | Nǐ xǐ huan chī yú ma ? | Do you like fish? |
| B：喜欢。 | Xǐ huan . | Yes, I do. |
| A：你喜欢吃蔬菜吗？ | Nǐ xǐ huan chī shū cài ma ? | Do you like vegetables? |
| B：喜欢。 | Xǐ huan . | Yes, I do. |
| A：你吃饱了吗？ | Nǐ chī bǎo le ma ? | Are you full? |
| B：吃饱了，谢谢！ | Chī bǎo le , xiè xie ! | I'm full, thank you! |
