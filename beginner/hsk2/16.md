## 语法：打算 | Yǔ fǎ : dǎ suàn | Grammar: 打算 and 计划 + verb

> **打算** and **计划** both go before a verb to say what you intend: 我打算去中国旅游, 我们计划明年出国. Both are also nouns: 你有什么打算？ “What are your plans?”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 打算 去 哪儿？ | Nǐ dǎ suàn qù nǎ r ? | Where are you planning to go? |
| 这 个 计划 很 好。 | Zhè gè jì huà hěn hǎo . | This is a good plan. |

## 语法：送到 | Yǔ fǎ : sòng dào | Grammar: 送到: deliver to, see off to

> **送** + **到** + place: take something or someone all the way there. 我送你到机场 “I'll take you to the airport.”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|

## 语法：旅游 | Yǔ fǎ : lǚ yóu | Grammar: 旅行, 旅游, 旅客 and 游客

> **旅行** is travelling; **旅游** is touring as a holiday. A **旅客** is a traveller or passenger; a **游客** is a sightseer.
