## 语法：愿意 | Yǔ fǎ : yuàn yì | Grammar: 愿意 + verb: willing to

> **愿意** goes before a verb: 你愿意帮助我吗？ “Are you willing to help me?” 我愿意 on its own is also “I do” at a wedding.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 愿意 帮助 我 吗？ | Nǐ yuàn yì bāng zhù wǒ ma ? | Are you willing to help me? |

## 语法：对 | Yǔ fǎ : duì | Grammar: 对 + person: toward someone

> **对** “toward” + person + adjective: 中国人对我很友好 “Chinese people are friendly to me”, 我对考试有信心.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 中国 人 对 我 很 友好。 | Zhōng guó rén duì wǒ hěn yǒu hǎo . | Chinese people are friendly to me. |

## 语法：要求 | Yǔ fǎ : yāo qiú | Grammar: 要求 and 请求

> **要求** is to require or demand; **请求** is a polite request. 答应 is to agree to one; 接受 is to accept something offered.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 老师 的 要求 很 高。 | Lǎo shī de yāo qiú hěn gāo . | The teacher's standards are high. |
| 我 有 一 个 请求。 | Wǒ yǒu yí gè qǐng qiú . | I have a request. |

## 语法：不错 | Yǔ fǎ : bú cuò | Grammar: 不错: pretty good

> **不错** “not wrong” is warm praise: “pretty good, nice”. 很不错 is stronger.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 的 汉语 很 不错！ | Nǐ de Hàn yǔ hěn bú cuò ! | Your Chinese is really good! |
