## 语法：好处 | Yǔ fǎ : hǎo chu | Grammar: 好处 and 坏处

> **好处** “good point” and **坏处** “bad point” are benefits and harms: 运动有很多好处.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 运动 有 很 多 好处。 | Yùn dòng yǒu hěn duō hǎo chu . | Exercise has many benefits. |

## 语法：一般 | Yǔ fǎ : yì bān | Grammar: 一般: usually; so-so

> Before a verb, **一般** means “usually”: 我一般七点起床. As an adjective it is “ordinary, so-so”: 这个饭馆很一般.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 一般 七 点 起床。 | Wǒ yì bān qī diǎn qǐ chuáng . | I usually get up at seven. |

## 语法：清楚 | Yǔ fǎ : qīng chu | Grammar: Verb + 清楚: clearly

> **清楚** after a verb is “clearly”: 听不清楚 “can't hear clearly”, 说清楚 “say it clearly”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 听 不 清楚。 | Wǒ tīng bù qīng chu . | I can't hear clearly. |

## 语法：正确 | Yǔ fǎ : zhèng què | Grammar: 正确 and 准确

> **正确** is “correct, right”; **准确** is “accurate, exact”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 说 的 很 正确。 | Nǐ shuō de hěn zhèng què . | What you said is right. |
