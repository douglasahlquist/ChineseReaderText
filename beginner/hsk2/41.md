## 语法：方 | Yǔ fǎ : fāng | Grammar: 东方, 西方, 南方, 北方

> **方** after a direction makes a region: **南方** “the south”, **北方** “the north”. **东方** and **西方** also mean “the East” and “the West”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 北京 在 北方。 | Běi jīng zài běi fāng . | Beijing is in the north. |

## 语法：东北 | Yǔ fǎ : dōng běi | Grammar: Compass points in Chinese order

> Chinese names the east-west direction first: **东北** “northeast”, **西北** “northwest”, **东南** “southeast”, **西南** “southwest”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 东北 很 冷。 | Dōng běi hěn lěng . | The northeast is cold. |

## 语法：以上 | Yǔ fǎ : yǐ shàng | Grammar: 以上, 以下, 以外

> After a number or place, **以上** is “above, over”, **以下** “below, under”, **以外** “outside, besides”. 以上 / 以下 alone also mean “the above / the following”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 十八 岁 以上。 | Shí bā suì yǐ shàng . | Eighteen and over. |
| 五 岁 以下 的 孩子。 | Wǔ suì yǐ xià de hái zi . | Children under five. |

## 语法：到处 | Yǔ fǎ : dào chù | Grammar: 到处都是 …

> **到处都是** + noun: “… everywhere”: 到处都是人.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 到处 都 是 人。 | Dào chù dōu shì rén . | There are people everywhere. |
