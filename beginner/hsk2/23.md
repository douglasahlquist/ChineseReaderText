## 语法：请客 | Yǔ fǎ : qǐng kè | Grammar: 请客: it's my treat

> **请客** is to treat others, usually to a meal: 今天我请客！ “It's on me today!” 请 + person + 吃饭 is to invite someone to eat.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天 我 请客！ | Jīn tiān wǒ qǐng kè ! | It's my treat today! |

## 语法：打工 | Yǔ fǎ : dǎ gōng | Grammar: 打工 and 工作

> **工作** is work in general; **打工** is working a job for wages, often part-time or away from home.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 在 饭馆 打工。 | Wǒ zài fàn guǎn dǎ gōng . | I work at a restaurant. |

## 语法：主人 | Yǔ fǎ : zhǔ rén | Grammar: 主人 and 客人

> **主人** is the host or owner; **客人** is the guest. 家里来客人了 “we have guests”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 是 主人。 | Wǒ shì zhǔ rén . | I'm the host. |

## 语法：服务 | Yǔ fǎ : fú wù | Grammar: 服务: service

> **服务** is service: 这儿的服务很好. It is also “to serve”: 我们在教会服务.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这儿 的 服务 很 好。 | Zhè r de fú wù hěn hǎo . | The service here is good. |
