## 语法：给 | Yǔ fǎ : gěi | Grammar: Verb + 给 + person

> **给** after a verb marks who receives: **送给** “give as a gift to”, **交给** “hand to”, 借给 “lend to”: 他借给我一辆自行车.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 是 送给 你 的。 | Zhè shì sòng gěi nǐ de . | This is for you. |

## 语法：借 | Yǔ fǎ : jiè | Grammar: 借: borrow or lend

> **借** is both “borrow” and “lend”; 借给 + person is “lend to”: 借我一点儿钱 “lend me some money”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 能 借 你 的 笔 吗？ | Wǒ néng jiè nǐ de bǐ ma ? | Can I borrow your pen? |

## 语法：带 | Yǔ fǎ : dài | Grammar: 带 and 带来

> **带** is to have with you or take along: 你带钱了吗？ **带来** is “bring here”: 我给你带来了礼物.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 别 忘记 带 护照。 | Bié wàng jì dài hù zhào . | Don't forget your passport. |

## 语法：拿出 | Yǔ fǎ : ná chū | Grammar: Direction words: 出, 下, 到

> Verbs take result words: **拿出** “take out”, **放下** “put down”, **拿到** “get hold of”, **留下** “leave behind”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 请 拿出 你 的 书。 | Qǐng ná chū nǐ de shū . | Please take out your books. |
