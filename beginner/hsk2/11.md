## 语法：一共 | Yǔ fǎ : yí gòng | Grammar: 一共: altogether

> **一共** “altogether” goes before the verb or the number: 一共多少钱？ “How much altogether?”, 我们一共五个人 “there are five of us”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 一共 多少 钱？ | Yí gòng duō shǎo qián ? | How much is that altogether? |

## 语法：斤 | Yǔ fǎ : jīn | Grammar: Weights: 斤, 公斤, 千克, 克

> Markets in China sell by the **斤** (jīn), which is 500 grams. A kilogram is **公斤** or **千克** (two 斤), and a gram is **克**. Prices are given “money per weight”: 十块钱一斤 “ten yuan a jin”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 鱼 多少 钱 一 斤？ | Yú duō shǎo qián yì jīn ? | How much is the fish per jin? |
| 一 公斤 是 两 斤。 | Yì gōng jīn shì liǎng jīn . | A kilogram is two jin. |

## 语法：怎么卖 | Yǔ fǎ : zěn me mài | Grammar: 怎么卖? Asking a price

> Besides 多少钱？, shoppers ask **……怎么卖？** “how is it sold?”, which invites a price per jin or per piece.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 怎么 卖？ | Zhè gè zěn me mài ? | How much is this? |

## 语法：收 | Yǔ fǎ : shōu | Grammar: 收 and 收到

> **收** is to take or accept: 这儿收信用卡吗？ “Do you take credit cards?” **收到** is to have received something: 我收到了.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这儿 收 信用卡 吗？ | Zhè r shōu xìn yòng kǎ ma ? | Do you take credit cards? |
| 我 收到 了。 | Wǒ shōu dào le . | I got it. |
