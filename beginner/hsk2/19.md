## 语法：它 | Yǔ fǎ : tā | Grammar: 它 and 它们: it and they

> **它** is “it” for animals and things; **它们** is the plural. In speech it sounds the same as 他 and 她 (tā).

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 它 很 可爱。 | Tā hěn kě ài . | It's cute. |
| 它们 是 我 的 猫。 | Tā men shì wǒ de māo . | They're my cats. |

## 语法：养 | Yǔ fǎ : yǎng | Grammar: 养: keep, raise, grow

> **养** covers keeping pets (养猫, 养狗), raising children and growing plants (养花).

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 奶奶 养 了 很 多 花。 | Nǎi nai yǎng le hěn duō huā . | Grandma grows lots of flowers. |

## 语法：叫作 | Yǔ fǎ : jiào zuò | Grammar: 叫作: be called

> **叫作** names a thing: 这叫作饺子 “this is called jiaozi”. It is more formal than 叫. The thing's name itself is its **名称**.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 鸟 叫作 什么？ | Zhè gè niǎo jiào zuò shén me ? | What is this bird called? |

## 语法：照顾 | Yǔ fǎ : zhào gu | Grammar: 照顾 and 关心

> **照顾** is to look after: 照顾孩子. **关心** is to care about, to be concerned for: 妈妈很关心我.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 谢谢 你 的 照顾。 | Xiè xie nǐ de zhào gu . | Thank you for looking after me. |
| 谢谢 你 的 关心。 | Xiè xie nǐ de guān xīn . | Thank you for your concern. |
