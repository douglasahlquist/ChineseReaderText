## 语法：让 | Yǔ fǎ : ràng | Grammar: 让 + person + verb: let, make

> **让** is “let” or “make”: 让我看看 “let me see”, 这个故事让我很感动 “this story moved me”. 不让 is “not allow”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 让 我 看 看。 | Ràng wǒ kàn kàn . | Let me see. |
| 妈妈 不 让 我 去。 | Mā ma bú ràng wǒ qù . | Mom won't let me go. |

## 语法：排队 | Yǔ fǎ : pái duì | Grammar: 排队: queue

> **排队** “line up in a row” is to queue: 我排队买票. **排** alone is a row: 前排, 后排.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 请 排队！ | Qǐng pái duì ! | Please queue! |

## 语法：通过 | Yǔ fǎ : tōng guò | Grammar: 通过: pass, through

> **通过** is to pass (an exam) and also “through, via”: 通过朋友认识的 “we met through a friend”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 通过 了 考试！ | Wǒ tōng guò le kǎo shì ! | I passed the exam! |

## 语法：来自 | Yǔ fǎ : lái zì | Grammar: 来自: come from

> **来自** is a formal “come from”: 我们来自不同的国家. In speech, 从 … 来.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 来自 哪儿？ | Nǐ lái zì nǎ r ? | Where are you from? |
