## 语法：早餐 | Yǔ fǎ : zǎo cān | Grammar: 早餐 and 早饭

> **早餐, 午餐, 晚餐** mean the same as 早饭, 午饭, 晚饭 (HSK 1), but sound a little more formal; you'll see them on menus and signs.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 早餐 吃 什么？ | Zǎo cān chī shén me ? | What's for breakfast? |

## 语法：平时 | Yǔ fǎ : píng shí | Grammar: How often: 平时, 平常, 经常, 老是

> **平时** and **平常** mean “usually, normally”. **经常** means “often”. **老是** means “always”, and usually complains: 你老是来晚 “you're always late”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 平时 七 点 起床。 | Wǒ píng shí qī diǎn qǐ chuáng . | I usually get up at seven. |
| 你 老是 来 晚。 | Nǐ lǎo shi lái wǎn . | You're always late. |

## 语法：出门 | Yǔ fǎ : chū mén | Grammar: Verb + object words: 出门, 洗澡, 做饭

> Many everyday activities are a verb with its object built in: **出门** “go out (of the door)”, **洗澡** “wash (a bath)”, **做饭** “make (a meal)”. They need no other object.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 七 点 出门。 | Wǒ qī diǎn chū mén . | I leave home at seven. |

## 语法：夜 | Yǔ fǎ : yè | Grammar: 夜, 夜里 and 半夜

> **夜** is night; **夜里** is “at night, in the night”; **半夜** is “midnight, the middle of the night”. 平安夜, “peaceful night”, is Christmas Eve.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 夜里 下雨 了。 | Yè li xià yǔ le . | It rained in the night. |
| 平安 夜。 | Píng ān yè . | Christmas Eve. |
