## 语法：没想到 | Yǔ fǎ : méi xiǎng dào | Grammar: 没想到: didn't expect

> **没想到** “didn't think of” expresses surprise: 没想到你也来了 “I didn't expect you to come too”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 没 想到 你 也 来 了。 | Méi xiǎng dào nǐ yě lái le . | I didn't expect you to come too. |

## 语法：想起 | Yǔ fǎ : xiǎng qǐ | Grammar: 想起来: remember

> **想起** is to call something to mind: 我想起来了！ “I remember now!”, 我想起了我的奶奶.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 想起 来 了！ | Wǒ xiǎng qǐ lái le ! | I remember now! |

## 语法：听说 | Yǔ fǎ : tīng shuō | Grammar: 听说: I hear that

> **听说** opens news you heard from someone: 听说你要回国了. 我听说过他 “I've heard of him”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 听说 你 要 回国 了。 | Tīng shuō nǐ yào huí guó le . | I hear you're going home. |

## 语法：走进 | Yǔ fǎ : zǒu jìn | Grammar: 走 + direction

> **走** combines with direction words: **走进** “walk in”, **走过** “walk past”, **走开** “walk away”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他 走进 了 教室。 | Tā zǒu jìn le jiào shì . | He walked into the classroom. |
