## 语法：已经 | Yǔ fǎ : yǐ jīng | Grammar: 已经 … 了: already

> **已经** goes before the verb, usually with **了** at the end: 他已经走了 “he's already left”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 已经 十 点 了。 | Yǐ jīng shí diǎn le . | It's already ten o'clock. |

## 语法：就要 | Yǔ fǎ : jiù yào | Grammar: 就要 / 快要 … 了: about to

> **就要 … 了** and **快要 … 了** say something is about to happen: 飞机就要起飞了. Put 就要 after a time word: 明天就要考试了.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 飞机 就要 起飞 了。 | Fēi jī jiù yào qǐ fēi le . | The plane is about to take off. |

## 语法：才 | Yǔ fǎ : cái | Grammar: 才 and 就: late and early

> After a time, **才** says “not until, later than expected”: 他十点才来. **就** says “already, earlier than expected”: 他八点就来了. 刚才 is “a moment ago”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他 十 点 才 来。 | Tā shí diǎn cái lái . | He didn't come until ten. |

## 语法：又 | Yǔ fǎ : yòu | Grammar: 又: again (already happened)

> **又** “again” is for repetition that has happened: 又下雨了. **又 A 又 B** is “both A and B”: 又便宜又好.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 又 下雨 了。 | Yòu xià yǔ le . | It's raining again. |
