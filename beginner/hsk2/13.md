## 语法：封 | Yǔ fǎ : fēng | Grammar: 封: the measure word for letters

> Letters are counted with **封**: 一封信, 两封信. To write to someone, put 给 + the person first: 我给奶奶写信.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 封 信 是 给 你 的。 | Zhè fēng xìn shì gěi nǐ de . | This letter is for you. |
| 我 给 奶奶 写 信。 | Wǒ gěi nǎi nai xiě xìn . | I'm writing to Grandma. |

## 语法：照相 | Yǔ fǎ : zhào xiàng | Grammar: 照相 and 照片

> **照相** is to take a photo; **照片** is the photo. To photograph someone: 给 + person + 照相: 我给你照相.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 给 你 照相。 | Wǒ gěi nǐ zhào xiàng . | I'll take your picture. |
| 这 是 我 家 的 照片。 | Zhè shì wǒ jiā de zhào piàn . | This is a photo of my family. |

## 语法：笔记本 | Yǔ fǎ : bǐ jì běn | Grammar: 笔记 and 笔记本

> **笔记** are notes; **笔记本** is a notebook. 笔记本电脑 “notebook computer” is a laptop.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 上课 的 时候 我 写 笔记。 | Shàng kè de shí hou wǒ xiě bǐ jì . | I take notes in class. |
| 笔记本 电脑。 | Bǐ jì běn diàn nǎo . | Laptop. |

## 语法：表 | Yǔ fǎ : biǎo | Grammar: 表 and 手表

> **手表** is a wristwatch, and **表** on its own also means a watch, as well as a form or a table: 时间表 “timetable”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 请 写 这 个 表。 | Qǐng xiě zhè gè biǎo . | Please fill in this form. |
