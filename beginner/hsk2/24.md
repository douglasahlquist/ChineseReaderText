## 语法：年级 | Yǔ fǎ : nián jí | Grammar: 几年级: which grade

> Grades are numbered: 一年级, 二年级. Ask with **几年级**: 你几年级？ “What grade are you in?”

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 几 年级？ | Nǐ jǐ nián jí ? | What grade are you in? |

## 语法：长 | Yǔ fǎ : cháng | Grammar: 班长, 组长, 校长

> **长** zhǎng “head” makes leaders: **班长** “class monitor”, **组长** “group leader”, **校长** “principal”. (Compare 市长, 队长 and 院长.)

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 谁 是 班长？ | Shéi shì bān zhǎng ? | Who's the class monitor? |

## 语法：组 | Yǔ fǎ : zǔ | Grammar: 组 and 小组

> **组** is a group: 三个人一组 “groups of three”. **小组** is a small group, such as a church small group.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们 三 个 人 一 组。 | Wǒ men sān gè rén yì zǔ . | We're a group of three. |

## 语法：成绩 | Yǔ fǎ : chéng jì | Grammar: 成绩 and 分数

> **成绩** is your results in general; **分数** is the number of marks.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你 的 成绩 很 好。 | Nǐ de chéng jì hěn hǎo . | Your grades are good. |
