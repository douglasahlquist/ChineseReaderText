## 语法：疼 | Yǔ fǎ : téng | Grammar: Body part + 疼: it hurts

> Say what hurts with the body part + **疼**: 我头疼 “I have a headache”, 我脚很疼 “my foot really hurts”. Ask 你哪儿疼？ “Where does it hurt?” 疼 + a person means to dote on them: 奶奶很疼小明.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 头 疼。 | Wǒ tóu téng . | I have a headache. |
| 你 哪儿 疼？ | Nǐ nǎ r téng ? | Where does it hurt? |

## 语法：舒服 | Yǔ fǎ : shū fu | Grammar: 舒服 and 不舒服

> **舒服** is “comfortable”, of a chair or the weather. **不舒服** is how you say you feel unwell: 我不舒服, 你哪儿不舒服？

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 不 舒服。 | Wǒ bù shū fu . | I don't feel well. |

## 语法：个子 | Yǔ fǎ : gè zi | Grammar: Topic and comment: 他个子很高

> Chinese often names a person, then the part being described: 他个子很高 “he — his height is tall”, 她眼睛很大 “she has big eyes”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他 个子 很 高。 | Tā gè zi hěn gāo . | He's tall. |
