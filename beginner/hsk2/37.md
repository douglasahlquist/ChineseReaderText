## 语法：推拉 | Yǔ fǎ : tuī lā | Grammar: 推 and 拉

> Doors in China are marked **推** “push” or **拉** “pull”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 推 还是 拉？ | Tuī hái shi lā ? | Push or pull? |

## 语法：碰见 | Yǔ fǎ : pèng jiàn | Grammar: 碰到 and 碰见

> **碰见** and **碰到** are “run into, meet by chance”: 我在街上碰见了小月. 碰到 can also be “touch”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 在 街 上 碰见 了 小月。 | Wǒ zài jiē shàng pèng jiàn le Xiǎo yuè . | I bumped into Xiaoyue in the street. |

## 语法：掉 | Yǔ fǎ : diào | Grammar: 掉 and 倒

> **掉** is to drop or fall from somewhere: 手机掉了. **倒** is to fall over from standing: 树倒了.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我 的 手机 掉 了。 | Wǒ de shǒu jī diào le . | I dropped my phone. |
| 树 倒 了。 | Shù dǎo le . | The tree fell down. |

## 语法：弄 | Yǔ fǎ : nòng | Grammar: 弄: the all-purpose verb

> **弄** “do, handle” stands in for a more specific verb: 我来弄 “I'll deal with it”, 别弄坏了 “don't break it”.

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这 个 怎么 弄？ | Zhè gè zěn me nòng ? | How do you do this? |
