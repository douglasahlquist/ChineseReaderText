# HSK 2 介绍 | HSK èr jiè shào | About HSK 2

> **Draft** — the lessons are not yet reviewed by a native speaker.

## 目标 | Mù biāo | The goal

> HSK 2 is the second level of the HSK 3.0 standard (2021). It adds about 750 words to the 500 of HSK 1. This course teaches the **744 HSK 2 words** not already met in the HSK 1 lessons, in **51 lessons** of about fifteen new words each. Every sentence uses only HSK 1 words and the HSK 2 words taught before it.

> By the end you can talk about your day, the seasons and the weather, your health, food, shopping, getting around and travel; give reasons and opinions with 因为, 所以, 但是 and 如果; compare things; say what has already happened or is about to; and follow longer conversations at home, at church and in town.

## 每课 | Měi kè | How each lesson works

> Each lesson works like HSK 1: the goal and an always-visible word list first, then about ten sentences for each new word, grammar notes, and dialogs that use everything learned so far. Tap a word for its pinyin and sound; double-tap a line for its English.

> Six HSK 2 words were already taught in HSK 1 and aren't repeated: 千, 万, 亿 (lesson 6), 喂 (lesson 23) and 刻, 分钟 (lesson 52).
