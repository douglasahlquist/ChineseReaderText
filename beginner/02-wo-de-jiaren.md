# 第2课：我的家人 | Dì èr kè : wǒ de jiā rén | Lesson 2: My family

## 第一组：介绍家人 | Dì yī zǔ : jiè shào jiā rén | Group 1: Introducing your family

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| A：你家有几个人？ | Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ gè rén ? | How many people are in your family? |
| B：我家有四个人。 | Wǒ jiā yǒu sì gè rén . | There are four people in my family. |
| A：都有谁？ | Dōu yǒu shéi ? | Who are they? |
| B：有爸爸、妈妈、哥哥和我。 | Yǒu bà ba , mā ma , gē ge hé wǒ . | My dad, my mom, my older brother and me. |
| A：你有哥哥吗？ | Nǐ yǒu gē ge ma ? | Do you have an older brother? |
| B：有，我有一个哥哥。 | Yǒu , wǒ yǒu yí gè gē ge . | Yes, I have one older brother. |
| A：你有姐姐吗？ | Nǐ yǒu jiě jie ma ? | Do you have an older sister? |
| B：没有，我没有姐姐。 | Méi yǒu , wǒ méi yǒu jiě jie . | No, I don't have an older sister. |
| A：你有兄弟姐妹吗？ | Nǐ yǒu xiōng dì jiě mèi ma ? | Do you have any brothers or sisters? |
| B：有，我有一个妹妹。 | Yǒu , wǒ yǒu yí gè mèi mei . | Yes, I have a younger sister. |

## 第二组：我的爸爸妈妈 | Dì èr zǔ : wǒ de bà ba mā ma | Group 2: My dad and mom

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| A：你的爸爸叫什么名字？ | Nǐ de bà ba jiào shén me míng zi ? | What is your dad's name? |
| B：我爸爸叫David。 | Wǒ bà ba jiào David . | My dad's name is David. |
| A：你的妈妈呢？ | Nǐ de mā ma ne ? | And your mom? |
| B：我妈妈叫Lisa。 | Wǒ mā ma jiào Lisa . | My mom's name is Lisa. |
| A：你的爸爸是做什么的？ | Nǐ de bà ba shì zuò shén me de ? | What does your dad do? |
| B：我的爸爸是医生。 | Wǒ de bà ba shì yī shēng . | My dad is a doctor. |
| A：你的妈妈呢？ | Nǐ de mā ma ne ? | And your mom? |
| B：我的妈妈是老师。 | Wǒ de mā ma shì lǎo shī . | My mom is a teacher. |
| A：你爸爸喜欢做什么？ | Nǐ bà ba xǐ huan zuò shén me ? | What does your dad like to do? |
| B：他喜欢运动。 | Tā xǐ huan yùn dòng . | He likes sports. |
| A：他喜欢什么运动？ | Tā xǐ huan shén me yùn dòng ? | What sports does he like? |
| B：他喜欢打篮球。 | Tā xǐ huan dǎ lán qiú . | He likes playing basketball. |
| A：你妈妈喜欢做什么？ | Nǐ mā ma xǐ huan zuò shén me ? | What does your mom like to do? |
| B：她喜欢看书和听音乐。 | Tā xǐ huan kàn shū hé tīng yīn yuè . | She likes reading and listening to music. |
| A：你喜欢和妈妈一起做什么？ | Nǐ xǐ huan hé mā ma yì qǐ zuò shén me ? | What do you like to do with your mom? |
| B：我喜欢和妈妈一起看电影。 | Wǒ xǐ huan hé mā ma yì qǐ kàn diàn yǐng . | I like watching movies with my mom. |

## 第三组：猜猜是谁 | Dì sān zǔ : cāi cai shì shéi | Group 3: Guess who

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| A：她是女生。她喜欢唱歌。她也喜欢做饭。她是谁？ | Tā shì nǚ shēng . Tā xǐ huan chàng gē . Tā yě xǐ huan zuò fàn . Tā shì shéi ? | She's female. She likes singing. She also likes cooking. Who is she? |
| B：她是你的妈妈吗？ | Tā shì nǐ de mā ma ma ? | Is she your mom? |
| A：对，她是我的妈妈。 | Duì , tā shì wǒ de mā ma . | Yes, she's my mom. |
| A：他是男生。他很高。他喜欢踢足球。他是谁？ | Tā shì nán shēng . Tā hěn gāo . Tā xǐ huan tī zú qiú . Tā shì shéi ? | He's male. He's tall. He likes playing soccer. Who is he? |
| B：他是你的爸爸吗？ | Tā shì nǐ de bà ba ma ? | Is he your dad? |
| A：对，他是我的爸爸。 | Duì , tā shì wǒ de bà ba . | Yes, he's my dad. |
| B：她是女生。她很小。她喜欢玩玩具。她是谁？ | Tā shì nǚ shēng . Tā hěn xiǎo . Tā xǐ huan wán wán jù . Tā shì shéi ? | She's a girl. She's little. She likes playing with toys. Who is she? |
| A：她是你的妹妹吗？ | Tā shì nǐ de mèi mei ma ? | Is she your younger sister? |
| B：对，她是我的妹妹。 | Duì , tā shì wǒ de mèi mei . | Yes, she's my younger sister. |

## 第四组：和家人一起做什么 | Dì sì zǔ : hé jiā rén yì qǐ zuò shén me | Group 4: Doing things with family

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| A：你周末喜欢和谁一起出去？ | Nǐ zhōu mò xǐ huan hé shéi yì qǐ chū qù ? | Who do you like to go out with on weekends? |
| B：我喜欢和爸爸妈妈一起出去。 | Wǒ xǐ huan hé bà ba mā ma yì qǐ chū qù . | I like going out with my mom and dad. |
| A：你喜欢和爸爸一起做什么？ | Nǐ xǐ huan hé bà ba yì qǐ zuò shén me ? | What do you like to do with your dad? |
| B：我喜欢和爸爸一起踢足球。 | Wǒ xǐ huan hé bà ba yì qǐ tī zú qiú . | I like playing soccer with my dad. |
| A：你喜欢和妈妈一起做什么？ | Nǐ xǐ huan hé mā ma yì qǐ zuò shén me ? | What do you like to do with your mom? |
| B：我喜欢和妈妈一起看电影。 | Wǒ xǐ huan hé mā ma yì qǐ kàn diàn yǐng . | I like watching movies with my mom. |
| A：你喜欢和妹妹一起玩吗？ | Nǐ xǐ huan hé mèi mei yì qǐ wán ma ? | Do you like playing with your younger sister? |
| B：喜欢。 | Xǐ huan . | Yes, I do. |
| A：你们喜欢玩什么？ | Nǐ men xǐ huan wán shén me ? | What do you like to play? |
| B：我们喜欢一起玩游戏。 | Wǒ men xǐ huan yì qǐ wán yóu xì . | We like playing games together. |

## 第五组：我的家人 | Dì wǔ zǔ : wǒ de jiā rén | Group 5: My family

| Hanzi | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| A：你喜欢你的家人吗？ | Nǐ xǐ huan nǐ de jiā rén ma ? | Do you like your family? |
| B：当然喜欢！ | Dāng rán xǐ huan ! | Of course I do! |
| A：你最喜欢和谁一起玩？ | Nǐ zuì xǐ huan hé shéi yì qǐ wán ? | Who do you like playing with most? |
| B：我最喜欢和妹妹一起玩。 | Wǒ zuì xǐ huan hé mèi mei yì qǐ wán . | I like playing with my younger sister most. |
| A：为什么？ | Wèi shén me ? | Why? |
| B：因为她很可爱。 | Yīn wèi tā hěn kě ài . | Because she's very cute. |
| A：你觉得你的家人怎么样？ | Nǐ jué de nǐ de jiā rén zěn me yàng ? | What do you think of your family? |
| B：我的家人很好，我们都很开心。 | Wǒ de jiā rén hěn hǎo , wǒ men dōu hěn kāi xīn . | My family is great. We're all very happy. |
